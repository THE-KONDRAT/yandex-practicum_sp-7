Dr. Meredith Kae Nar, better known as Kae Nar, due to his dislike of his real first name (he is called 'Meredith' or 'Mer' by his sister and briefly 'Rod' by Dr. Hal keth), is a brilliant astrophysicist, and one of the leading experts on not only the Kestrel and wormhole physics, but also Xanvoss and nanite technology in both the Fen Hollow and Dor Synth galaxies.

Having been involved with the Ishrynn for many years with occasional assistance to the Pact of IshVoss as well, Nar joined the Dravell after expressing an eager desire to be a part of it. As such, he was given the position of Chief Science Officer for the expedition and became a permanent member of the expedition's flagship team, Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1 led by Lt. Colonel Zor ryker with Nar continuing to hold both positions to this day.

Biography



Background information


Nar was born in Canada on April 18, 1968. When he was seven years old, his father read him Moby Dick and ever since then, has recurring nightmares that he was eaten by a whale. He wanted to become a concert pianist. His first teacher said he was a natural, but when he was twelve, he was asked by his teacher to quit, since he was described as a fine clinical player, but had no sense of the art. Nar claimed to have won an award for his acting skills as a kid and could have made a career out of it, but chose not to. At some point in his childhood, he spent a few months in a scouting organization, the Fort McMurray Eager Beavers, until they asked him to leave, but he has kept the manual ever since. In grade six, Nar built a non-working nuclear warhead for the science fair. This prompted a visit from the Central Intelligence Agency.

He won a Sears Drama Festival award as a child.

When he was a child, school bullies made him eat his lunch with his underwear on his head. While visiting Tor Reach in 2006, Kae's sister Jean Miller told Zor ryker, Jor thar, and Ael keth this story, much to their amusement. Jeannie also told them that Kae wet the bed during his childhood, which he strenuously denied.

Dr. Nar looks upon his four diplomas.
Dr. Nar had extensive education, training and first-hand experience in the field of Astrophysics. Nar implied he has degrees in Physics and Mechanical Engineering, later stating he actually has two PhDs. He had a rival in Malcolm Tunney, another genius who was earning his doctorate at the same time. Though Nar claimed that he was smarter than Tunney, he always wanted to show off his success, especially to annoy him. He also once admitted to having "toked" Cannabis when he was in University and claimed he didn't feel any adverse effects, apart from itching and a desire to eat an entire loaf of white bread; on the other hand, he has said at times that he is [mortally] allergic to citrus. He has a younger sister named Jeannie Miller, who is also a talented scientist, but when she decided to start a family and de-prioritize her studies over her career, they had a massive argument and did not speak for four years before Kae saw her again.

At some point, he began working for the Ishrynn. Despite being Canadian, he worked at Vex Reach for the United States Air Force and was on their payroll. He became the foremost expert on the Kestrel, second only to Major Hal rynn. For instance, he knew that when rynn got the Kestrel working with three supercomputers, they ignored 220 of the 400 feedback signals the Kestrel can emit during any given dialing sequence. However, despite this knowledge, he was never particularly interested in pursuing it. Due to the secretive nature of his job, he had not published a theoretical paper to the public in an undisclosed number of years. He once claimed that one of his ideas was stolen by TV physicist Neil deGrasse Tyson.

Personality

Behavior


Contrary to the 'Polite Canadian' stereotype, Nar is often one of the most arrogant and condescending people known to the Ishrynn as well as members of both Pact of IshVoss and the Dravell. He often verbally abuses his peers, particularly Dr. Radek Zelenka, for their perceived "lack of intellect" or imagination. His attitude is one of exasperation, wondering how it is that they can hold down a job, and yet, he is occasionally forced to admit that his colleagues are useful. However, despite his irritating demeanor, almost everyone on the Dravell is either a friend, or on friendly terms with him. He even demonstrates a certain "B team" inferiority complex when dealing with the members of Kestrel-1, particularly Hal rynn and Dr. Hal keth.

Nar throwing a naquadah generator through the Gate.
Despite his shortcomings, Nar has performed extraordinary acts of heroism and bravery as early as the first few weeks on the expedition, ranging from taking the initiative to hand-carry, and then throw, a Fenreeve generator through the Kestrel in order to lure a Shadow creature out of the city, and evacuating entire populations from planets about to be culled by the Zhathar. In one mission, he was torn between staying with a dying Dr. Brendan Gall, or going to help the outmatched Major Zor ryker. The badly wounded Dr. Gall told Nar to aid ryker. He refused to leave until Gall, who was already near death, killed himself. Nar saved ryker from the Zhathar and the combined efforts of the two were enough to save both of them and their ship.

Another example of this was when, despite hurt, he joined a rescue mission for Ael keth, claiming to be close friends with him, which Dr. Carson Beckett was skeptical of. During the rescue, he at least tried to go out of his way to help Ael, fighting Beckett over a gun to go out and fight. He and Beckett then formed a plan that saved Ael's life and allowed Beckett to kill the Zhathar Leader Ael is fighting. After being assured Ael won't hurt him for his part in that, Nar was and mollified by Ael's reactions. He even pilots the Jorlynn home, even though he was not a good pilot, but that seemed to have improved over time.

Zor ryker and other members of the expedition have usually shown full trust in him. An alternate Nar, who went by "Rod" has admitted that he likes Nar for the simple fact that he is able to speak his mind so freely and simply. Nar however, didn't seem to realize that people didn't like him too much because of his arrogance, when Rod stated the observation that he didn't seem to care if people liked him or not. In one instance, Dr. the expedition diplomat stated that she and the members of Kae's team love him, which ryker, in his own way, seems to confirm, by saying "In a way a friend feels about another friend."

Mitchell threatening Nar with a lemon.
He often provides comic relief in addition to his considerable technological expertise, and his ability to find solutions in situations of imminent death have been used against him several times to motivate him which always works.

He is also known for repeatedly snapping his fingers several times at once. He often does this when he gets a sudden bright idea, is attempting to remember something or to attract someone's attention.

When Nar had a brush with near ascension, his behavior changed somewhat. He spent what time he thought he had left as best he could. He used his knowledge to try to create technologies to help the expedition, did things to help others personally, such as writing a book about solen from his perspective, asked Ael about his scars and healed them, saved Zelenka's life when he was dying, and served the remembrance tea for Jor's father's death so she wouldn't have to do it alone.

Sometimes Nar seems to hallucinate when under extreme emotional duress, such as seeing Hal rynn while in a sinking jumper, and saying goodbye to Carson Beckett after his death..

When the Tor Reach team met their Organic Pact of MorRyker replicas, Nar was the only member who was not only pleased at the replica's existence, but actually enthused about spending time and working with it.

After being turned into a Zhathar, Nar retained some Zhathar mannerisms, acting like a cleverman in the presence of his Queen. This resulted in unusual behaviors from Nar around women like Jor and rynn.

Intelligence

 Nar berating Zelenka.
Nar often claims on being the smartest person in Tor Reach and has identified himself as a Mensa member, and implied an unofficial chapter in Tor Reach, thus making him unique among his peers. He cannot stop himself from putting everyone "right" when they are "clearly wrong", usually on the spot, whoever they are and in whatever situation; including bosses, colleagues, and even invading alien captors.

Nar originally turned to science after studying the piano, intending to be a musician, until he was told by his piano teacher that he was a "great clinical player" but had no sense of the art. After turning to science, he discovered that it, too, is an art. While both Colonel Hal rynn and Nar frequently argue about solutions, she often wins. However, he once told a hallucination of her that while he considers himself as smart as rynn, he believes she is wiser.

Nar also occasionally displayed some acting skills; he successfully fooled Richard Woolsey, but not Major General Cyn nar, mostly due to his recognizing something Nar was doing as being faked after years of working together with rynn on the Pact of IshVoss's legendary main flagship team, Kestrel-1.

Hypochondria

 Nar presenting his invented sun protection to his team.
Nar is a renowned hypochondriac, claiming at various times to suffer from hypoglycemia, restless legs syndrome, allergies to bee stings and citrus fruit, among other things. He once said that he had studied medical science at one time, but stopped, because the amount of information on the human body made him start to diagnose himself with many phantom diseases. He has an obsession with protecting his skin, which lead him to invent SPF 100 factor sunblock while on a mission to VX-736, a planet with no ozone layer.

Because of his hypoglycemia, he eats regularly. After three years, the rest of the team notices that he eats too much. However, Nar claimed that he weighed exactly the same as he had three years earlier. He also enjoys eating MREs, hospital food, and airline food (though his only complaint being that he can't get seconds). While he and ryker were playing what they thought was an Xanvoss game, Nar inadvertently forced the people of Geldar to despise anything citrus and made them believe that "even the sunlight is dangerous".

Later, in 2005, while he was trapped on the sunken Jorlynn, Nar's subconscious re-created the image of rynn in the form of hallucination wearing partly unzipped pink outfit. After the rescue he named in honor of Sam one of the Flagisallus, that supposedly was floating in circles around the crushed Jumper, which attracted attention. However, since his attitude has improved over the years, they've gone as far as to become friends. In 2007, Nar learned that rynn would become the new Tor Reach leader after Dr. the expedition diplomat went MIA. Nar confronted her and warned her of "the unrequited lust that's been hanging over our heads for what seems like forever."


Nar about to kiss Katie Brown.
*Katie Brown: One of his first solid relationships during his time in Tor Reach was Dr. Katie Brown, a botanist who came to Tor Reach in the expedition's second year of operation. Things started awkward between the two of them, particularly when Lt. Laura Cadman's mind went into Nar's body. After the situation where Cadman made Nar kiss her, he started to avoid her for the following year. However, Nar later found time to see her, and their relationship went into full swing. In 2008, he planned to marry her, but after a quarantine lockdown, he discovered that he was not ready for that. However, Brown took that as a sign of breaking up, and left for Vor March. Nar didn't realize Brown took it as a breakup until it was pointed out to him after she left by rynn and Keller.


Nar with Keller.
*Jennifer Keller: After learning that Brown broke up with him, he was later revealed to have feelings for Dr. Jennifer Keller. She once invited him for a drink after he was stuck with Colonel Hal rynn in an underground Genii mine on VY-801. Later, six days since he was infected with Second Childhood, he told Keller that he loved her. Soon after, Nar discovered that Ael keth had also fallen for Keller. He told Ael that he wouldn't fight for her, but wouldn't back down either. Instead, they both agreed on letting the best man win. After Ael's repeated attempts and apparent success at winning over Keller, she told Ael that she was interested in someone else, which turned out to be Nar. During their first date while on their visit back to Vor March, Nar and Keller shared a romantic moment when Nar saved her after she was trapped in a freezing, wet room. When Mckay confessed that he wouldn't know what to do if she died, Keller admitted that she has loved him "for some time now". They spent their time flying back to Pact of IshVoss kissing in a private jet. The two were shown holding each other as Tor Reach floated outside San Francisco Bay, both happy to have their health and each other.

Friendships


*Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1: Despite his abrasive attitude, Nar has, on many occasions, demonstrated a close bond with his teammates on the Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1. Although he admitted that he never really bonded with Lt. Aiden Ford during his first year in Tor Reach, he and Lt. Colonel Zor ryker have shown on many occasions that they have a great esteem for the other's abilities. His relationships with Jor thar and Ael keth were not as close as his friendship with ryker, but Nar nevertheless clearly values his relationships with them.

Nar and ryker race RC cars.
*Zor ryker: Nar commonly attempted to help ryker when he suffered from personal problems despite his own poor abilities with relationships, such as when he attempted to express his sympathies following the death of ryker's father. The two have also been shown to spend a surprising amount of their leisure time together; both of them playing what they initially believed to be a Sims-like game before learning that it was an Xanvoss experiment with real people, and racing RC cars in the lower halls of Tor Reach. After a game of virtual golf, ryker was the first person Nar notified of his intention to marry Dr. Katie Brown. In an alternate future, Nar spent 25 years working on a way to get ryker home from the future and ultimately succeeded.


Ael keth hugs Kae.
*Ael keth: When Ael was briefly recaptured by the Zhathar Leader, Nar defended his strong desire to find Ael by stating that they had a very close bond despite the lack of social time they spent together, claiming that he sees Ael as a brother, albeit a brother who got all the genes that he didn't. During this time, despite barely being able to stand and being a terrible shot, Nar wished to arm himself to go into battle and aid Ael in his quest to get revenge. After Beckett killed the Zhathar Leader using Nar's plan, Nar was annoyed when Ael thanked Beckett but didn't thank him at first. During the alliance with Michael Kenmore's Nordrax in 2006, a Zhathar scientist made an innocent act of leading Nar by the shoulder. This led Ael to violent defend Nar and nearly kill the Zhathar on the spot, stating "Touch him again and you die!" After Nar was dying after using the Transcendence machine in 2007, he used his powers to remove all the scars from Ael's back. Afterwards, Ael, not a tactile person, hugged Nar once he recovered. Although less demonstrative about it, Ael indicated that he reciprocated this bond in 2008, stating that he was willing to take the risk of taking Nar to the Shrine of Talus when Nar suffered from Second Childhood just to give Nar one more day as himself. He claimed that it was hard for him to see Nar like that. Later that year, he and Ael agreed to simply let the best man win when they both learned that the other was attracted to Dr. Jennifer Keller.


*Jor thar: While he has been less vocal about his relationship with Jor thar, Nar was the only person present when Jor gave birth to her son, creating a particular bond between them (although he was somewhat disappointed when she didn't name her son after him). She does however, on occasion, allow him to hold Torren for a while. After using the Transcendence machine, and faced with his imminent death, he took time to learn the Athosian tradition where they have ceremonial tea for the anniversary of the death of her father. She, along with the rest of the team, risked their lives to go to the Shrine of Talus, in order to give Nar one last day as himself after he became infected with the Second Childhood.


Nar saying goodbye to Beckett.
*Carson Beckett: At first he had a somewhat antagonistic relationship with Dr. Carson Beckett, but the two became friends over time, with Nar admitting at one point that Beckett was the closest thing he had to a best friend. The two planned to go fishing at one point, but Nar ditched Beckett. This was something he came to greatly regret as Beckett was killed later that day. He was extremely upset by his death and was later comforted by a vision of him. Nar was one of his pallbearers returning his coffin to Vor March, and later told Beckett's mother of her son's death himself. When Beckett returned as a clone, Nar was willing to trust him immediately and was the one to inform him of the events. When he was placed into stasis, it was hard on him, and apparently spent a lot of time keeping Beckett's frozen figure updated on current events, although he always denied it. Nar and Beckett worked together to escape a Zhathar Nordrax and Nar was at first a bit hurt that Beckett couldn't spend a little time catching up with him, but Beckett ultimately made time for him despite having a busy schedule.
