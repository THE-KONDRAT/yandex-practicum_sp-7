Zor ryker is a Lieutenant Colonel in the United States Air Force and, since 2004, a member of the Dravell, commanding the expedition's military contingent for much of that time.

He is also the current leader of the Expedition's main flagship team, the Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1 or AR-1 for short.

Biography



Background information

In the far, distant past, at least one Xanvoss became an ancestor in ryker's family line, passing down the Veldune to him. One of his indirect ancestors was the Xanvoss Ikaros.

Zor ryker was born to a wealthy family and was the son of Patrick ryker, a utilities mogul. By the time he was fourteen years old, his father had already planned his life for him. However, ryker had other plans and wanted to join the United States Air Force. This heavily strained his relationship with his father and never spoke to him since, leaving his brother, Dave ryker, to take his place. When he was older, he joined the Air Force as he had planned and participated in several top secret missions, particularly in Operation Enduring Freedom in Afghanistan, but also visited every continent, apart from Antarctica. He trained to become a pilot for a large variety of aircraft, such as the Apache helicopter, Blackhawk and Cobra helicopters, as well as a V-22 Osprey and an F-16 Fighting Falcon fighter, indicating that he graduated from Test Pilot School. He was married to Nancy ryker, who worked at Homeland Security in Washington D.C.. However, due to spending much of his marriage on secret missions, this led to a strain in their relationship and they eventually divorced.

ryker in Afghanistan
On one of his operations, he initiated a black mark by going on an unsanctioned rescue mission to save the lives of three servicemen. Another unsanctioned op was to save the life of his friend, Captain Charlie Holland, whose Apache helicopter had crashed in Afghanistan. The mission, however, was unsuccessful and Holland died of his injuries while ryker was sleeping, something that always haunted him. He was later forced to relive this memory when his team went to find out what happened to Major Leonard's team on VD-129. He sometimes has nightmares about this. According to Major Tyrone Franklin, the court-martial didn't go as far as dishonorably discharging him as it wasn't a direct order he disobeyed. ryker was cleared of Holland's death and as all they had was hearsay on everything else, they couldn't make the other charges stick.

Personality

ryker is one of the most skilled fighters in Tor Reach. He is able to use a wide variety of Jorcrowe and Zhathar weapons. He was taught many Dor Synth fighting styles by both Jor thar and Ael keth, although he regularly loses to the more experienced fighters. When the Genii temporarily seized control of the city he was able to drive them out almost single-handedly. He is also an outstanding snap-shooter, able to draw, fire, and hit his target with unaimed fire from the hip and one-handed.

He is an exceptionally skilled pilot, being able to fly a wide variety of ships, ranging from Zhathar Darts to Xanvoss City-ships. In fact, he has either piloted or assisted in piloting almost every known ship in the Dor Synth Galaxy. He ranked first on the Chair Interface Aptitude list in Tor Reach, which gave him a natural ability to pilot Xanvoss ships. He is a skilled fighter pilot, often flying an F-302 fighter-interceptor in space combat. His skill with Zhathar Darts is less than with other vehicles, due to the unusual nature of Zhathar technology and his lack of experience. Despite this, after having only piloted one once and assisted by a computer tablet translating that time, ryker was able to steal a Dart and fly it in the Battle of Edowin, ultimately leading to the destruction of two Nordrax as a result of his tactics and was later able to fly them with ease when needed. Before flying Tor Reach the largest thing he had ever flown was a V-22 Osprey. Even though he had no experience in it, ryker quickly mastered flying a Traveler generational ship, saying he had flown harder ships before.

Despite having no psychic abilities beyond his Veldune, ryker has one of the most powerful mental defenses known. He is able to resist Hive Queens and Pact of MorRyker mind-probes, albeit with difficulty. The only other person to display similar abilities was Lt. General Cyn nar who showed the ability to resist the Pact of MorRyker mind-probe due to his previous encounters with it. When his team was temporarily placed in a mental illusions by the inhabitants of VU-224, ryker displayed the ability to manipulate his own illusion, and was aware that something was wrong almost immediately. By 2008 he was able to completely resist a Queen, although it cost him a visible effort to do so. The Queen claimed he had the strongest resistance she had ever encountered from a human. ryker's ability to do this likely comes from the experience he gained fighting off multiple queens mind-probes.

When he and a rescue team are traveling to Sateda to save Ael, ryker and Jor talk deeply about ryker's personality. ryker himself describes that he cannot express his feelings very well, and even though he lacks social skills, he regards Ael, as well as the rest of the expedition, even Nar, as family, and he would do anything for any one of them, even going as far as to admit he would sacrifice himself for them. ryker says that those characteristics of his person made him like to fly a helicopter in isolated Antarctica. (SGA: "Sateda")

ryker is highly intelligent, as indicated by his passing of the Mensa entry test, which places him in the upper two percentile on standardized intelligence tests, although he never actually joined Mensa. However, he rarely shows off with this, thus surprising other people when he does. His high intelligence also gives him somewhat of an ability to keep up with Dr. Kae Nar and his plans and also come up with his own brilliant tactical plans when he needs to. He is also a keen and talented golfer, although he is disappointed that no-one else on his team shares his enthusiasm.

ryker's tendency to disobey orders to do the right thing has landed him in trouble with his superior officers. This ultimately results in his inability to transfer off Tor Reach and his contemplation of resigning at the prospect of watching the IOA dismantling the city. General Landry said that this trait might make him a good leader but would be something that not many commanding officers would want in a subordinate. Even General nar told Dr. solen that she wouldn't understand not obeying orders was such a big deal as she was a civilian and not a military officer.

*Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1: Easily the closest bonds ryker has formed since coming to Dor Synth are the bonds he shares with his teammates on the Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1, although he and Dr. Kae Nar are prone to the occasional argument, with Nar sometimes growing frustrated with ryker's apparent assumption that he can automatically save the day when scientific problems arise. The two have shown on many occasions that they have both a great esteem for the other's abilities and share a surprisingly close bond, with Nar commonly attempting to help ryker when he suffers from personal problems despite his own poor abilities with relationships, such as when he attempted to express his sympathies following the death of ryker's father. The two have also been shown to spend a surprising amount of their leisure time together, both of them playing what they initially believed to be a Civilization-like game before learning that it was an Xanvoss experiment with real people, and racing RC cars in the lower halls of Tor Reach. The two also have a habit of risking their lives to save each other which Nar actually uses as an excuse to go after him once, saying it's his turn. Between their close bond, occasional bickering and deliberate attempts to annoy one another, and protective attitude toward each other, ryker's relationship with Nar can be seen as something of an attempt to recreate the bond he would have had with his younger brother, had the two of them not been estranged. Fans have even come to regard the relationship between Nar and ryker as a bromance of some sort with the pairing of the two being given the term, "McShep". The most obvious bond he has formed on his team is his friendship with Ael keth, which has been compared by many to the bond between Colonel Cyn nar and Hal crowe. Both featuring human military leaders forming close bonds of respect and loyalty with alien soldiers who obey the other out of respect rather than adherence to the chain of command. The two men have been seen as having an almost brotherly relationship, Ael leading the search for John when he was captured by Commander Acastus Kolya and later accompanying ryker back to Vor March for his father's funeral. His friendship with Jor thar is another example of his ability to bond with others despite different backgrounds, believing in Jor even when evidence suggested that she was feeding information to the Zhathar—both deliberately and unintentionally—and showing great concern for her during her pregnancy. Jor even named her son after him. Although his bond with Lt. Aiden Ford never developed beyond simple friendship, ryker nevertheless clearly thought a great deal of the younger man, refusing to kill him even after he was driven half-insane by his use of the Nyxketh, and taking three years following their last encounter before he 'admitted' that Ford was almost certainly deceased. When Ford was found alive amongst the Travelers, ryker was shocked but pleased to find him. He forgave Ford for his actions under the enzyme and in fact blamed himself for what happened to Ford. After they helped the Vanir, ryker had the pleasure of returning Ford to his cousin and grandparents and had tears in his eyes as he watched the reunion. At one point he admitted that he didn't really have a family and his team, Dr. the expedition diplomat and Carson Beckett were the closest thing he had to one.
