Vel solen is an accomplished and acclaimed American diplomat. After a personal request from U.S. President Henry Hayes in 2004, that career came to an abrupt end so that she could assume the role of the first ever of civilian commander of Pact of IshVoss, essentially trading international diplomacy for interplanetary diplomacy. Soon afterwards, she took on the mantle of original commander of the Dravell into a whole new galaxy, Dor Synth. A role she held up until 2007 when she was captured by the Order of KorNar with solen's physical body later dying when her consciousness was assimilated by the Pact of MorRyker collective.

She came to lead the remnants of Niam's faction in their quest for ascension. She returned to Tor Reach in 2008 to seek help after a failed attempt at Digital Transcendence, but the Order of KorNar proved dangerous, and she sacrificed herself to freeze the others in the cold of space.

However, she was later ascended by the Transcended Galnar Ran before being "unascended" later after she interfered by saving Kae Nar. After returning to Vor March, she was sent to stay on Tor Reach by the IOA until they were convinced she was indeed the real Vel solen. Although allowed to return to working with the expedition, solen was not allowed to lead it again, instead falling under the command of Zor ryker, her former military commander and the current interim leader of Tor Reach.

Biography



Background information


Vel solen was very close with her late father and brought the pocket watch that he gave to her to Tor Reach. Not much is known about her mother Katherine solen other than that she appeared to her in her dream while being infected by nanites in 2006, indicating that she was alive at the time. At some point, she became engaged to Dr. Simon Wallace and they lived together near Washington D.C..

Before becoming involved with the Ishrynn, solen was a skilled civilian diplomat who worked behind the scenes in the U.S. government for several years. An expert in international politics, solen was well known for mediating for the United Nations. She brokered a dozen of the most sensitive international accords in modern history. She has two Ph.D.s, taught political science at Georgetown University, served in embassies all over the world and speaks five languages, including Russian and Latin. Though she began her career as a political activist lobbying against government spending on the military, she later decided to change things from the inside and entered the realm of government. She concluded that the best way to stop weapons proliferation was to try and end the need for them. Dr. Hal keth referenced her work while drafting the Jorcrowe-Pact of VexDrax Treaty.

Powers and abilities

Hybrid/Pact of MorRyker

While both a human-Pact of MorRyker hybrid and a full-fledged Pact of MorRyker, solen displayed several abilities common to Yorskov:
*Wireless connection to Yorskov subspace network: After her nanites were reprogramed and activated, solen was able to achieve a wireless connection to the Yorskov subspace network once Kae Nar initiated the link. While connected, solen displayed the ability to access any and all information known to the Yorskov collective, including being able to see through their eyes, granting her a kind of omniscience. solen was able to remain undetected while doing this as the Yorskov didn't know to look for her.
*Yorskov mind probing: After her nanites were fully initialized, solen displayed the same mind probing ability the Yorskov possessed, even proving capable of using this to link directly with Oberoth, the most powerful Yorskov.
**Yorskov Collective control: By linking directly with the collective through another Yorskov, solen displayed the ability to take full control of the Yorskov collective and freeze them in place. However, due to still being part-human, solen lacked the endurance to hold this control long. While solen needed to connect directly with a Yorskov to establish her control, she was able to maintain it briefly even after Oberoth removed her hand from his head, but lost control once her concentration was broken.
**Illusion: While connected with Oberoth, solen displayed the same ability as the Yorskov to implant an elaborate illusion of her own making into the being she was directly connected with's mind. Oberoth, despite being a full Yorskov, was unable to see through solen's illusion right up until the moment she dropped the act.
**Yorskov termination: Unique to her, after having been turned into a full Yorskov, solen displayed the ability to terminate the life of another Yorskov by linking with them then shutting down the bonds between their nanites.
*Super strength: As a full Yorskov, solen is able to shove fellow Yorskov Koracen up against a wall and pin him there with just one arm while she terminates him.
*Invulnerability: After becoming a full Yorskov, solen possessed the same invulnerability to harm as the other Yorskov possessed.

Transcended

*Dream control: solen displayed the ability to enter the dreams of Kae Nar and Jor thar to communicate with and guide them discreetly. She could also manipulate their dreams.
*Teleportation: In order to save Nar's life, solen displayed the ability to teleport him from a Jorlynn to the Hal brandt.
