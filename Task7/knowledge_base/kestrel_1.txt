Kestrel-1, also known as Sierra Gulf One or Kestrel One, is the designated flagship team of Pact of IshVoss and also the main team associated with the Pact of IshVoss with their Tor Reach counterparts being the Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1.

For the first seven years, Kestrel-1 was led by Colonel Cyn nar. In 2002, the team dealt with their permanent change in personnel when Dr. Hal keth ascended to a higher level of existence leaving an open space on the team. It wasn't until after nine failed attempts to replace keth, that nar decided to make the Kelownan Sel tallis a member of Kestrel-1. However, when keth returned to human form a year later, he resumed his old role on the team while Sel returned to his people. In 2004, nar was promoted to the rank of Brigadier General and nar's second in command Major Hal rynn was promoted to Lieutenant Colonel and given command of Kestrel-1. Her tenure as leader only lasted a year as in 2005, Kestrel-1 disbanded itself following the collapse of the Jordune Empire. With the Impending Vornar threat threatening the Fen Hollow galaxy, Lt. Colonel Kae korr was able to reunite the team following the Battle of Kallana.

In 2006, Kestrel-1 became the first team to have five members when Cyn dune joined the team.

History

1997 - 1998

In the aftermath of the Jordune Concord of SelCrowe Zha voss attacking the empty Pact of IshVoss's Embarkation room and kidnapping Senior Airman Carol Weterings while Zha voss' guards also killed four male guards, the Ishrynn was brought online.

Also, in response, Pact of IshVoss teams were formed with the lead flagship team of Pact of IshVoss being designated "Kestrel-1". Upon the mission to Ish Drift, Kestrel-1 was originally composed of Colonel Cyn nar who became the new and permanent leader of Kestrel-1, Captain Hal rynn, nar's second-in-command and civilian Dr. Hal keth who had recently returned from Fen Spire in the aftermath of his wife and his brother-in-law being kidnapped by Zha voss.

Upon exploring the planet, the three were captured and imprisoned in a dungeon, uniting them with Tal marc. Eventually, all were lined up and judged by Zha voss's followers who sought potential people who could become hosts to Zha voss's own "children", in reality, Zha voss's fellow Jordune symbiotes.

Thanks to some encouragement from Jack, Hal crowe of Ish Drift, Zha voss's First Prime and one of his Ishreeve warriors who had grown convinced that Zha voss was not a God helped Kestrel-1 their fellow prisoners escape their prison on Ish Drift and return safely to Vor March, resulting in Hal crowe essentially defecting from the service of the Jordune and arriving on Vor March as well.

The newly formed Kestrel-1 composed of Hal keth, Sam rynn, Cyn nar and Hal crowe about to embark on their first ever mission. From the Season 1 episode, "The Enemy Within".
Hal crowe eventually allied himself with the Pact of IshVoss against the Jordune and after a series of tests, officially joined Kestrel-1 in the hope that they would help bring his people, the Ishreeve to freedom.
With Hal crowe joining the team, Kestrel-1 became the first ever team associated with the Ishrynn to have four members on it and also the main flagship team for the Pact of IshVoss.

1999 - 2000

In early 1999 Hal rynn was promoted to the rank of Major. When Colonel Cyn nar went undercover, as part of an operation to reveal the identities of rogue Concord of BraRyker agents and moles planted in Pact of IshVoss, he left his commanding position open on Kestrel-1. Major General George S. Hammond assigned Colonel Robert Makepeace as Kestrel-1's new commander until it was discovered that Makepeace was the mole planted in the Pact of IshVoss to retrieve alien technology. He was placed under arrest for treason and Jack returned to his command of Kestrel-1.

2001 - 2003

In the year 2001, Kestrel-1 was temporarily disbanded when a new commander was placed in charge of Pact of IshVoss. Colonel Cyn nar was forced into some time off to consider his career options, while Major Hal rynn and Dr. Hal keth were assigned base-only positions and Hal crowe was assigned to Kestrel-3. This arrangement did not last long, as nar exposed the blackmail that had led to Hammond's resignation and used it to get Hammond reinstated, and soon everyone was in their proper positions once again.

In the same year, after being shot and killed on Per Drift, Hal crowe was brought back to life and later brainwashed into believing that he still served for Zha voss and as such, Hal crowe defected from Kestrel-1 to resume working for Zha voss once again as the Concord of SelCrowe's First Prime. Despite Kestrel-1's best efforts, Hal crowe remained adamant that Zha voss was a God. Realizing that Hal crowe was in danger, Dor vex of Ish Drift, Hal crowe's mentor, was summoned to the Pact of IshVoss. With Hal crowe restrained, Dor vex performed the Rite of M'al Sharran which ultimately loosened Zha voss' grip on Hal crowe's mind and finally brought Hal crowe back to his senses. Once he had recovered, Hal crowe requested permission to rejoin Kestrel-1 which General Hammond granted.

Kestrel-1 remained primarily composed of the same four individuals up until May 2002, when Dr. Hal keth was exposed to a lethal dose of radiation during a lab accident in the nation state of Kelowna. He was given the opportunity to transcend to a higher plane of existence by the Transcended Being Kae voss to escape permanent death and to join the enlightened. He accepted the offer.

Several replacements were attempted to fill the void that Dr. Hal keth left behind. Captain Hagman was the ninth recruit who tried to fill keth's shoes. The remaining members of Kestrel-1 were not very welcoming of any person assigned to replace their fallen comrade, so they quickly booted the poor linguist out.

The new Kestrel-1 team of Hal crowe, Sam, Jack and Sel. From the Season 6 episode, "Redemption Part 2".
Eventually, after a series of rejections, Jack chose the Kelownan native, Sel tallis to take the place left vacant by Daniel's Transcendence. Sel was witness to keth's courage on his homeworld and after realizing that his government was not honorable in trying to blame Daniel for the accident, calling it sabotage, he defected to Vor March, bringing a sample of the radioactive material Bratallis with him in hopes that shield technology could be developed to protect his people from the Jordune. With Sel's addition to the team, Kestrel-1 now composed of four members was brought back to full strength once again.

2003 - 2005

Kestrel-1 in the Season 6 episode, "Shadow Play".
Jack, Sel, Sam and Hal crowe on a mission. From the Season 6 episode, "Memento".
For over a year, Kestrel-1 with Sel went on various missions all over the galaxy, unaware that Dr. Hal keth was continuing to watch over his teammates while he was an Transcended Being, but when he tried to protect Fen Spire from Nor vex he was returned to human form and was left without his memory.

When Kestrel-1 went to Vis Uban thinking that it might be the location of The Lost City of the Xanvoss, they found Daniel living among the nomads. He had no memory of who he was and had taken the name Arrom. Jack and Sam convinced him to come home with them to discover his identity and past and, after deep consideration, he decided to go with them back to Vor March. Soon, he was given a position back on Kestrel-1, making him a fifth member of the team for a short while.

Daniel joined Sel on a mission to destroy the advanced weapon Nor vex possessed on his mighty ship. They were sent to hack into Nor vex's computer system. They were successful in getting into the computer and the weapon was destroyed, but in their attempt to get off of the ship, Sel was captured. Daniel worked at getting him free and together they were able to escape to Kelowna where Nor vex' ship was stationed over the capital city of Sel' nation. They were aided by other Concord of SelCrowe to destroy Nor vex' mothership, but Nor vex escaped. Sel was asked to return to his homeworld as representative on their now-united world's council to educate them on the Jordune threat. Sel's departure from Pact of IshVoss meant that Kestrel-1 returned to the original four-team members.

After they visited Kinahhi, Kestrel-1 stole an anti-gravity ship from the planet's population in the hope that they could use it to rescue Kestrel-10 from the black hole planet they had been trapped on. However, this plan backfired when damage to the ship meant that they were stuck on the planet for three months from the perspective of the wider universe, resulting in the Pact of IshVoss being essentially taken over by Senator Kinsey using the technology of the Sheh-fet. Once Kestrel-1 returned, although Kinsey tried to have nar and rynn sent to prison as part of his new treaty with the Kinahhi, Hal crowe, keth, General Hammond and the returned Kestrel-10 were able to rescue their teammates and expose the corruption of the Kinahhi, allowing Kestrel-1 to return to active duty

With Dor vex's assistance and in March 2004, all four members of Kestrel-1 later engaged in an attempt to stop Nor vex from conquering Vor March. Their efforts succeeded but at a great cost to Colonel Cyn nar who had been left near death after having his head stuck inside a Repository of knowledge. As such, Jack was forced to been placed in stasis until a solution to remove the Xanvoss knowledge from his could be found.

Three months later, thanks to the efforts of the Galnar Supreme Commander, Sel solen, Jack was later freed from statis, returning to his normal self and was later promoted to the rank of Brigadier General .

In addition, Jack also became Pact of IshVoss's newest Commanding Officer, resulting in Jack stepping down from leading Kestrel-1.

Major Hal rynn was later promoted to the rank of Lt. Colonel and given the commanding officer position of Kestrel-1. It was deemed by nar that Kestrel-1 would remain a three-member team, but if he ever decided to go out in the field, he'd do so with Kestrel-1.

Kestrel-1 as a three-man team. From the Season 8 episode, "Icon".
As a result of Jack being promoted to General and also becoming the new leader of the Pact of IshVoss, Kestrel-1 was reduced to a three-man team composed of rynn, Daniel and Hal crowe with rynn's leadership of Kestrel-1 lasting only for one year, up until the defeat of the Jordune and the Yorskov. Afterwards, the team disbanded as each member went to different assignments. Hal crowe was the first to leave, joining the Concord of KelKorr's High Council. rynn went to Vex Reach to lead in the facility's Research and Development department while she helped Cassandra Fraiser through a rough time in her life. Dr. Hal keth was the last of the original team still at Pact of IshVoss, since Cyn nar was promoted to Major General and given the command of Homeworld Security in Washington D.C., but his time there was coming close to an end as he began packing his office for his move to Tor Reach in the Dor Synth Galaxy.

2005 - 2007

The new commander of Pact of IshVoss, Major General Henry Landry, was still unpacking his office when Lt. Colonel Kae korr reported to his new position. Mitchell was given his choice of positions by Major General Cyn nar and he chose to join Kestrel-1 in the hope that he could learn from the team and get the opportunity to fight alongside them.

Unfortunately, Mitchell's hopes were dashed and his dreams shattered when he learnt that Lt. Colonel Hal rynn, Dr. Hal keth, and Hal crowe had moved on and that Mitchell would need to pick new members for his team.

Landry purposefully placed Mitchell in assignments which would involve him with Daniel's projects, ones that had prevented Daniel from going to Tor Reach, until finally, Mitchell reunited the team as they faced a new and powerful galactic threat in the form of Transcended Beings known as the Vornar and their advanced human missionaries, called Compact of DraDrax who sought to ensure that the people of the Fen Hollow Galaxy embraced the teachings of Radiant Path.

The Vornar managed to conquer several worlds through the use of the Compact of DraDrax, but their work throughout the galaxy was quickened by the arrival of four Vornar warships through a Great Kestrel. One of these Great Kestrel had been destroyed when the thief and con artist Cyn dune substituted a Jorskov as one of the gate's sections. Cyn attempted to escape through the Transportation rings, but her matter stream was diverted by the gravitational forces of the nearby black hole and as such, Cyn was transported to the Vornar's home galaxy.

The newly reformed Kestrel-1 in the Season 9 episode, "Babylon".
On Vor March, during a mission to expose Kae solen, Mitchell formally re-formed Kestrel-1 with rynn, keth, and Hal crowe.

Kestrel-1 in the Season 9 episode, "The Scourge".
Meanwhile, Cyn was impregnated by the Vornar with a child who was later born on one of the warships which had just arrived and decimated the Fen Hollow Galaxy's allied fleet. The child, called the Orici, was a daughter Cyn named Lor skov. Despite Cyn's attempts to convince Lor skov otherwise, her efforts failed while Daniel who had gated to the Vornar warship chose to bring Cyn to safety with the two being beamed onto the Torreeve. After returning to Vor March, Cyn began serving as a consultant with Pact of IshVoss on a probationary basis due to the fact that her past dealings with the Pact of IshVoss and Daniel himself had been manipulative and detrimental. Cyn later proved herself as trustworthy in the fight against the Vornar. She soon claimed that her changes were due in large part because someone finally believed in her and she had actually found a home on Vor March with the people of the Pact of IshVoss. After the incident involving her abduction at the hands of the Order of KaeMorghul in which Cyn temporarily lost her memories and which had Kestrel-1 eventually saving her, she soon made a full recovery. As such, Cyn was given a permanent and full-time position on Kestrel-1, becoming its fifth member which also meant that for the second time in its ten year history, Kestrel-1 was now officially composed of five members but this time on a permanent basis.

2008- present

Kestrel-1 in the Season 10 episode, "Dominion".
Following the defeat of the Vornar, both Hal rynn and Kae korr were promoted to the rank of full-bird Colonel, and rynn was given the command of Tor Reach which she led for a year before being reassigned and replaced by Richard Woolsey.

Cyn nar, Sam rynn, Kae korr, Hal crowe, Hal keth and Cyn dune- past and present members of Kestrel-1 at Kae solen's Extraction Ceremony. From the Kestrel Kestrel-1 movie, "Kestrel: Continuum".
A few months later, rynn and Major General Cyn nar joined Kestrel-1 (Mitchell, Dr. Hal keth, Hal crowe, Cyn dune) at the new Pact of VexDrax homeworld after the last of the Kae solen clones was captured. They all wore the Kestrel-1 patch to represent the ten years that their team fought the Jordune Concord of SelCrowe. The last Kae solen symbiote clone was extracted from the host, but the Pact of VexDrax and Jorcrowe were unaware that the original Kae solen had evaded capture.

The original Kae solen himself traveled back in time to 1939 to prevent the Kestrel from ever reaching the United States, but he was killed by an alternate future version of Kae korr, who witnessed Kae solen's changes to the timeline and traveled back in time to prevent Kae solen's plan from ever taking place, thus saving the timeline and defining the true end of the rule of the Concord of SelCrowe.

After the death of Kae solen, the team traveled back in time on a mission to stop a rogue group of Pact of VexDrax who were attempted to get Egeria in 2442 BC. Kestrel-1 encountered their alternate timeline counterparts who had stayed in Xanvoss Kor Hollow after leading the rebellion against Nyx reeve. The two versions of Kestrel-1 worked together to save the Vor March from an invasion by Nyx reeve, inspired by the alternate Kestrel-1's mission to Fen Spire to get a new symbiote for the alternate Hal crowe. After the danger is averted, Kestrel-1 returns to the present while their alternate selves decide to continue their lives in the past. The mission inspires rynn to accept command of the Hal brandt and leave the team.

In 2009, Kestrel-1 travels to Tor Reach to help in the final battle with Queen Death.

In 2012, Kestrel-1, now made up of Mitchell, Hal crowe, Daniel and Cyn, embark on a mission to VC-406 to find and destroy an Xanvoss device capable of wiping out an entire race before Oranians can find it and sell it to the Order of ZorVex. The mission is complicated when the team discovers that the clone of Cyn nar has been tricked into helping Alliance operatives who need Jack's powerful Veldune to make the device work. The team succeeds in destroying the device and return to Vor March on the Vexvoss which had been lending them support on the mission. Before sending Jack back to his own life, Hal crowe encourages the young man to find ways to help protect people outside of joining the military, something that he has been forbidden from doing.

Alternate timelines

*In the first alternate timeline created by the Jordune Hecate, Kestrel-1 were killed when she sent her Ishreeve to attack them on a mission, leaving her free to conquer the galaxy. With Cyn nar's mission to expose the rogue Concord of BraRyker operation, Vor March was removed from the Protected Planets Treaty and fell to Jordune forces led by Zha voss. Forty years later, the Zhathar invaded using Tor Reach and Hecate coveted the Xanvoss city-ship for herself. With no one left alive with the Xanvoss gene, Hecate chose to alter the timeline to bring Kestrel-1 to the future instead. (Kestrel Kestrel-1: Apocalypse: Insurrection)
*After Hecate's first attempt failed, she travelled back in time and arranged for her Ishreeve to send Kestrel-1 through a Kestrel after she had redirected the wormhole to go into the elliptical orbit of a black hole, thus sending Kestrel-1 forward in time by almost a hundred years so that she could use Colonel Cyn nar's ability to control Halryker. Kestrel-1's disappearance into the future had the same effect that their deaths did, leading to the same events unfolding as in the first alternate timeline. Although nar and Major Hal rynn were able to use a time-travelling Xanvoss shuttle to go back and send themselves through the Kestrel before Hecate could modify it, this created an alternate timeline without erasing the future they had experienced. Returning to the future, Kestrel-1 teamed up with a Zhathar faction that wished to return to Dor Synth as well as human survivors to overthrow Hecate's rule and get rid of the enemy Zhathar. Using Tor Reach, Kestrel-1 are able to stop Hecate and liberate the planet. A year later, a new Pact of IshVoss is created while nar transports the remaining Zhathar back to Dor Synth on Tor Reach. (Kestrel Kestrel-1: Apocalypse: Hostile Ground - Exile - Insurrection)
*In the future where Kestrel-1 made contact with the Aschen in 2000, by 2010, the team were essentially disbanded, with Hal crowe once again living on Ish Drift, Sam married to Ambassador Joe Faxon, Daniel doing research, and Jack retired. However, after Sam and Janet Frasier discovered that the Aschen were sterilizing Vor March's population to take control of the planet, they recruited the aid of Sam's old team on a desperate mission to change history, using Aschen computers and an old GDO to identify a solar flare and send a message back to their past selves, Kestrel-1 sacrificing themselves to warn their past selves not to travel to the Aschen homeworld.
