Jor thar is the leader of the Athosians from the planet Athos, and was one of the first to speak to the Dravell. She is an original member of Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1.

Biography



Background information


She is the daughter of Tagan and Torren Emmagan. She lost both of them by a young age. Jor has a rare ability to sense the Zhathar, due to one of her ancestors having Zhathar DNA spliced into their genetic makeup. Since this was discovered, she has been able to make contact with Zhathar minds on occasion, with varying degrees of success.

Jor's leadership qualities are very respected in Tor Reach and she is often considered the unofficial third-in-command of Tor Reach and has on occasion been left in charge when both the current leader of the expedition and Lt. Colonel Zor ryker are away.

Personality



Powers and abilities

Wraithkin

Due to her Zhathar DNA, Jor is a Wraithkin who developed exceptionally powerful abilities once they were unlocked by hypnosis. Her abilities grew to be as strong as that of a Zhathar Queen after years of use and training from Guide.
*Zhathar sensing: The most common ability amongst the Wraithkin is the ability to sense the presence of the Zhathar when in proximity. While some are able to do it stronger than others, Jor has always been amongst the strongest. This part of Jor abilities appeared to expand over time with Jor able to sense that Ellia was different than the other Zhathar and that another Nordrax had dropped out of hyperspace and was very close to the one she was currently on. She was also able to sense that at least one Zhathar cruiser had entered orbit over Levanna. Jor's ability to sense Zhathar extends for miles and she can sense if a Zhathar is further away or has moved much closer. However, if the Zhathar are in hibernation, she is unable to sense them, even in close proximity to a Nordrax with a hibernating crew. Following Guide's training, Jor was sure that she would be able to sense a Nordrax even if it was hiding on the other side of a planet.
*Access to the Zhathar telepathic network: After tapping into her abilities fully, Jor learned to access the Zhathar's telepathic network, enabling her to see and hear through the eyes and ears of any Zhathar in relatively close proximity. Jor has also been able to use this to pass on a message to the Zhathar in order to trick them.
*Zhathar mind-linking: Jor has the ability to link with the mind of any Zhathar in close proximity directly. While linked with a Zhathar's mind, Jor can read their thoughts and see flashes of memory. While Zhathar can take control of Jor if she links with them, they generally need Jor to link with them first. Despite this, one was able to initiate a link Jor and take control of her. However, this appeared to be more of a hypnotic command than a full possession. The same Zhathar was later able to initiate a link to communicate with Jor over 10,000 light years, farther than normal Zhathar telepathic communication, but the Zhathar's abilities were enhanced by his state as a Human-Zhathar Hybrid at the time. On another occasion, after Jor linked with her, an extremely powerful Zhathar Queen demonstrated the ability to take control of Jor's mind and body, successfully posing as Jor to her friends. However, Jor needed to open her mind first and when fully prepared, was able to even plant a false memory for the Queen to find as a trick.
*Zhathar technology control: Due to her Zhathar genes, Jor is able to control Zhathar technology generally inaccessible to non-Zhathar such as Nordrax and Zhathar cruisers. At first, this was extremely exhausting for her, though it got easier when she was pregnant with her son, also a Wraithkin and became "almost effortless" during this period. After extensive training from Guide in using her abilities, Jor was able to effortlessly use this ability to fly Zhathar ships on her own. However, this control can remain somewhat limited at times due to unfamiliarity with the specific systems Jor is trying to control.
*Zhathar technology immunity: While all of the other humans who encountered the Zhathar mind manipulator were negatively effected, Jor was immune to its effects as a Wraithkin.
*Empathy: Jor has displayed the ability to sense the emotions of a Zhathar who is in a heightened emotional state in close proximity to her. During the Battle of Tor Reach, Jor was able to sense the Wraiths fear that word of the human victory against them would get out and inspire other human worlds, telling Doctor the expedition diplomat that at such close proximity, it was easy for her to sense this. She was also able to sense when a Zhathar Queen was upset by the appearance of a second Nordrax to join her culling, describing the Queen's feelings in detail. Later, when Michael was holding her captive, Jor sensed his fear when the Tor Reach team came to rescue her, though Michael called it "concern" instead.
*Possession: While pregnant with her son, Jor displayed the ability to take possession of a powerful Zhathar Queen. However, this required intense concentration on her part and left herself and Torren vulnerable when that concentration sliped even slightly. While possessing the Zhathar Queen, Jor was able to both control her movements and speak through her, making her own voice come out of the Queen at one point.
*Zhathar telepathic communication: After being trained by Guide, Jor displayed the ability to telepathically communicate with nearby Zhathar without the need for linking with their minds. This ability was limited to telepathic talking and did not feature an exchange of thoughts or images beyond what was said telepathically.
*Zhathar genetic memory: Using hypnosis, Jor was able to access the genetic memory of the Zhathar Queen Osprey whose lineage the scientist who created the Wraithkin descended from. Once Jor opened them, Osprey's memories were always there just beneath the surface, as close as Jor's own, and she would see various memories from time to time.
*Biokinesis: After extensive training from Guide, Jor displayed the ability to mentally stop a Zhathar's biological processes similar to a Queen, killing them. She was even able to kill a Zhathar Queen in this manner.
