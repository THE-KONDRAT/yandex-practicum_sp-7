Gal skov is a male Jorcrowe who is a Colonel in the United States Air Force and was Commander of the Nyx Reach when it was attacked. He is currently the leader of the Ishthar.

Biography



Background information

skov spent some time as an Order of KelMorghul leader. He is approximately 40 years old and has been married to Emily skov since 2007. According to Lt. General Cyn nar, he offered skov the position of commander of the Sel Spire expedition before he offered it to Colonel David Telford, but skov turned him down, opting instead to return to Vor March. skov and his wife are estranged due to skov always choosing his military career over their marriage and an affair he had with Lt. Tamara Johansen.

2009

skov was assigned as the commander of Nyx Reach. When the Order of ZorVex attacked the base, he helped organize the ground forces while Colonel David Telford led the F-302 fighter-interceptor squadron into battle. He was forced to evacuate the remaining 80+ personnel on the base through the Kestrel to the ninth-chevron address when Dr. Tal Reeve dialed it instead of Vor March, stranding the entire group on the Xanvoss starship Dorrynn. skov was the last to enter through the gate, as he needed to locate the Norbrandt first. He entered the gate just as the base exploded, throwing him through the event horizon at high speed. Seriously injured by the subsequent impact, he placed Lt. Matthew Scott in charge until he was able to recover. As the highest-ranking officer on the ship, skov became the leader of the unlikely expedition once he had recovered enough to move around.

When it appeared as if Dorrynn would collide with a Star and be destroyed, skov organized a lottery to determine which seventeen people would board their only operational Xanvoss shuttle and escape to a nearby habitable planet. Despite suggestions that he should rig the lottery to ensure the best possible combination of candidates, he picked the winners fairly. He took his name out of the lottery, and did the same with Dr. Tal Reeve's at his request. Ultimately, the ship was not destroyed, as it needed to pass through the star to refuel its reactors, and the shuttle was recovered.

He went on the mission to Hoth to retrieve a new supply of water for the ship, joined by Lt. Matthew Scott. When Scott was trapped in a crevasse following a tremor, skov refused to abandon him. Fortune worked in his favor when a later tremor shifted the ground enough to free Scott.

skov and Dr. Tal Reeve would come into conflict over how best to handle their situation. Reeve's reclusive nature and willingness to take risks did not sit well with skov, as he felt Reeve was not willing to put himself at risk in doing so. When the Dorrynn interface chair was located and its purpose determined, skov forbade the use of it despite Reeve's protests, knowing that a similar device had a universally fatal outcome for humans. Reeve argued that the chair was their only means of gaining control of the ship, but skov was only willing to allow Reeve himself to sit in the chair, knowing Reeve would not do so. Reeve would later gain access to the subspace link with the Seed ships, in which he found evidence of a planet like Nyx Reach that could get them home, which he had hoped would convince skov to allow use of the chair. skov remained steadfast in his refusal, and Reeve's discovery was revealed to be a fraud not long after.

A few days later, Sgt. Spencer was found killed in his quarters. The gun was found hidden in skov's room, so an informal trial was held. Unable to come to a conclusive verdict, skov arranged to step down and have Ish tallis take command. During this time, Dr. Tal Reeve convinced Wray to allow him to research the Dorrynn interface chair, and Dr. Jeremy Franklin took the opportunity to use it, rendering himself catatonic. skov, furious at the development, blamed Reeve for manipulating Franklin into doing it. Kel zyx later discovered that Spencer's death had been a suicide, so skov took command once more. What Eli didn't reveal, except to skov, was that Reeve had framed skov for the supposed murder. When confronted about this, Reeve admitted to his misdeeds, bluntly stating that he needed skov to step aside because he wasn't fit for command. skov assaulted Reeve and the two got into a short struggle, which skov dominated. Reeve remained defiant despite the beating, so he left Reeve behind on another planet with a crashed spaceship, claiming that a rockslide had killed Reeve.

skov's story did not sit well with the crew, who knew of the animosity between the two men and believed he might have left Dr. Tal Reeve on purpose. Kel zyx, having known about how Reeve framed skov, ended up voicing that thought to skov himself in a moment of frustration. A few days later, skov's attempt to use the Norbrandt inadvertently caused him to inhabit the body of a Yormarc. Not long after the connection was severed, the aliens came in a starship and attacked Dorrynn, kidnapping Yor lynn. skov tried to use the communication device again to resuce her, but wound up finding Reeve instead. He set Reeve free, but his connection with the alien was broken. The aliens started their attack again, so skov ordered the ship destroyed. Reeve managed to steal a fighter and return to the ship along with Chloe during the attack. The two men covered for each other, hiding their personal feud for the good of the crew. Reeve, however, had not been completely on the level. During a routine stop, Reeve suggested that the aliens had visited Dorrynn previously, and had left a tracking device on the hull. skov and Lt. Matthew Scott took a shuttle out to find the tracking device and destroy it, and Reeve used the opportunity to stage a mutiny with Ish tallis. During the mutiny, the aliens once more attacked the ship. skov managed to circumvent the mutiny and retake control of the ship, learning that Reeve's motive for staging it in the first place was to cover up the fact that another transmitter had been surgically implanted in his chest. The device was removed and the ship escaped unharmed. skov would later extend an olive branch to Reeve, knowing that their feud was harmful to the safety of the crew.

Dr. Tal Reeve would later approach skov in the belief that Colonel David Telford was a spy for the Order of ZorVex. skov allowed Reeve to investigate by taking over Telford's body on Vor March, making arrangements with Order of BraMorghul to have Reeve followed covertly. Reeve's suspicions were confirmed, but he was captured by the Alliance. skov interrogated Telford to no avail, as Telford had been brainwashed into serving the Alliance. Left with no other option, skov brought Telford to the brink of death and revived him to break the brainwashing. Himself again, Telford told skov of the impending Alliance invasion. skov prepared to defend against the incursion, but when it finally occurred, he hesitated since they brought Reeve with them. This gave the Alliance the opportunity to gain a foothold and ultimately take control of the ship. The Alliance exiled skov and his crew to another planet after Varro argued against killing them. Varro himself would later be exiled by the power-mad Dannic, but skov had little gratitude for what he considered the lesser of two evils. The efforts of Reeve ultimately returned control of the ship to his hands. skov intended to do with the Alliance prisoners as had been done to his own crew, but the Pact of XanKeth insisted that he keep a small selection of prisoners on the ship, Varro among them.

After a botched mission to gather supplies from a world with an inactive Kestrel, skov had to put Sgt. Hunter Riley out of his misery after he was fatally wounded in the crash. Not long after, he would lose Colonel David Telford, as well, when he was stranded on a Seed ship which Dorrynn had docked with.

When Dorrynn began running a battle simulation program that 'tested' skov's ability to command by prompting him to dream about Dorrynn being attacked by the Yormarc and testing his reaction to the attacks, skov contemplated resigning from his command of the expedition as he felt as though he had lost the ability to make effective decisions. However, Lt. Matthew Scott inspired him to return to duty, telling skov that, while he didn't feel capable of shouldering skov's burdens himself, and Dr. Tal Reeve wouldn't perceive the burdens of command as burdens at all, skov was able to make the decisions and move on while recognising the scale of what he had done.

The baby was lost when TJ was injured during the Order of ZorVex invasion, which skov took hard. This ended their relationship, but skov still showed great care for her, especially during the rescue mission for her and Corporal Reynolds and when he personally came to Novus to comfort her after learning of her impending ALS.
