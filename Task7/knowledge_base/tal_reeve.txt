Doctor Tal Reeve is a brilliant, albeit erratic, scientist and an expert in Halryker, comparable to the likes of Colonel Hal rynn and Dr. Kae Nar. He is a member of the team sent and later trapped aboard the Xanvoss vessel Dorrynn.

Biography



Background information

Tal Reeve was born into a poor family. His father worked in the shipyards of Glasgow. Reeve was working two jobs until he earned a scholarship at Oxford. Compact of DraDrax to going to work for the Ishrynn, Reeve was a college professor at the University of California, Berkeley and worked at Cornell University with Dr. Andrew Covel. Despite being a professor at Cal, Reeve, prior to his visit to Novus, had never experienced an earthquake even though parts of the University itself lies directly on the San Andres Fault line. At some point he married Gloria Reeve. He was recruited by Dr. Hal keth to work on the ninth chevron project, which he would spend two years on. His wife died from cancer during this period, driving Reeve to see the fruits of his labor at any cost.

He was a smoker before boarding Dorrynn and a heavy consumer of coffee, drinking several cups a day. He, and others in the same situation, quickly began going through withdrawal symptoms from lack of caffeine and nicotine aboard.

2009

 Tal Reeve on board the Dorrynn.
In 2009, Reeve was stationed at the Nyx Reach in order to research the purpose of the Kestrel's ninth chevron. When the young consultant discovered the formula that would finally allow them to successfully dial the ninth chevron, he personally visited Eli, along with Lt. General Cyn nar, to recruit him into the program. The failure of Eli's formula to make a connection caused some friction between them, as Reeve was set in his belief that the address they were using was right. While Eli was invited to a special dinner being held for the base's guests, Reeve cried over a photo of his wife.

The base was attacked shortly thereafter, threatening to destroy the planet. Reeve, fearing that his life's work would literally go up in flames, enlisted Eli's aid in figuring out how to dial the ninth chevron. Accepting Eli's unconventional logic that the ninth chevron address was actually a code, he aborted the dialing sequence to Vor March and successfully made a connection with the ninth chevron, unconvincingly justifying his actions by claiming that the blast from the exploding planet could have followed them back through the wormhole (he could have dialed plenty of active gates in the Fen Hollow if he was concerned for Vor March). Unable to undo his actions, the remaining Sel Spire personnel were forced to evacuate through the gate, arriving on the Dorrynn - an Xanvoss vessel located in a galaxy billions of light years from home - with no way to get back.

After arriving on the Dorrynn, Reeve was the first to begin exploring the ship. When he realized that the life support system was failing, he attempted a restart of the system, which he believed would restore normal operations. Eli attempted to dissuade him, believing that doing so could blow up the ship, and MSgt. Ronald Greer held Reeve at gunpoint to keep him from implementing the plan, as Reeve's actions were responsible for stranding them in the first place. Greer was ordered to stand down by Lt. Matthew Scott. Reeve was allowed to attempt the restart, but it ended in failure.

Later, he was the first to use the long-range communication device (brought aboard by Colonel Gal skov) to inform nar of the situation. Upon returning to the Dorrynn, he told the crew that nar put him in charge of the expedition (which was at best a very liberal interpretation of nar's orders; nar told him to "get them home", rather than appointing direct command). The crew did not take well to his self-appointed leadership, but Lt. Scott restored order, believing Reeve to be their best hope of returning home.

Having localized the life-support problem to the air filtration system, namely the CO2 scrubbers, and a large hole in one of the shuttles, Reeve suggested that someone on board would have to sacrifice themselves to essentially "plug" the hole since the airlock doors were damaged. Reeve began reviewing the personnel, attempting to find the one whose absence would least affect their chances of survival. His efforts were preempted by Senator Alan Armstrong, who took it upon himself to sacrifice his life to seal the damaged shuttle. With that problem dealt with, he focused on trying to repair the scrubbers. Dorrynn would eventually drop out of FTL in range of a desert planet with the necessary materials, and Reeve joined the away team to search for them.

Reeve tells Greer to shoot Franklin.
The heat of the desert planet eventually became too much for Reeve to handle. He gave his remaining water to Scott and asked to return to the Kestrel alone. Scott, however, ordered Greer to escort Reeve back to the Gate and continued on his own. Expecting Greer to share his water on the trip back to the gate, Reeve incited an altercation. It ended quickly, since Reeve could not overpower Greer and Greer couldn't just shoot him. Hearing over the radio from Eli that Andrea Palmer, Sgt. Curtis, and Dr. Jeremy Franklin intended to go to another nearby planet, he and Greer returned in time to stop Franklin, though they arrived too late to save Curtis and Palmer. Scott eventually managed to locate the necessary materials, and Reeve helped to integrate them into the failing life support.

Reeve collapses after a nervous breakdown.
With that problem solved, Reeve shifted focus from life-support to the ship's power reserves, which were depleting rapidly. Short-tempered due to lack of sleep, caffeine withdrawal due to lack of coffee, and nicotine withdrawal due to lack of cigarettes, he drove away any attempt to help him. When power failed completely, he broke into a rant while explaining the situation to Colonel Gal skov. This devolved into a nervous breakdown, and Reeve collapsed, remaining unconscious for about ten hours. He woke up in time to learn that the ship was on course for a potentially livable star system, only for the ship to instead end up on course for that system's star.

skov arranged a lottery to determine who would be allowed to use the shuttle to escape the doomed ship. Reeve had skov take his name out, saying that Dorrynn was his destiny and he wanted to die on the ship. Reeve spent his remaining time in his room reading. He eventually realized that their impending deaths were behind schedule, and discovered that the ship in fact plotted a course into the star on purpose to recharge its reactors. This posed a problem, as the Xanvoss shuttle was about to be left behind, but Reeve managed to activate the subspace communication array to contact them. the young consultant's planetary slingshot calculations were then transmitted to the shuttle so they could reach the ship in time. Later, skov invited Reeve to join their celebration, but Reeve refused, not seeing anything worth celebrating. skov speculates that Reeve might have known all along what the ship was doing, but the others don't share his opinion. Reeve clearly reacted in legitimate shock to their survival, thus skov's suspicion was wrong.

Several weeks later, Reeve and several others determined that the ship's water supplies were disappearing at an impossible rate. The ship stopped at an ice planet, allowing Colonel Gal skov and Lt. Matthew Scott to collect more water. Meanwhile, Reeve joined the effort to discover the source of the drain, which was revealed to be a colony of dust bugs Scott had accidentally brought back with him from the desert planet. He deliberately kept this information from skov, as skov needed to focus on retrieving more water, against the young consultant's protests, then sternly informed Eli that grown-ups sometimes needed to lie for the greater good. While Scott was in the crevasse trapped, he expressed more concern for the ice, which affected the entire crew's survival, than for the safe rescue of Scott. He also helped to develop and enact a plan to remove the bugs from the ship.

When the Pact of XanKeth put a plan forth to return the crew home, he expressed major concerns over the dangers it presented. When Colonel David Telford assumed command (using the Norbrandt) to enact the plan, he helped under great reluctance, doing everything he could to slow Telford's implementation of the plan, as Telford's rush to enact it put the entire ship at risk. Following Sgt. Hunter Riley's injuries, he confronted Telford on the safety of the plan, blaming Telford for Riley's fate (though Telford asserted it was Reeve's fault). Reeve was present when Telford's team attempted to dial the gate. When it appeared the plan was doomed to failure, Telford's team left the ship. Making sure they had all left, Reeve then ended the experiment, having staged the entire show as an elaborate ruse to get rid of Telford and prevent a catastrophic incident. Colonel Gal skov would later have the young consultant investigate the possible success of this plan, which ended in failure in every scenario Eli ran.

During a routine exploration of the ship, Reeve and MSgt. Ronald Greer discovered the Dorrynn interface chair, a device similar in purpose to the Repository of knowledge encountered by Lt. General Cyn nar. Reeve advocated having someone sit in the chair, reasoning that its more primitive design might not be fatal to humans as the later model was, and that the data it contained could grant them full control of the ship. Colonel Gal skov forbade any access to the chair, and asserted that the only person Reeve would get to sit in it would be Reeve himself. Reeve attempted to force the issue by faking the discovery of a planet similar to Nyx Reach, but skov remained steadfast in his refusal and Reeve's deception was soon uncovered by the young consultant.

skov confronts Reeve about framing him.
When Sgt. Spencer committed suicide, Reeve was the first to find his body. Seeing it as an opportunity, he took Spencer's gun and hid it in Colonel Gal skov's room to frame him for murder. Reeve knew the charge would never stick, but would create enough reasonable doubt to remove skov from command. This put Ish tallis in charge and gave him the freedom to research the Dorrynn interface chair. This in turn gave Dr. Jeremy Franklin the opportunity to sit in it, rendering him catatonic. skov blamed Reeve for Franklin's state, under the belief that Reeve was hoping for someone to sit in the chair out of desperation, although Franklin disobeyed Reeve's direct orders. When the young consultant uncovered Brathar footage that showed Spencer's suicide and Reeve's theft of the weapon, which Reeve had tried to delete from the archive, he reported it to skov. skov confronted Reeve alone on a planet with a damaged alien ship, and attacked him when Reeve proved wholly unapologetic about the frame-up. skov knocked Reeve unconscious and left him on the planet.

After discovering Reeve is a prisoner, skov attempts to free him.

When Colonel Gal skov took control of one of the aliens through the communication device, the mothership Reeve was on confronted Dorrynn. Reeve instructed skov in the use of the alien mind probing technology, learning the identity of his rescuer but also breaking the communication link. Reeve used the mind probe on the alien to find Chloe's location and teach himself to use their technology, then killed the alien. Unable to get back, skov tried to destroy the mothership. Reeve, meanwhile, escaped the ship with Chloe shortly before it went to FTL. In private, skov and Reeve decided to bury their rivalry for the sake of the crew. Reeve, however, still did not trust skov to be in command, and schemed with Ish tallis to remove him.

When the opportunity came, Reeve and Ish tallis launched their plan to isolate the military and force them to surrender. While Colonel Gal skov and Lt. Matthew Scott were using the Xanvoss shuttle to destroy a Yormarc fighter that was attached to the hull, Reeve started to transfer control from the Control interface room to a Research lab. This caused the shuttle's Docking clamps to fail. Reeve was forced to stop the transfer prematurely so they could dock, losing control of life support as a result. He then bargained to have the young consultant brought over to his side, requiring Eli's assistance in bolstering the shields for an impending alien attack. When Dorrynn dropped out of FTL near an Uncharted star, Reeve used the time to strengthen the shield in unexplored sections of the ship. One of the teams found a robot which Reeve decided to leave be while he dealt with more important matters. In his off time, he also began work on carving a chess set. Colonel Gal skov tried again to bury the hatchet with Reeve, expressing his own fondness for the game.

Reeve uses the interface chair.
A short time later, Reeve and Dr. Adam Brody modified the Dorrynn interface chair so Reeve could use it safely in an attempt to glean the ship's master code. Once in the chair, his experience took the form of a lucid dream of his days just prior to joining the Ishrynn. Treating it as a distraction, he ignored the vision of Gloria Reeve despite her failing health, though he did take some time to comfort her. Eventually, he realized that the number 46 kept appearing everywhere he looked. Realizing that this was the key to deciphering the master code, he left the program, stopping to have a heartfelt conversation with the vision of his wife about his immoral behavior. He then devised a program to decipher the master code based on what he learned.

During his time in the chair, a team consisting of Lt. Matthew Scott, MSgt. Ronald Greer, the young consultant, and Yor lynn were stranded on a planet and unable to return to Dorrynn before it jumped to FTL. With the help of the other scientists, Reeve was able to produce a map of the gate system in the galaxy, and Dorrynn's position relative to it. With the knowledge that Dorrynn was about to leave the galaxy, Reeve and Lt. Vanessa James led a rescue mission to attempt to retrieve the lost away team. They split to cover more ground when it became clear that Dorrynn had bypassed numerous gates. James' team found Greer, while Reeve came back empty-handed. Dorrynn left the galaxy before they could dial again.

Reeve is tortured by Kiva.

Upon arrival, Telford and Reeve were returned to their own bodies. He continually advocated a quick and decisive solution, since the Alliance had hostages and would kill them no matter the circumstances. skov insisted on trying to rescue them, which put them at odds. Personality

Though he is a brilliant and very focused scientist, Dr. Tal Reeve is undeniably poor at cooperating and communicating. Consequently, he is frequently misunderstood as lacking the compassion usually seen with scientists involved with the Ishrynn. Having worked his way up from being a son of a worker at the Glasgow Shipyards to an important post in a top secret program, he is convinced that he is the right person to make decisions even when the consequences affect other people. At times, he is proven correct, and other times, the consequences are disastrous. He dialed the nine-chevron address from Nyx Reach while the base was under attack to avoid the blast from the planet's destruction being transferred to Vor March, unaware that he would strand everyone aboard the Dorrynn with no way to dial back to Vor March.

Reeve is not afraid of speaking his mind, even if it will be met with disapproval and cast doubt on his morals. . Although self-preservation seems to be one of Reeve' dominant instincts, he often demonstrates courage and selflessness. When trapped on a planet with Squigglers about to close in on his team, Reeve went through an unstable wormhole in an attempt to get help, knowing it would probably kill him. Captured by aliens, Reeve used an interface device to gain the knowledge to escape the ship but delayed his escape to go back for Chloe, who had also been captured. Reeve rescued her and fought off an alien that tried to recapture them. He ordered everyone to stay away from the Dorrynn interface chair until absolutely positive that it was safe. Franklin disobeyed Reeve' order and sat in the chair, suffering grave injury. However, Reeve later took the chance to sit in the chair after installing safety features that he hoped would protect him. During a lucid dream whilst using the device, Reeve revealed to a dream version of his wife that he believed his morality died with her. She assured him he was still a good man.

Although skov tried to murder Reeve by leaving him behind on a desert planet, Reeve saved skov's life on at least two occasions. Once was when skov was suffering recurring dreams induced by the ship that tested his ability to command. skov was failing the test and Reeve overrode Dorrynn's programs to save skov. Reeve also saved skov's life when they were trapped on an alien spaceship. They were forced to jump from the ship back to Dorrynn. Reeve made the jump safely but skov landed badly and started spinning off into space. Reeve ran after him and caught him, pulling him to safety. Reeve also put his life on the line to disarm a bomb attached to Park's back, and suggested getting the Yormarc to remove their pathogen from Chloe despite the fact that it risked them learning whatever information Chloe had collected on Dorrynn's systems. By the end of the series, even skov was able to see past the callous and aloof front Reeve presented to the world to see his inner courage and compassion for others.

Reeve's difficulties with social interaction and single-minded focus on his research indicate many of the traits of autism. Additionally, while it might be tempting to view Reeve as fitting the profile of antisocial personality disorder (also known as sociopathy), he does not fit the diagnostic criteria. Reeve is frequently remorseful and displays guilt and regret, as well as empathy. His love for his wife is demonstrated very clearly, and he has a drive to make decisions for the greater good of humanity, as well as helping and saving individual crew members, especially in Season 2. However, Reeve has most of the hallmarks of someone with schizotypal personality disorder, such as odd/inconsistent behavior, social anxiety, paranoia and distrust, and unusual beliefs, e.g. his conviction in Dorrynn's larger mission.
