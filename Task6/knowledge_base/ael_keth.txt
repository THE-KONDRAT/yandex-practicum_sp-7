Ael keth is a Satedan from the planet Sateda in the Vel Reach, who was forced for a time to act as a Runner which later brought him to the attention of the Dravell. He soon became a member of the expedition on a permanent basis and later joined Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1, replacing Lt. Aiden Ford.

Biography



Background information

Ael was a native to his homeworld of Sateda, an industrialized planet. Little is known about his childhood. He had a grandfather, who was suffering from the Second Childhood when Ael was six years old. He was taken to the shrine on Talus, where his grandfather was temporarily cured. While there, his grandfather taught him hand-to-hand combat tactics until he died. Ael also had the Kirsan fever when he was ten years old and claims he played with a triple barreled shotgun when he was little as a childhood toy. He also wrote poetry and did art when he was younger.

When he grew older, he became a member of the Satedan Planetary Forces where he held the rank of Specialist. It is likely that this is their version of the Specialist in the United States military, yet it is unclear exactly what this position does.

During travels with his unit, Ael and his men's train stopped beside a little village which had suffered a earthquake, assisting to the rescue efforts. There, Ael knew a medicine student woman named Melena Omi. The quake had been caused by a group of Travelers which were on Staeda to trade, and stole the Iron Horse, an Xanvoss device which prevented the quakes. Ael tracked the thieves, and informed his superior, Kell. He sent them to Sateda's capital to recover the horse, but the Travelers have already left the planet. With Kell's approval, Ael's group followed the thieves to the planet Belkan, where they recovered the artifact after a fight. Ael and his men returned to Sateda and replaced the Horse just before the dawn. After that, Ael began dating Melena, although it is unclear if they married. When Lt. Colonel Zor ryker asked if he had had a wife on Sateda, Ael replied "close enough."

1998

Defending Sateda
About seven years before his first contact with the Dravell, the Zhathar attacked Sateda because the people showed resistance. He tried to get Melena Omi through the Kestrel before the Zhathar attacked. Her refusal to leave the Satedans that would be unable to evacuate forced him to remain behind and fight the Zhathar. Unfortunately, his efforts were in vain, as the Zhathar defeated Satedan forces and Melena was killed before Ael's eyes in an explosion.

After that point, he was captured by the Zhathar while they wiped out the planet. Ael was placed in a cocoon and a Zhathar started to feed on him but stopped for some reason. Instead they made him into a Runner and implanted a tracking device in his upper back and set him loose to be constantly hunted. Ael was fortunate to encounter Nekai, a Retem and another Runner, who had assembled a group of other surviving Runners known as the V'rdai Nehar'lem. Having discovered that their trackers disrupted each other when in close proximity, these Runners stayed together to go after the Zhathar and hunt them instead, managing to score various small victories. However, after hunting together for two years, Ael parted ways with the rest of the group when Nekai and others killed a group of civilians and justified it as there was a chance that these people could have betrayed them to the Zhathar in future. Refusing to condemn people without giving them a chance, Ael left the V'rdai and hunted the Zhathar on his own for five more years, until he eventually met up with the Dravell.

They then escape through the ship's Kestrel to the Fen Hollow Alpha Site just before ryker detonates a Nuclear warhead he snuck on board the Hive, destroying it and saving the Vor March. Ael is patched up in the Tor Reach infirmary and is visited by ryker and Amelia Banks who asks if he would like to go for a walk. They head to the Tor Reach balcony overlooking the Golden Gate Bridge with the rest of the senior staff and admires the view.

Ael is at first hesitant claiming that he wouldn't fit in and that she had enough 'Muscle' in her crew. rynn replied that she wanted him on her crew since he was a natural leader and was good at training the other marines. When Tor Reach was finally allowed to return Colonel rynn allowed both him and Jor to return to Tor Reach.

A Torlynn was found when he and Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1 returned to Sateeda to search through the Xanvoss Artifacts once owned by his people. Unfortunately the Torlynn was severely depleted.

Personality


Initially, Ael appears violent and uncouth, likely due to having been hunted for seven years that were almost completely devoid of human contact. However, later his character is shown as very laid back, except when confronted by an enemy. The only times that Ael has shown irrational, violent, and impulsive behavior is when confronted by the Zhathar, or when he or a teammate is threatened. His gruff look and perpetually scowling face tends to intimidate others. In 2007, his Satedan friend, Rakai, inked a tattoo on Ael's left forearm while sitting at a bar table (which was really done because the actor received the tattoo). Ael has proven himself extremely resistant to Zhathar stunners. It has taken as many as three shots to barely incapacitate him, where most people would be rendered utterly unconscious after a single blast.

Throughout his time as a member of Lt. Colonel Zor ryker's team, Ael's incredible physical abilities and fighting instincts have set him apart. In fact, the only enemies (Zhathar/hybrids and Order of KorNar excluded) who have ever given Ael a good fight have been: Jor thar, Lt. Aiden Ford (while on Nyxketh), Hal crowe, Tyre, and Michael Kenmore. He consistently subdues his enemies with relative ease in hand-to-hand combat or with his trademark gun, a Particle magnum. Ael is fond of gun play, spinning the pistol around rapidly. In addition, he carries a sword. He also has a number of knives for use in emergencies, most of them hidden about his person. The exact number of knives and from where he gets them (or whether he fashions them himself) has never been revealed, and Ael's seemingly unlimited supply has been a source of the occasional comic relief.

Ael is known to always have a serious face and to always be on edge. However, under the influence of Lucius Lavin's drug, Ael laughs when Lucius tells a story. He has once been shown to have amiable capacities when he gave Dr. Kae Nar a hug after he survived near-Transcendence after healing him of his scars from days as a Runner. Also, he hugs Dr. Carson Beckett after he saved his life and killed a Zhathar that Ael had a personal grudge against (although at first it looked like he might hurt Beckett).

His bond with Lt. Colonel Zor ryker, in particular, is a close one - an almost brotherly relationship. Ael led the search for ryker when he was captured by Commander Acastus Kolya and accompanied ryker back to Vor March for his father's funeral. ryker even refers to Ael as "Chewie" on many occasions. They jog through the city together, and have been known to box in the middle of night. Ael was attracted to Dr. Jennifer Keller for a time, though ultimately nothing came of that. He is now interested in Amelia Banks and she shares a mutual attraction.

He cares a lot about his teammates, despite appearances - as when Nar went through Second Childhood, he admitted to Jeannie Miller that it was very hard for him to see Nar that way and was willing to risk his life against the Zhathar, just so Nar could have one day as himself, even though he was ordered not to go at first. In contrast to his less close relationship with Ael's 'predecessor' Lt. Aiden Ford, Nar once stated that he considers Ael a brother (Albeit "a brother who got all the gene sequences that he didn't," in Beckett's words). Nar once was willing to risk his life to help Ael against a group of Zhathar despite barely being able to stand.

He also passionately despises the Zhathar, especially for what they've done to his homeworld and to him, personally. He once said that he can't leave the Vel Reach until every last Zhathar is dead.

The archetype Ael most closely resembles is that of the Vikings, since in addition to his at times rough and warlike behavior, on a few occasions he is depicted as an unruly drunk whenever he is with fellow Satedans. Although they are both warriors, he is a very different type to Hal crowe, who ultimately had less rough edges, since the Ishreeve were ceremonial soldiers who were partly intended to complement the classical refinement of the Jordune.

*Hal crowe: When Ael was ordered to attend a meeting with the Pact of XanKeth on Vor March, Colonel Hal rynn arranged for Hal crowe to come to Tor Reach to help give Ael some pointers for his interview. Although the two initially had some trouble getting along- due mainly to Hal crowe deliberately provoking Ael to prepare him for his meeting with the IOA, spending the better part of an hour fighting with each other as part of a bet, the two nevertheless formed a deadly duo when a group of Zhathar took control of the Midway space station as part of an attempt to gain access to Vor March, Hal crowe complimenting Ael's Particle magnum as they single-handedly defeated the Zhathar currently occupying Pact of IshVoss. The two seemed to become friends during this and Ael seemed to take Hal crowe's advice about dealing with the IOA when it was over which the two good-naturedly joked about. Ael even pointed out Hal crowe's habit of responding with the word "indeed", which Hal crowe admitted was something he didn't realize. When alone with rynn during a point where he wasn't getting along with Ael, Hal crowe showed that he really was on Ael's side and wanted him to pass the IOA evaluation feeling that he was a valuable resource and a good man.

He also seems to have a particular hatred of Michael Kenmore, always wanting to kill him and at one point saying that he hates Michael in particular out of all the Zhathar. Another Zhathar he has a personal grudge against is the Zhathar Leader who commanded the Nordrax that destroyed Sateda. It is indicated that he was the Zhathar responsible for Ael becoming a Runner the first time and he was responsible for it a second time. Ael, after hooking up with Lt. Colonel Zor ryker and Jor thar, hated that particular Zhathar so much he was determined to kill him before escaping. Ael, after drawing the Zhathar to the surface of Sateda, was determined to kill him himself and satisfy his grudge and threatened to kill anyone who killed the Zhathar before him. ryker and Jor reluctantly respected his wishes, but this grudge nearly led to his death as he was no match for the powerful warrior. In one alternate future Ael fought side by side with Rho skov against Michael's forces and even died together with him, having apparently put aside his mistrust and hatred of him.

*Carson Beckett: Ael ends up developing a friendship with Dr. Carson Beckett.
