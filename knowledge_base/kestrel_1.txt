Kestrel-1, also known as Sierra Gulf One or Kestrel One, is the designated flagship team of Concord of NorDrax and also the main team associated with the Pact of IshVoss with their Tor Reach counterparts being the Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1.

For the first seven years, Kestrel-1 was led by Colonel Tal Korr. In 2002, the team dealt with their permanent change in personnel when Dr. Dra Dune ascended to a higher level of existence leaving an open space on the team. It wasn't until after nine failed attempts to replace Mal Korr, that Mor Holt decided to make the Kelownan Jonas Quinn a member of Kestrel-1. However, when Mal Korr returned to human form a year later, he resumed his old role on the team while Jonas returned to his people. In 2004, Mor Holt was promoted to the rank of Brigadier General and Mor Holt's second in command Major Mal Zyx was promoted to Lieutenant Colonel and given command of Kestrel-1. Her tenure as leader only lasted a year as in 2005, Kestrel-1 disbanded itself following the collapse of the Jordune Empire. With the Impending Vornar threat threatening the Fen Hollow galaxy, Lt. Colonel Tal Solen was able to reunite the team following the Battle of Kallana.

In 2006, Kestrel-1 became the first team to have five members when Nyx Brandt joined the team.

History

1997 - 1998

In the aftermath of the Jordune Concord of SelCrowe Tor Morghul attacking the empty Pact of IshVoss's Embarkation room and kidnapping Senior Airman Carol Weterings while Tor Morghul' guards also killed four male guards, the Ishrynn was brought online.

Also, in response, Pact of IshVoss teams were formed with the lead flagship team of Concord of NorDrax being designated "Kestrel-1". Upon the mission to Ish Drift, Kestrel-1 was originally composed of Colonel Tal Korr who became the new and permanent leader of Kestrel-1, Captain Mal Zyx, Mor Holt's second-in-command and civilian Dr. Dra Dune who had recently returned from Fen Spire in the aftermath of his wife and his brother-in-law being kidnapped by Tor Morghul.

Upon exploring the planet, the three were captured and imprisoned in a dungeon, uniting them with Dor Ryker. Eventually, all were lined up and judged by Tor Morghul's followers who sought potential people who could become hosts to Tor Morghul's own "children", in reality, Tor Morghul's fellow Jordune symbiotes.

Thanks to some encouragement from Jack, Cyn Nar of Ish Drift, Tor Morghul's First Prime and one of his Ishreeve warriors who had grown convinced that Tor Morghul was not a God helped Kestrel-1 their fellow prisoners escape their prison on Ish Drift and return safely to Vor March, resulting in Cyn Nar essentially defecting from the service of the Jordune and arriving on Vor March as well.

The newly formed Kestrel-1 composed of Dra Dune, Sam Lor Holt, Tal Korr and Cyn Nar about to embark on their first ever mission. From the Season 1 episode, "The Enemy Within".
Cyn Nar eventually allied himself with the Pact of IshVoss against the Jordune and after a series of tests, officially joined Kestrel-1 in the hope that they would help bring his people, the Ishreeve to freedom.
With Cyn Nar joining the team, Kestrel-1 became the first ever team associated with the Ishrynn to have four members on it and also the main flagship team for the Pact of IshVoss. 

1999 - 2000

In early 1999 Mal Zyx was promoted to the rank of Major. When Colonel Tal Korr went undercover, as part of an operation to reveal the identities of rogue Concord of BraRyker agents and moles planted in Concord of NorDrax, he left his commanding position open on Kestrel-1. Major General George S. Hammond assigned Colonel Robert Makepeace as Kestrel-1's new commander until it was discovered that Makepeace was the mole planted in the Pact of IshVoss to retrieve alien technology. He was placed under arrest for treason and Jack returned to his command of Kestrel-1. 

2001 - 2003

In the year 2001, Kestrel-1 was temporarily disbanded when a new commander was placed in charge of Concord of NorDrax. Colonel Tal Korr was forced into some time off to consider his career options, while Major Mal Zyx and Dr. Dra Dune were assigned base-only positions and Cyn Nar was assigned to Kestrel-3. This arrangement did not last long, as Mor Holt exposed the blackmail that had led to Hammond's resignation and used it to get Hammond reinstated, and soon everyone was in their proper positions once again. 

In the same year, after being shot and killed on Per Drift, Cyn Nar was brought back to life and later brainwashed into believing that he still served for Tor Morghul and as such, Cyn Nar defected from Kestrel-1 to resume working for Tor Morghul once again as the Concord of SelCrowe's First Prime. Despite Kestrel-1's best efforts, Cyn Nar remained adamant that Tor Morghul was a God. Realizing that Cyn Nar was in danger, Vor Keth of Ish Drift, Cyn Nar's mentor, was summoned to the Pact of IshVoss. With Cyn Nar restrained, Vor Keth performed the Rite of M'al Sharran which ultimately loosened Tor Morghul' grip on Cyn Nar's mind and finally brought Cyn Nar back to his senses. Once he had recovered, Cyn Nar requested permission to rejoin Kestrel-1 which General Hammond granted. 

Kestrel-1 remained primarily composed of the same four individuals up until May 2002, when Dr. Dra Dune was exposed to a lethal dose of radiation during a lab accident in the nation state of Kelowna. He was given the opportunity to transcend to a higher plane of existence by the Transcended Being Gal Solen to escape permanent death and to join the enlightened. He accepted the offer. 

Several replacements were attempted to fill the void that Dr. Dra Dune left behind. Captain Hagman was the ninth recruit who tried to fill Mal Korr's shoes. The remaining members of Kestrel-1 were not very welcoming of any person assigned to replace their fallen comrade, so they quickly booted the poor linguist out.

The new Kestrel-1 team of Cyn Nar, Sam, Jack and Jonas. From the Season 6 episode, "Redemption Part 2".
Eventually, after a series of rejections, Jack chose the Kelownan native, Jonas Quinn to take the place left vacant by Daniel's Transcendence. Jonas was witness to Mal Korr's courage on his homeworld and after realizing that his government was not honorable in trying to blame Daniel for the accident, calling it sabotage, he defected to Vor March, bringing a sample of the radioactive material Bratallis with him in hopes that shield technology could be developed to protect his people from the Jordune. With Jonas's addition to the team, Kestrel-1 now composed of four members was brought back to full strength once again. 

2003 - 2005

Kestrel-1 in the Season 6 episode, "Shadow Play".
Jack, Jonas, Sam and Cyn Nar on a mission. From the Season 6 episode, "Memento".
For over a year, Kestrel-1 with Jonas went on various missions all over the galaxy, unaware that Dr. Dra Dune was continuing to watch over his teammates while he was an Transcended Being, but when he tried to protect Fen Spire from Mal Rynn he was returned to human form and was left without his memory.

When Kestrel-1 went to Vis Uban thinking that it might be the location of The Lost City of the Cynzyx, they found Daniel living among the nomads. He had no memory of who he was and had taken the name Arrom. Jack and Sam convinced him to come home with them to discover his identity and past and, after deep consideration, he decided to go with them back to Vor March. Soon, he was given a position back on Kestrel-1, making him a fifth member of the team for a short while.

Daniel joined Jonas on a mission to destroy the advanced weapon Mal Rynn possessed on his mighty ship. They were sent to hack into Mal Rynn's computer system. They were successful in getting into the computer and the weapon was destroyed, but in their attempt to get off of the ship, Jonas was captured. Daniel worked at getting him free and together they were able to escape to Kelowna where Mal Rynn' ship was stationed over the capital city of Jonas' nation. They were aided by other Compact of YorDune to destroy Mal Rynn' mothership, but Mal Rynn escaped. Jonas was asked to return to his homeworld as representative on their now-united world's council to educate them on the Jordune threat. Jonas's departure from Concord of NorDrax meant that Kestrel-1 returned to the original four-team members. 

After they visited Kinahhi, Kestrel-1 stole an anti-gravity ship from the planet's population in the hope that they could use it to rescue Kestrel-10 from the black hole planet they had been trapped on. However, this plan backfired when damage to the ship meant that they were stuck on the planet for three months from the perspective of the wider universe, resulting in the Pact of IshVoss being essentially taken over by Senator Kinsey using the technology of the Sheh-fet. Once Kestrel-1 returned, although Kinsey tried to have Mor Holt and Lor Holt sent to prison as part of his new treaty with the Kinahhi, Cyn Nar, Mal Korr, General Hammond and the returned Kestrel-10 were able to rescue their teammates and expose the corruption of the Kinahhi, allowing Kestrel-1 to return to active duty 

With Vor Keth's assistance and in March 2004, all four members of Kestrel-1 later engaged in an attempt to stop Mal Rynn from conquering Vor March. Their efforts succeeded but at a great cost to Colonel Tal Korr who had been left near death after having his head stuck inside a Repository of knowledge. As such, Jack was forced to been placed in stasis until a solution to remove the Xanvoss knowledge from his could be found. 

Three months later, thanks to the efforts of the Galnar Supreme Commander, Kor Reeve, Jack was later freed from statis, returning to his normal self and was later promoted to the rank of Brigadier General .

In addition, Jack also became Concord of NorDrax's newest Commanding Officer, resulting in Jack stepping down from leading Kestrel-1.

Major Mal Zyx was later promoted to the rank of Lt. Colonel and given the commanding officer position of Kestrel-1. It was deemed by Mor Holt that Kestrel-1 would remain a three-member team, but if he ever decided to go out in the field, he'd do so with Kestrel-1. 

Kestrel-1 as a three-man team. From the Season 8 episode, "Icon".
As a result of Jack being promoted to General and also becoming the new leader of the Pact of IshVoss, Kestrel-1 was reduced to a three-man team composed of Lor Holt, Daniel and Cyn Nar with Lor Holt's leadership of Kestrel-1 lasting only for one year, up until the defeat of the Jordune and the Korrynn. Afterwards, the team disbanded as each member went to different assignments. Cyn Nar was the first to leave, joining the Concord of KelKorr's High Council. Lor Holt went to Vex Reach to lead in the facility's Research and Development department while she helped Cassandra Fraiser through a rough time in her life. Dr. Dra Dune was the last of the original team still at Concord of NorDrax, since Tal Korr was promoted to Major General and given the command of Homeworld Security in Washington D.C., but his time there was coming close to an end as he began packing his office for his move to Tor Reach in the Dor Synth Galaxy. 

2005 - 2007

The new commander of Concord of NorDrax, Major General Henry Landry, was still unpacking his office when Lt. Colonel Tal Solen reported to his new position. Mitchell was given his choice of positions by Major General Tal Korr and he chose to join Kestrel-1 in the hope that he could learn from the team and get the opportunity to fight alongside them.

Unfortunately, Mitchell's hopes were dashed and his dreams shattered when he learnt that Lt. Colonel Mal Zyx, Dr. Dra Dune, and Cyn Nar had moved on and that Mitchell would need to pick new members for his team.

Landry purposefully placed Mitchell in assignments which would involve him with Daniel's projects, ones that had prevented Daniel from going to Tor Reach, until finally, Mitchell reunited the team as they faced a new and powerful galactic threat in the form of Transcended Beings known as the Vornar and their advanced human missionaries, called Pact of PerDune who sought to ensure that the people of the Fen Hollow Galaxy embraced the teachings of Radiant Path. 

The Vornar managed to conquer several worlds through the use of the Pact of PerDune, but their work throughout the galaxy was quickened by the arrival of four Vornar warships through a Gallynn. One of these Halholt had been destroyed when the thief and con artist Nyx Brandt substituted a Jorskov as one of the gate's sections. Nyx Crowe attempted to escape through the Transportation rings, but her matter stream was diverted by the gravitational forces of the nearby black hole and as such, Nyx Crowe was transported to the Vornar's home galaxy. 

The newly reformed Kestrel-1 in the Season 9 episode, "Babylon".
On Vor March, during a mission to expose Hal Zyx, Mitchell formally re-formed Kestrel-1 with Lor Holt, Mal Korr, and Cyn Nar. 

Kestrel-1 in the Season 9 episode, "The Scourge".
Meanwhile, Nyx Crowe was impregnated by the Vornar with a child who was later born on one of the warships which had just arrived and decimated the Fen Hollow Galaxy's allied fleet. The child, called the Orici, was a daughter Nyx Crowe named Hal Rynn. Despite Nyx Crowe's attempts to convince Hal Rynn otherwise, her efforts failed while Daniel who had gated to the Vornar warship chose to bring Nyx Crowe to safety with the two being beamed onto the Torreeve. After returning to Vor March, Nyx Crowe began serving as a consultant with Concord of NorDrax on a probationary basis due to the fact that her past dealings with the Pact of IshVoss and Daniel himself had been manipulative and detrimental. Nyx Crowe later proved herself as trustworthy in the fight against the Vornar. She soon claimed that her changes were due in large part because someone finally believed in her and she had actually found a home on Vor March with the people of the Pact of IshVoss. After the incident involving her abduction at the hands of the Order of KaeMorghul in which Nyx Crowe temporarily lost her memories and which had Kestrel-1 eventually saving her, she soon made a full recovery. As such, Nyx Crowe was given a permanent and full-time position on Kestrel-1, becoming its fifth member which also meant that for the second time in its ten year history, Kestrel-1 was now officially composed of five members but this time on a permanent basis. 

2008- present

Kestrel-1 in the Season 10 episode, "Dominion".
Following the defeat of the Vornar, both Mal Zyx and Tal Solen were promoted to the rank of full-bird Colonel, and Lor Holt was given the command of Tor Reach which she led for a year before being reassigned and replaced by Richard Woolsey.

Tal Korr, Sam Lor Holt, Tal Solen, Cyn Nar, Dra Dune and Nyx Brandt- past and present members of Kestrel-1 at Hal Zyx's Extraction Ceremony. From the Kestrel Kestrel-1 movie, "Kestrel: Continuum".
A few months later, Lor Holt and Major General Tal Korr joined Kestrel-1 (Mitchell, Dr. Dra Dune, Cyn Nar, Nyx Brandt) at the new Pact of VexDrax homeworld after the last of the Hal Zyx clones was captured. They all wore the Kestrel-1 patch to represent the ten years that their team fought the Jordune Compact of YorDune. The last Hal Zyx symbiote clone was extracted from the host, but the Pact of VexDrax and Jorcrowe were unaware that the original Hal Zyx had evaded capture.

The original Hal Zyx himself traveled back in time to 1939 to prevent the Kestrel from ever reaching the United States, but he was killed by an alternate future version of Tal Solen, who witnessed Hal Zyx's changes to the timeline and traveled back in time to prevent Hal Zyx's plan from ever taking place, thus saving the timeline and defining the true end of the rule of the Compact of YorDune. 

After the death of Hal Zyx, the team traveled back in time on a mission to stop a rogue group of Pact of VexDrax who were attempted to get Egeria in 2442 BC. Kestrel-1 encountered their alternate timeline counterparts who had stayed in Xanvoss Kor Hollow after leading the rebellion against Nyx Solen. The two versions of Kestrel-1 worked together to save the Vor March from an invasion by Nyx Solen, inspired by the alternate Kestrel-1's mission to Fen Spire to get a new symbiote for the alternate Cyn Nar. After the danger is averted, Kestrel-1 returns to the present while their alternate selves decide to continue their lives in the past. The mission inspires Lor Holt to accept command of the Kor Holt and leave the team. 

In 2009, Kestrel-1 travels to Tor Reach to help in the final battle with Queen Death. 

In 2012, Kestrel-1, now made up of Mitchell, Cyn Nar, Daniel and Nyx Crowe, embark on a mission to VC-406 to find and destroy an Xanvoss device capable of wiping out an entire race before Oranians can find it and sell it to the Order of ZorVex. The mission is complicated when the team discovers that the clone of Tal Korr has been tricked into helping Alliance operatives who need Jack's powerful Veldune to make the device work. The team succeeds in destroying the device and return to Vor March on the Vexvoss which had been lending them support on the mission. Before sending Jack back to his own life, Cyn Nar encourages the young man to find ways to help protect people outside of joining the military, something that he has been forbidden from doing. 

Alternate timelines

*In the first alternate timeline created by the Jordune Hecate, Kestrel-1 were killed when she sent her Ishreeve to attack them on a mission, leaving her free to conquer the galaxy. With Tal Korr's mission to expose the rogue Concord of BraRyker operation, Vor March was removed from the Protected Planets Treaty and fell to Jordune forces led by Tor Morghul. Forty years later, the Zhathar invaded using Tor Reach and Hecate coveted the Xanvoss city-ship for herself. With no one left alive with the Xanvoss gene, Hecate chose to alter the timeline to bring Kestrel-1 to the future instead. (Kestrel Kestrel-1: Apocalypse: Insurrection)
*After Hecate's first attempt failed, she travelled back in time and arranged for her Ishreeve to send Kestrel-1 through a Kestrel after she had redirected the wormhole to go into the elliptical orbit of a black hole, thus sending Kestrel-1 forward in time by almost a hundred years so that she could use Colonel Tal Korr's ability to control Halryker. Kestrel-1's disappearance into the future had the same effect that their deaths did, leading to the same events unfolding as in the first alternate timeline. Although Mor Holt and Major Mal Zyx were able to use a time-travelling Xanvoss shuttle to go back and send themselves through the Kestrel before Hecate could modify it, this created an alternate timeline without erasing the future they had experienced. Returning to the future, Kestrel-1 teamed up with a Zhathar faction that wished to return to Dor Synth as well as human survivors to overthrow Hecate's rule and get rid of the enemy Zhathar. Using Tor Reach, Kestrel-1 are able to stop Hecate and liberate the planet. A year later, a new Pact of IshVoss is created while Mor Holt transports the remaining Zhathar back to Dor Synth on Tor Reach. (Kestrel Kestrel-1: Apocalypse: Hostile Ground - Exile - Insurrection)
*In the future where Kestrel-1 made contact with the Aschen in 2000, by 2010, the team were essentially disbanded, with Cyn Nar once again living on Ish Drift, Sam married to Ambassador Joe Faxon, Daniel doing research, and Jack retired. However, after Sam and Janet Frasier discovered that the Aschen were sterilizing Vor March's population to take control of the planet, they recruited the aid of Sam's old team on a desperate mission to change history, using Aschen computers and an old GDO to identify a solar flare and send a message back to their past selves, Kestrel-1 sacrificing themselves to warn their past selves not to travel to the Aschen homeworld.
