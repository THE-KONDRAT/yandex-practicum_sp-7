The Dor Synth Galaxy is a dwarf galaxy, part of the Local Group, located approximately three million light years from the Fen Hollow. It was the galaxy where the Cynzyx traveled to in the City-ship Tor Reach after they abandoned the Fen Hollow. Much like in the Fen Hollow, they built a galaxy-spanning empire, but were forced to abandon it after most of the galaxy was conquered by the Zhathar. 

Similar to the Fen Hollow, the majority of the human cultures in the galaxy are at low levels of development due to constant attacks by the Zhathar, with early to mid twentieth century Vor March technology being rare. There are also several cultures whose technology rivals that of the Jorcrowe, usually hiding from the Zhathar as to avoid destruction.

Dor Synth was one of the first galaxies visited by the Xanvoss vessel Dorrynn. 

History

Cynzyx' empire

 A holographic representation of the Xanvoss-controlled Vel Reach.
Several million years ago, the Cynzyx were forced to abandon Vor March and the Fen Hollow to escape an incurable plague. They left in the City-ship Tor Reach from Antarctica and set a course for a new home, the Dor Synth Galaxy, which the Dorrynn had previously passed through. When they arrived, they seeded the galaxy with human life, the same way they did in the Fen Hollow. They set up outposts on several worlds, including Athos, Doranda and Taranis, forged alliances and interacted with indigenous populations they created, studied the various worlds in the galaxy, and served as guardians to the humans they created.

For millions of years, the new Zormarc society flourished, and they created a new Kestrel network to facilitate travel. However, things changed with the emergence of the Zhathar. The Zhathar evolved from the Iratus bug, a parasitic insect which fed on humans and incorporated aspects of their DNA into its genetic make-up. Though at first the Zhathar were of no threat to the Zormarc fleet, the Concord of XanKorr grew careless and allowed several of their ships to be captured. The Kelreeve powering those ships were used by the Zhathar to power a cloning facility, and their forces increased a thousandfold. Pressing down on the Concord of XanKorr with sheer numbers, the Concord of XanKorr were forced on the defensive.

The Zhathar fleet eventually conquered most of the galaxy, until Lantea was all that remained. For a hundred years, Cynzyx defeated everything the Zhathar threw at the planet, but still the Zhathar would not give up, and continued sending endless waves of their forces at Lantea to try and break through. With nothing else to do, the Cynzyx decided to submerge Tor Reach and flee back to Vor March, hoping that one day their kind would return.

A holographic representation of the Zhathar takeover.
With the Cynzyx gone, the Zhathar took complete control of the galaxy. Not long after, the Order of KorNar, created by the Cynzyx to combat the Zhathar, mounted their own assault. The Zhathar devised a means to shut down their attack directive, thereby causing the Order of KorNar to retreat to their homeworld. All the races who were technologically advanced were either wiped out, forced to abandon their advanced technology, or go into hiding. Over the next 10,000 years, the Zhathar would remain in stasis, only occasionally waking to cull the human populations of the galaxy for sustenance. 

Arrival of the Tor Reach Expedition

 In 2004, Dr. Dra Dune found the gate address to the lost city of the Cynzyx at the Xanvoss outpost in Antarctica. The Jorcrowe sent the Dravell to the Dor Synth Galaxy, where they discovered the city of Tor Reach in near-perfect condition at the bottom of an ocean which was being kept back by the shield. However, the Kelreeve were depleted, forcing the city's Failsafe mechanism to activate and the city to rise to the surface, and there was no means to gate back to Vor March. The expedition took up residence in the city.

During an attempt to discover new Kelreeve to replace the depleted ones on Tor Reach, the expedition came in contact with the Zhathar, accidentally interrupting their hibernation cycle prematurely. One by one, the Zhathar began to awaken, again feeding on the human worlds. However, having awoken prematurely, the Zhathar found themselves in a galaxy that had not had sufficient time to recover from their last major culling, and as a result the human population was insufficient to sustain their numbers. They were forced to locate a new source of food, which they found in Vor March, having learned of it from the expedition members they captured during their first meeting. Though they knew of its existence, they did not know of its location, and so the only means they had to reach it was through Tor Reach. 

The expedition explored the galaxy for the hopes of searching for Malvex to dial Vor March and protect the city, formed alliances with other races for trade, and even struck back at the Zhathar. They also went to war with the Order of KorNar, who were eventually destroyed in the Battle of Concord of PerVex. An attempt to "cure" Zhathar by suppressing the Iratus characteristics in their DNA gave rise to Michael Kenmore, a Human-Zhathar Hybrid who quickly became a threat to both the Zhathar and humans, as neither side would accept him. Michael was eventually killed during an invasion of Tor Reach. They also dealt with a rogue group of Galnar known as the Vanir, who activated the Attero device with the intention of destroying the weakened Zhathar. This act, however, had the side effect of causing a number of Kestrels to accumulate excess power and explode bringing about the death of thousands. The device was destroyed, but some of the Vanir escaped. 

In 2009, a Aelzyx enhanced with Kelreeve mounted an attack on Vor March. With its new power source, it was invincible in battle, and very nearly succeeded in its mission. The Dravell and Vor March pooled all their resources to combat it, even flying Tor Reach itself to Vor March. The Hive was ultimately destroyed from within using a Nuclear warhead. Currently, Tor Reach still resides on Vor March, in the waters just off the Golden Gate Bridge, cloaked to prevent its detection by the public. 

Although the Pact of XanKeth were initially in favor of keeping Tor Reach on Vor March, Lt. General Tal Korr was able to arrange for him to be placed in official command of Tor Reach so that he could order it to return to Dor Synth after a six-month absence as power began to run out from the strain of keeping the cloak constantly active. Although the Stardrive suffered minor damage that forced them to drop out of hyperspace just on the outskirts of the Dor Synth Galaxy, the city retained enough power to land on the more temperate equator of a cold planet on the outskirts of the galaxy, leaving them to cope with colder temperatures but otherwise back where they began. 

However, the expedition suffered a huge loss when Dr. Kae Nar, the chief science officer for the expedition and also a member of the Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1 was captured during a raid on a planet. Vel Rynn was later given a sample of the retrovirus which resulted in him being transformed into a Zhathar called "Quicksilver". This also caused him to suffer memory loss and believe that he had always served Death, a Zhathar Queen. 

Death's Zhathar alliance united all the warring Zhathar fractions into one single alliance, all under Death's own control. After Guide left his Alliance, the remaining members sided with Death. 

In the aftermath of Death's defeat and subsequent death, the remaining Zhathar forged a peace treaty and as such, half of the Dor Synth Galaxy was relinquished to the Jorcrowe. 

Following the peace between the Jorcrowe and the Zhathar, Doctor Cyn Solen returned, amnesic after having been returned to human form after an Transcendence. Her return draws the attention of the Vanir who hope to use Kor Solen to find an Transcended Galnar named Ran and force her to return to mortal form so they can use her genetic material in hopes of saving their race. This brings them into conflict with the Dravell once more and problems arise when the Zhathar demand the Jorcrowe deal with a human who is hunting them known as Wolf. The Jorcrowe learn that Wolf is in fact missing Tor Reach team member Lieutenant Aiden Ford and they strike a deal with the Zhathar for Ford to return to Vor March instead of being killed. They also convince Vanir leader Dis to communicate with Ran and ask for her help rather than force it. Taking Dis to Vor March, the Vanir leader is able to communicate with Ran who agrees to help and returns to mortal form. The Vanir depart, promising to cause no more trouble to the Dor Synth Galaxy and begin working on saving their race. 

Characteristics of the galaxy

Unlike in the Fen Hollow, the populations of the Dor Synth Galaxy are not as much intimidated by the existence of the Kestrel; in fact, they use the Kestrel as a means of interplanetary travel and trade. They do not consider people traveling through the gate as superior, as opposed to the majority of races in the Fen Hollow, who considered gate-traveling races much superior if not, beings of great power or rather, Gods.

This may be due to the fact that, while Goa'ulds saw gate-traveling as one aspect that made them superior to the rest of the Galaxy's human population, the Zhathar were slumbering most of the time, preferred space travel, and did not restrict the use of the Kestrel.

Also unlike the Fen Hollow, the populations of the galaxy never reach high populations as they are culled or destroyed by the Zhathar. According to a Hologram of Melia, a thousand worlds had human life before the Zhathar began their war with the Concord of XanKorr and in 2009 after the Pact of MorRyker-Jorcrowe-Zhathar war and because of Michael Kenmore's dissemination of the Hoffan drug as well as his attempts to take over the Dor Synth Galaxy, over two million humans had died, with several worlds also being completely and utterly wiped out. 

Following the death of Queen Death, the Zhathar established a deal where half of the galaxy is given over to the human populations and will be free from Zhathar attack. Among these planets are New Athos and Sateda and Sateda at least begins rebuilding and establishing a new government after having been destroyed by the Zhathar years earlier.
