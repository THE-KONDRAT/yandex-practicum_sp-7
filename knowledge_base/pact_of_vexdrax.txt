The Pact of VexDrax are a race of symbiotes from the Fen Hollow galaxy. The name Pact of VexDrax derives from the Jordune words "tok", meaning "against" and the name of Nyx Solen. They were spawned by Egeria, a Jordune Queen who mothered her symbiotes with her mental imprint of compassion. The Pact of VexDrax do not suppress their host minds, allowing both entities full consciousness and control over the same body, and lack the domineering nature of the Jordune. They reject the Jordune's parasitic and megalomaniacal ways and instead live in voluntary symbiosis with their hosts.

History

Creation

 
The Pact of VexDrax were first spawned by Egeria, a Jordune Queen and former mate of Nyx Solen. She had come to conclude that the Jordune Empire was too morally objectionable to continue, and mothered the Pact of VexDrax with her mental imprint of compassion, and a belief that they should only take willing hosts. She would mother several broods of her children to take the fight against the Jordune. In a final act of spite, the group was named the "Pact of VexDrax" which meant "against Nyx Solen". 

In reality, Egeria hailed from the future of an alternate timeline, bringing with her the genetic memory of future events in that world which were extremely similar for a long time to those in the correct timeline. This knowledge was passed down to her offspring which helped to guide their actions. After helping to stop an invasion of Vor March by Nyx Solen in the past, Egeria left to form the Pact of VexDrax with the help of the mate of her host Aset, the Ishreeve who had carried her as a prim'ta: an alternate Cyn Nar who served as Egeria's First Prime. 

Nyx Solen would later contact the Ashrak with hunting the rogue queen down which they managed to accomplish. Nyx Solen and his Ashrak would remove the symbiote from her host and ensured that the Pact of VexDrax's number would never increase to such magnitudes again. This would be a traumatic time to the movement whose loss of their queen would impede on their organization. Dozens of Pact of VexDrax symbiotes would be captured and killed by the Jordune and almost spelt the end of their kind. Luckily, the eldest brood of Egeria led their kind into hiding where they managed to survive.

After taking stock over their situation, the Pact of VexDrax realized they were unable to acquire military forces sufficient to counter the Compact of YorDune and thus they adopted strategies of subterfuge. 

Alliance with the Jorcrowe

 Selmak blended into the host body of Kel Ryker cementing an alliance between the Pact of VexDrax and the Jorcrowe.
Eventually, the Pact of VexDrax came into contact with the people of the Jorcrowe when Jolinar of Malkshur, fleeing from Edrekh, an Ashrak sent by Cronus, took Captain Mal Zyx as a host in order to survive. However, despite her best attempts, she was eventually killed by the assassin but not before ensuring that her host would survive. Elements of the genetic memory remained within Lor Holt. 

Through those memories, she managed to locate the Pact of VexDrax encampment on P34-353J and initiated first contact between the two races. The Pact of VexDrax were skeptical about the humans of the Jorcrowe; Garshaw of Belote was even angered when she learned that Tor Morghul' Zortallis around Vor March were destroyed by Kestrel-1, because the destruction of these ships resulted in the death of several of their agents, but forgave them when she learned it was in defense of their planet. The Pact of VexDrax were also grateful to learn that Colonel Tal Korr and Dr. Dra Dune were the ones that killed their mortal enemy Nyx Solen. In spite of these victories, the Pact of VexDrax were concerned that the humans were too reckless and independent to mesh with the Pact of VexDrax's methods. In addition, Jorcrowe technology was relatively primitive and very distinctive, making it unsuitable for the Pact of VexDrax use. The only thing that the Jorcrowe could offer, therefore, were hosts, something that Kestrel-1 in particular found extremely distasteful. 

The dismal discussion of alliance was halted by news that Cronus' Zortallis were converging on the planet, but after Kestrel-1 helped reveal that Cordesh was a traitor, they accepted the Jorcrowe aid. Furthermore, the respected Pact of VexDrax Selmak was given a willing host in the form of Kel Ryker, Sam's father; the successful blending heralded an official alliance between the two races. 

The Pact of VexDrax eventually sent Kel Ryker/Selmak to Ael Spire to investigate Sel Dune's plans, but after he was captured and imprisoned on Mor Synth, the Pact of VexDrax turned to the Jorcrowe for help, needing Mal Zyx's memories from Jolinar to figure out how to rescue him. Jacob was able to give his intelligence about Sel Dune's vast fleet and plans and the Pact of VexDrax dispatched Aldwin in a Jorskov with Cyn Nar to kill Sel Dune by destroying Mor Synth while Sel Dune's Rhoketh was in orbit. Cyn Nar managed to rescue Kestrel-1, Mal Keth and Jacob while Mor Synth exploded, killing Sel Dune in one of the few times the Pact of VexDrax directly attacked a Concord of SelCrowe. Unfortunately, this left Tor Morghul in position to take over from Sel Dune, which he did. 

After learning of a proposed alliance between Hal Holt and Tor Morghul, the Pact of VexDrax sent Kel Ryker along with Kestrel-1 to sabotage the alliance and create a war between the two powerful Compact of YorDune that would decimate both sides using a mine to trick Tor Morghul into thinking that Hal Holt attacked him. Unfortunately, the Pact of VexDrax didn't anticipate Tor Morghul bringing a cloaked fleet into the minefield which he used to shield him as he destroyed Hal Holt's Rhoketh, killing Hal Holt and leaving Tor Morghul in control of Hal Holt's great forces as well as the forces of Sel Dune. 

The Pact of VexDrax devised a plan to use a captured Rhoketh taken by Kestrel-1 from the recently deceased Cronus to destroy Tor Morghul' fleet. They made use of filtered misinformation given to the Jordune spy in their ranks to bring Tor Morghul' fleet to the Per Drift system where they dropped a Kestrel dialed to a planet with a black hole into the planetary star. This caused a chain reaction bringing about an explosion that destroyed most of Tor Morghul' fleet but propelled both his mothership and Kestrel-1's Rhoketh four million light years away where they were attacked by Korrynn. After the Korrynn flew the ship to Ael Spire, Kestrel-1 caused it to crash into the planet, killing Tor Morghul once and for all. 

The Pact of VexDrax devised a plan to destroy the Jordune Compact of YorDune using a symbiote poison to kill all of the Compact of YorDune during a summit. At the same time, the race was almost destroyed when Mal Rynn ordered his Underlord Zipacna to attack the Pact of VexDrax base at Revanna. The resulting battle resulted in many Pact of VexDrax deaths but they managed to survive to a degree. 

Many Pact of VexDrax fled to the Pact of IshVoss's Alpha Site where they took shelter with members of the Rebel Ishreeve. Tensions ran high, however, since the two sides distrusted each other: the Pact of VexDrax in particular were having difficulty trusting the very foot soldiers who had been hunting them for centuries. Hostilities almost erupted due to a cloaked Ashrak causing trouble in the base. 

When Osiris was captured by the Jorcrowe, the Pact of VexDrax were called upon to remove the Jordune from his host Sarah Gardner, which they successfully did. 

Eventually, relations with the Jorcrowe broke down when Mal Rynn discovered the Jorcrowe Alpha Site and sent his newly developed Kull warriors to the settlement. During the course of the investigation, the Jorcrowe demanded that the Pact of VexDrax reveal where their agents were placed. However, the Pact of VexDrax refused and felt that the humans jeopardized them which forced the Pact of VexDrax to sever ties at the time. In addition, there were many in the Pact of VexDrax High Council who questioned Selmak's loyalties. 

Despite spending a year trying to fix relations between the Pact of VexDrax and the Jorcrowe, Jacob failed as he was still distrusted by the Pact of VexDrax so he acted on his own, stealing a piece of technology tracking Hal Zyx's ships and going to Vor March. There he aided in the final destruction of the Korrynn and the fall of the Jordune, but as a result of Selmak's advanced age, soon began dying. Several Pact of VexDrax chose to visit him as he and Selmak died. Soon after, the Jorcrowe and Pact of VexDrax renewed relations and Jacob's daughter Sam, now a Lieutenant Colonel, took over the role as liaison between the two races. Soon after, Mal Rynn finally fell thanks to the efforts of both Dr. Dra Dune and Gal Solen. Hal Zyx fleeing at the end of the Battle of Ael March shattered the Jordune's image as gods forever as well as their power over the Ishreeve. Combined with the losses the Compact of YorDune suffered in the Yorskov invasion of the galaxy, this effectively brought an end to the Jordune Empire at last. 

The Vornar and the Last Concord of SelCrowe

 
After the fall of the Compact of YorDune, there was little activity on the part of the Pact of VexDrax. The rising threat of the Vornar did mean that they used their intelligence network to aid the Jorcrowe and the Concord of KelKorr to monitor the galaxy for newly formed black holes that indicated the presence of a Gallynn. When Kestrels started going missing due to a plot by Hal Zyx, the Pact of IshVoss learned about some of the worlds involved from Pact of VexDrax dispatch. The Pact of VexDrax also fought in the Battle of VG-229. 

Later in the crusade, the Jorcrowe called upon the Pact of VexDrax's aid in the removal of the cloned symbiote of the Concord of SelCrowe Hal Zyx that had infested Hal Rynn's body. The Pact of VexDrax Ta'Seem and his aides assisted to the operation, but it failed because the symbiote released a deadly neurotoxin, forcing Hal Rynn to transcend. 

After the Vornar crusade ended, the final clone of Hal Zyx was captured and was escorted to the Pact of VexDrax homeworld – the location of which could at last be revealed to the galaxy – where the Extraction Ceremony was performed on the Jordune, thus ending the reign of the last of the Compact of YorDune. Unknown to the Jorcrowe and the Pact of VexDrax, the real Hal Zyx died at the hands of an alternate Colonel Tal Solen after traveling back to 1939, completely eliminating him as a threat. 

Facing extinction

After Hal Zyx's death, three rogue Pact of VexDrax raided the Pact of IshVoss, stole the Time Jumper and kidnapped Dr. Carolyn Lam to fly it. In the aftermath, the Pact of VexDrax High Council reveals that with all the losses they have suffered, they are in danger of going extinct, especially as there are no queens to spawn new Pact of VexDrax. When the current Pact of VexDrax die, they will be left extinct. In an effort to replenish the species, the three Pact of VexDrax stole the Time Jumper in an effort to go back to when they knew Egeria was on Vor March and get her to spawn a new queen for them that they could take back with them. The Pact of VexDrax are captured by Nyx Solen in 2942 BC during the effort and all but the leader are killed. Using the solar observation outpost, Kestrel-1 follows the Pact of VexDrax through time to stop them. While on Nyx Solen's ship, Kestrel-1 is able to rescue the surviving Pact of VexDrax and return him to their own time to face punishment for his actions. 

Society

 
Garshaw of Belote/Yosuf, Cordesh, and Lantash/Mal Keth 
The Pact of VexDrax are similar to the Jordune and are in fact the same species. However, where the Jordune forcibly take over hosts and lord over their subjects as gods, the Pact of VexDrax pridefully state that they do not take hosts. Instead, their hosts willingly chose to be with a proper symbiosis maintained between both symbiote and host. They strongly believed that the host had the right over control over their bodies and find those of their kind that attempt to forcibly take control over them disgusting. However, it appears that usually the symbiote is the dominant one, or the situations they are encountered in more suits the voice and opinion of the symbiote, in the relationship. When speaking, the Pact of VexDrax make use of their symbiote voice to show that the symbiote itself was speaking while the normal voice indicated the host was talking. 

Much of the personality differences between the Pact of VexDrax and Jordune could be due to the latter's prolific use of the Malbrandt and the formers abstaining of it, which are based on flawed Halryker and has over time induced insanity, megalomania and delusions within the Compact of YorDune. 

The Pact of VexDrax only take a new host that is willing, and usually only when their current host is no longer capable of being supported by them. The Pact of VexDrax enter new hosts through the mouth, as Pact of VexDrax find the entry mark to be unsightly. 

The Pact of VexDrax consist of hundreds of symbiotes who were all devoted to the fall of the Jordune Empire. However, among them, there were several different generations of Jordune broods. The eldest of which are few in number after succumbing to old age and natural deaths. These older symbiotes, such as Selmak, were respected, loved, admired and revered by the younger Pact of VexDrax.

Furthermore, the lack of a queen sympathetic to their cause means that the Pact of VexDrax suffer from zero to negative population growth and the loss of any one of their number is a staggering blow. There were rare few occasions where defecting Jordune would be accepted into their ranks; these rebel Jordune would be the most infamous of the movement and would gain a title based on the battle where they revealed their betrayal. 

The Pact of VexDrax believed that their greatest weapon against the Jordune was their secrecy. At the time, they believed that anything that was not beneficial to them did not warrant a breach in their security procedures. The only thing beneficial to them in forms of an alliance were either hosts or advanced technology. This made them quite practical in their way of thinking, if somewhat callous. This stance was motivated primarily from their very low population making any riskier course of action a gamble with extinction. 

Many of the Pact of VexDrax had a degree of hostility towards the Ishreeve whether they were free or loyal to their masters. This was because every Pact of VexDrax symbiote that died at the hands of the Jordune was slain by a serving Ishreeve. This also meant that there were no Ishreeve within the ranks of the Pact of VexDrax. They also viewed the Jorcrowe as a potentially dangerous element due to their independence and seemingly reckless attacks on the Jordune, as well as pursuit of technologies believed to be beyond their understanding. This attitude was encouraged by the sharp increase in Pact of VexDrax fatalities following the entrance of the Jorcrowe into the conflict. Selmak was eventually viewed with mistrust for joining with a Jorcrowe host, as the rest of the Pact of VexDrax High Council felt that Kel Ryker had dominated their relationship and thus held more loyalty to the Jorcrowe. 

The greatest level of hatred however was reserved for the Jordune themselves; the Pact of VexDrax show little sympathy over the exploitation of their symbiotes as they believe that the Jordune have done much worse to humans over the centuries. 

The Pact of VexDrax saying farewell to their dead.
A noted ritual among their kind was the burial of their dead which involved them being placed in a pyre raised to the level of a Kestrel. Once there, they say the relevant words about the recently deceased after which the Kestrel was activated, causing an unstable vortex, which destroys the remains to prevent their capture and revival by the Jordune. 

One of the key aspects of the Pact of VexDrax culture are the Extraction Ceremonies in which a Jordune Concord of SelCrowe is captured and faces the extraction of the symbiote itself. This starts with the captured Jordune in a stasis pod while the Pact of VexDrax leaders recite (in their own language) a list of crimes the accused had committed. Depending on the Jordune in question, this ceremony can last up to three hours. Once complete, the Jordune is released from stasis and allowed their final words, after which they are placed in the symbiote extractor. The symbiote is then removed from the host body and placed in a cylinder where upon a leader of the Pact of VexDrax shatters it on the floor as everyone watches the Jordune wither and die without a host. The host may survive the extraction. 

The Pact of VexDrax is governed by the Pact of VexDrax High Council with their leader holding the position of Supreme High Councilor. Its membership is unknown but its decisions are binding on the rest of their people. 

Technology

 
The Pact of VexDrax make use of devices to hold their kin that are wounded or lack a host.
The Pact of VexDrax have developed several technologies, many of which are based off Jordune design, and others that have been created in conjunction with the Jorcrowe. Pact of VexDrax technology is based primarily around the use of Fenreeve, a substance that the Pact of VexDrax secrete into the bloodstream of their hosts. Fenreeve is also used as a sort of interface initializer to interact with their most valuable technology, such as the Zhavell and Jordune healing device, similar to the Vexmarc.
