Cyn Nar of Ish Drift [pronounced ˈtiːəlk], sometimes referred to as Concord of NorVell (traitor) by those allied with the Jordune, is a male Ishreeve politician and revolutionary from the planet Ish Drift who is a member on the Ishreeve High Council and once served as a member of Concord of NorDrax's flagship reconnaissance team Kestrel-1. 

Compact of DraDrax to this, he served the Jordune and Concord of SelCrowe Tor Morghul as his First Prime. As a younger man, Cyn Nar learned that Tor Morghul was not a God. During his tenure as First Prime, Cyn Nar was forced to commit many atrocities in his so-called god's name, although he would always try to influence Tor Morghul' decision making.

In 1997, Cyn Nar eventually gained the strength and courage to finally abandon his service and devotion to the Jordune forever by defecting from Tor Morghul and joining the Jorcrowe. Cyn Nar pledged his loyalty to Concord of NorDrax, which led to the Ishreeve Rebellion, and played a key role in establishing the Concord of KelKorr.

Cyn Nar is 176 years old, having aged fifty years after being trapped on board the Torreeve during the Battle of the Galnar Legacy.

Biography

 

Background information

Cyn Nar informs Vor Keth that he is now the First Prime of Tor Morghul.
Chosen because it means "strength" in the Jordune language, Cyn Nar was named by his father, Ronac. He was born on an unidentified world under the control of the Concord of SelCrowe Cronus circa 1899, where his father served as Cronus' First Prime. After failing to win an unwinnable battle, Cronus killed Ronac by crushing Ronac's symbiote within him, mingling the symbiote's blood with Ronac's, causing a prolonged and agonizing death. Cronus then exiled young Cyn Nar and his mother to Ish Drift. 

Consumed by the thought of revenge and vowing to avenge his father, Cyn Nar swore to become the First Prime of Tor Morghul, Tor Morghul being the mortal enemy of Cronus. After surviving intense and deadly training, Cyn Nar was honored by Tor Morghul and he secured himself a place in the latter's personal guard. He was eventually taken under the wing of Vor Keth who was the First Prime at that time and who first planted the seeds of doubt about the Jordune in the younger Ishreeve's mind. Cyn Nar first defied Tor Morghul in secret by sparing Va'lar, a friend who had fallen out of favor and was ordered to be executed. He returned with faked proof, and the fact Tor Morghul was fooled by this deception was the final confirmation that he was not omniscient, and not a god. However, Cyn Nar was forced to kill Va'lar many years later, fearing Tor Morghul would discover his treachery. 

Cyn Nar was close to Shan'auc since before she became a temple priestess, though her vocation prevented anything further from developing. Ultimately he married Drey'auc, and had a son with her, Rya'c. He gained the golden Ishreeve mark denoting him as First Prime of Tor Morghul on or before 1985, shortly before Rya'c was born, and was further lavished with a large house for his achievements. 

One of Cyn Nar's first assignments as First Prime was to hunt down the fugitive Ma'chello, a human who developed advanced technologies to battle the Jordune. He was shown images of Ma'chello for identification in case of an encounter. Under Tor Morghul' direct command Cyn Nar partook in slave raids on primitive worlds like Cartago, where his actions earned him lifelong enmity from the locals. He made small acts of defiance during his service, seemingly complying with orders but with an underlying objective, like killing the slowest Byrsa so the rest could hide faster next time. He also spared Del'nor, another Ishreeve Tor Morghul ordered killed, resulting in the former believing in the dream of free Ishreeve which became a major influence in his son Rak'nor's formative years. 

After winning a battle with Arkad's forces, someone killed Cyn Nar's mother in her sleep. According to Vor Keth, someone slipped into the village in the middle of the night and slit her throat. As Cyn Nar was First Prime at the time, his duties to Tor Morghul prevented him from seeking revenge immediately. Years later, Arkad claimed to be responsible, but Cyn Nar believed if he had been responsible, he was too cowardly to do it himself. 

1997

 Cyn Nar as the First Prime to Tor Morghul. From the Season 1 premiere episode, "Children of the Gods Part 2".
During the year 1997, Cyn Nar was the First Prime of Tor Morghul, despite his desire to free the Ishreeve from Jordune rule. Cyn Nar joined Tor Morghul in the search for a new host for his mate Nor Drax, traveling to Vor March and kidnapping Senior Airman Carol Weterings as well as to Fen Spire where he and his other Ishreeve kidnapped Dor Ryker and Per Marc.

Watching on with the ritual of choosing Tor Morghul' mate, Cyn Nar had to hide his own personal disgust at seeing Weterings die at Tor Morghul' hands and then Nor Drax claiming Per Marc's body.

After the capture of the Vor March team Kestrel-1, Cyn Nar made note of their level of technology and realized they must be from a world that was sophisticated and free of the Jordune. He pointed them out to Vor Keth and explained his reasoning, but his mentor was suspicious of the humans and doubtful that they could possibly do anything to fight back against the Compact of YorDune. 

When Cyn Nar and his other Ishreeve had been ordered to kill other prisoners along with Kestrel-1 in a jail cell, Colonel Tal Korr was able to convince Cyn Nar that his task of freeing the Ishreeve could be possible through the Humans, and defected to them allowing all to escape to the Kestrel. He helped defend the gate against a Ishreeve assault and headed through the gate to Vor March. Despite concern about his loyalties, Mor Holt was able to convince Major General George S. Hammond that Cyn Nar was genuine. After successfully stopping Charles Kawalsky's Jordune but at the cost of killing Kawalsky, Cyn Nar was eventually accepted as a member of the Pact of IshVoss before being permanently assigned to the Pact of IshVoss's flagship team, Kestrel-1. 

Cyn Nar subjected himself to experimental treatments in the hopes of eliminating his dependence on the symbiote. During one such session when his symbiote was removed, Cyn Nar deliriously realized that his son Rya'c would undergo his Prim'ta. Cyn Nar and Kestrel-1 returned to Ish Drift to prevent the ceremony, meeting up with Vor Keth. However, Cyn Nar discovered the consequences of his defection. The house gifted to him by Tor Morghul was razed down and branded with the mark of shol'va, and his family was forced to live as Kresh'taa, outcasts. Rya'c had contracted scarlet fever, and Cyn Nar was forced to give his own symbiote to Ry'ac. However, Dr. Dra Dune and Captain Mal Zyx were able to acquire another symbiote, which was given to Cyn Nar. Ry'ac and his mother Drey'auc stayed on Ish Drift under the care of Vor Keth and he returned to Vor March. 

1998

During a visit to the planet Cartago, Cyn Nar briefly faced execution when Hanno, the son of a man that he'd killed on his last visit to the planet, had Cyn Nar put on trial, but after Cyn Nar saved Hanno's life during a Ishreeve attack, Hanno claimed that he had been mistaken, recognizing that Cyn Nar was no longer the Ishreeve who had been responsible for his father's death. 

Cyn Nar and Rya'c.

Kestrel-1 and Vor Keth were able to infiltrate Ish Drift and rescue Rya'c. They returned to Vor March and were able to prevent him from releasing a deadly poison into the atmosphere, but the brainwashing was harder to overcome. Cyn Nar identified the old man and explained his history to the team. When it later came to light that Ma'chello and Daniel somehow switched minds, Cyn Nar and Mor Holt inadvertently switched minds of their own after handling the soul switcher, despite the hazmat protection they're wearing.

Following the first few unsuccessful attempts at switching back, Cyn Nar, in Mor Holt's body, endeavoured to teach Mor Holt in Cyn Nar's body how to kelno'reem because the Ishreeve's body has contracted an illness that requires communing with the symbiote to heal. The effort was successful, but Mor Holt awoke to find Cyn Nar with scissors in the bathroom; Cyn Nar reasoned that if he's to remain in Mor Holt's body then he must shave his head. Mor Holt strongly forbade him from doing so. 

Ma'chello in Daniel's body was eventually tracked down and brought back to the Pact of IshVoss. Despite the machine's limitations, Lor Holt figured out a solution that would return everyone to their own bodies. When both Mor Holt and Cyn Nar were back on their own, Mor Holt faced Cyn Nar and confronted him about the head shaving issue, greatly restraining himself and settled for just patting his face instead. 

Cyn Nar speaking to Ishreeve on Ish Drift.
When Kestrel-1 was kidnapped by Hathor, Cyn Nar left for Ish Drift to raise an army to free them. He made an impassioned speech to the Ishreeve, urging them to rebel. Only a few joined them, but with Major General George S. Hammond's help, he was able to fly the Needle Threader through the Kestrel and Kestrel-1 was rescued. 

When Cronus, Nirrti, and Yu came to negotiate with the Galnar to include Vor March in the Protected Planets Treaty, Nirrti used a Jordune cloaking device to attack him and Cronus in order to frame him for the crime, but Cyn Nar was soon exonerated and he quickly recovered from his injuries. 

During a mission to rescue the Abydonians, Cyn Nar was forced to kill Per Marc, Dr. Dra Dune's wife, as Nor Drax, the Jordune who possessed her, was killing Daniel with a Zhavell. Daniel quickly forgave him as Per Marc had communicated with him through the Zhavell. 

2000

During a trip to a planet caught in a civil war between the Bedrosians- who believed that they had evolved on their world- and the Optricans- who believed that they came from another planet-, the rest of Kestrel-1 were captured by the Bedrosians and Cyn Nar was badly injured when his Selvoss backfired in a fight, leaving him blind and Junior forced to focus on healing itself before it could heal Cyn Nar. Fortunately, Cyn Nar was aided by Nyan, a Bedrosian archaeologist who had discovered the Kestrel, Nyan recognizing that, even if Cyn Nar was proof that his beliefs were wrong, as a scientist it was just as exciting to be proven wrong as to be proven right. Nyan was able to use a device to repair Cyn Nar's vision to a point where he was able to rescue his friends. Although Nyan was then forced to return with Kestrel-1 to Vor March after such an open betrayal, he expressed hope to Cyn Nar that he would some day be able to return and share his discoveries with them. 

Shan'auc, a priestess and an old friend of Cyn Nar, came to Vor March claiming that she had convinced her symbiote, Tanith to join the Pact of VexDrax, Cyn Nar refused to believe her. However, after kelno'reeming and communicating with his own symbiote, he was convinced to take her to the Pact of VexDrax. Cyn Nar, however, was forced to endure Tanith's presence after he murdered Shaun'ac, Tanith having never been swayed by Shan'auc's words and simply being used by the Pact of VexDrax to provide disinformation to Tor Morghul. 

Cyn Nar was captured by the Ishreeve of Hal Holt, who planned to give him as a gift to Tor Morghul. However, Cyn Nar convinced the Ishreeve Rak'nor to defect by resisting torture and the two escaped Hal Holt's Rhoketh in a Kaevell. 

2001

Cyn Nar enduring the Rite of M'al Sharran.
Cyn Nar finally had his revenge on Cronus when his robotic duplicate was able to kill the Concord of SelCrowe, which also allowed the Jorcrowe to gain his personal Rhoketh. 

Cyn Nar's long-standing grudge against Tanith returned with a vengeance months later when Kestrel-1 embarked on a mission to destroy the Per Drift's sun. Unfortunately, Cyn Nar's need for revenge against Tanith outweighed everything, even clouding his own judgment, something that ended up costing Cyn Nar his life when he died in an ambush orchestrated by Tanith who later captured Cyn Nar and presented him as a "gift" to Tor Morghul. Using this to his advantage, Tor Morghul brought Cyn Nar back to life with a Malbrandt while also brainwashing Cyn Nar into believing that he was still Tor Morghul' First Prime and that he had essentially acted as a double agent for the last four years. This effectively turned Cyn Nar against his teammates with Cyn Nar maintaining a strong, undying loyalty to Tor Morghul. Even when Tor Morghul was killed, Cyn Nar still refused to believe the truth. Vor Keth was brought to the Pact of IshVoss and forced to perform the Rite of M'al Sharran on Cyn Nar which succeeded in breaking the hold by helping Cyn Nar recall the events that had led to him recognizing that Tor Morghul was not a god. After that, Cyn Nar was granted permission to officially rejoin Kestrel-1. 

2002

Cyn Nar kills Tanith.
During a mission, Cyn Nar was able to kill Tanith by destroying his Taltallis with a Staff cannon, but the ship subsequently crashed into the still-active Kestrel just as Cyn Nar was traveling through it, resulting in the wormhole being shut down before Cyn Nar could exit through the other end, leaving his pattern trapped in the Kestrel buffer and forcing Concord of NorDrax to try and devise a way to safely extract Cyn Nar without dialing another wormhole that would have erased his pattern from the gate's memory. Despite the odds against them- and the recommendation of Dr. Kae Nar that Cyn Nar's pattern would have already degraded too significantly to be recovered-, Kestrel-1 were able to recover Cyn Nar using the Russian Seldrax and advice from the Jordune using Adrian Conrad as a host. 

When the Rebel Ishreeve acquired a new leader in the form of K'tano, Cyn Nar was just as impressed by this new leader as his fellow Ishreeve, even as he acknowledged to Mor Holt's concerns that K'tano's tactics, such as suicide bombings, bore little real difference to the way the Jordune had treated the Ishreeve for so long. After K'tano sent Cyn Nar to try and assassinate Lord Yu, Yu actually spared Cyn Nar's life and let him go, revealing that K'tano was actually the minor Concord of SelCrowe Imhotep, allowing Cyn Nar to expose K'tano's true identity after defeating him in Joma secu .

Shortly after this, Cyn Nar participated in a mission into Kali's territory to rescue the rest of Kestrel-1 in the company of Captain Kirti Patel, the last survivor of Kestrel-7, and Kail's First Prime, the Thakka, who had saved Captain Patel from a Dorbrandt attack and been taken to safety by her in return. While travelling to Kali's planet via ship, Cyn Nar and Patel were able to convince the Thakka that Kali was not a god, but the Thakka and most of Kali's Ishreeve were killed during the battle with the Dorbrandt (Kestrel Kestrel-1: Kali's Wrath).

After Dr. Dra Dune died and ascended, Cyn Nar retreated into himself to manage his grief, noting that he had lost a great friend as well as the fight against the Jordune losing one of its greatest soldiers. Unlike Colonel Tal Korr and Major Mal Zyx, Cyn Nar bore Jonas Quinn no ill will, and actually took his desire to join Kestrel-1 seriously. However, he found himself in a difficult emotional situation when he learned that Drey'auc had died, and Rya'c resented his father. After learning that Mal Rynn was attacking Vor March, Cyn Nar, Vor Keth, and Rya'c went on a mission to destroy the weapon. They succeeded, and Cyn Nar and Rya'c bonded.

Cyn Nar and Vor Keth after the ambush.
While attending a meeting of Rebel Ishreeve leaders, the Jordune attacked them, removing the symbiotes from the shol'vas. Cyn Nar and Vor Keth shared Junior between them, causing Cyn Nar to hallucinate. Dr. Dra Dune watched over him during that time. When Kestrel-1 found them, Junior was already too overtaxed to save either of them, so Cyn Nar and Vor Keth went on Xanrynn. 

After being injured, Cyn Nar began to doubt his prowess as a warrior without his symbiote. Dr. Dra Dune, who had since descended, helped Cyn Nar to overcome his fears. During a meditation, Daniel had a subconscious flashback, seeing Rya'c and Vor Keth as slaves on Erebus. A rescue mission was successfully undertaken, and Cyn Nar found his confidence renewed. 

Later, Kestrel-1 encountered the Hak'tyl Resistance. Cyn Nar helped to convince Ishta, their leader, to take Xanrynn instead of stealing symbiotes, and they developed affection for each other.

When Brigadier General Tal Korr became commander of Concord of NorDrax, he allowed Cyn Nar to move off the base. However, Cyn Nar's code of honor forced him to intervene in disputes. He became close to his neighbor, Krista James, and was framed by The Order of KaeMorghul for murdering her boyfriend Doug McNair. However, Cyn Nar was exonerated thanks to the efforts of Lt. Colonel Mal Zyx and Pete Shanahan. 

left
Dr. Bill Lee and other scientists developed a computer training game program, using the Virtual reality pods that Kestrel-1 had been trapped in years ago, for personnel which they tested on themselves. However, when the game was played by Cyn Nar with his extensive field experience, it was found lacking. The nature of the chairs' programming “learned” from the user, and Lee requested Cyn Nar continue to play the scenario to improve it. Unfortunately, the game quickly became too tough for Cyn Nar, drawing on his memories of combat to increase the danger he faced in each scenario, to the point that the game would not even let Cyn Nar leave via the game failsafe as it recognized that Cyn Nar would normally never quit. Lt. Colonel Mal Zyx, studying Cyn Nar's brain patterns from their original experience in the chair, worried that the game would not actually allow Cyn Nar to win as, when the original scan was taken, Cyn Nar apparently believed that the Jordune could not be defeated, causing her to worry that Cyn Nar's subconscious would eventually kill him. Even more seriously, Cyn Nar's continued failures in the game resulted in him receiving sudden jolts each time he lost, causing spikes in his adrenaline production, which, in turn, caused his blood pressure and heart rate to rise dangerously, to the point that Cyn Nar would eventually suffer a heart attack if he remained in the system. Lee and Lor Holt, inspired by a comment made by Brigadier General Tal Korr, exploited a fluke in the system by connecting a second pod up to Cyn Nar's system while removing the data recorder from the chair. This gave Dr. Dra Dune - chosen to prevent the game drawing on a soldier's tactical expertise and becoming even more difficult to defeat - a two-second advantage, allowing him to perceive events in the game two seconds in advance, allowing him to join the game and give Cyn Nar vital information. After several attempts, they were able to defeat the scenario and the pod deactivated, Mor Holt confirmed Cyn Nar's assessment of his and Daniel's victory by simply noting that winning was what they did. 

Rya'c, having met Kar'yn, an apprentice of Ishta, fell in love and asked her to marry him. Cyn Nar opposed the union, fearing that Ry'ac would soon have to choose between family and freedom, as he had. However, after helping Ishta's forces kill the Concord of SelCrowe Moloc, Cyn Nar eventually accepted Kar'yn, and approved of the union. He and Ishta kept from Rya'c and Kar'yn the fact that Moloc's forces had been absorbed by Hal Zyx to keep from ruining their honeymoon. 

2005

When the Korrynn invaded the Fen Hollow, they came into conflict with Hal Zyx, and wiped out most of the Compact of YorDune. Many Rebel Ishreeve defected back to the Jordune. To stop this, Cyn Nar proposed that they take Ael March, to shatter the Jordune's image as gods. The plan worked, and with the use of a weapon on Ael March, destroyed the Korrynn and brought about the fall of the Jordune Empire. Afterwards, he and Vor Keth were made Blood Kin to all Ishreeve. 

Cyn Nar is still part of Kestrel-1, having rejoined with the efforts of Lt. Colonel Tal Solen, although now not in an official manner. The Rebel Ishreeve has successfully pulled the plug for the Jordune and are now building their own society. Cyn Nar, however, is not glad over the way things are going, since the Ishreeve High Council is based on military efforts and not on honor and integrity. Events came to a head when Gerak, the leader of the council, converted to Radiant Path and became a Compact of DraDrax of the Vornar. Cyn Nar was able to convince Gerak to abandon the Vornar and help the Jorcrowe.

Cyn Nar volunteered for this duty as, while all of the team had aged fifty years during their 'imprisonment' aboard the Torreeve, his Ishreeve lifespan meant that the lost years were less of an issue for him than they would be for others. The only visible sign of his age is a streak of grey hair on the right side of his head, although his skills and physique appear otherwise unaffected.

Personality

In spite of his intimidating appearance, Cyn Nar is courageous and willing to stand up for what he truly believes, even though it will render him dead or a disgrace in the eyes of his fellow Ishreeve. He is also loyal to his Kestrel-1 teammates and willing to assist them in the face of great danger. Upon his brainwashing by Tor Morghul, however, after being brought back from the dead following his death at the hands of one of Tor Morghul' new Ishreeve, Cyn Nar became emotionally fanatical, distant, cold and ruthless, showing no mercy or remorse, suggesting an almost psychopathic nature. 

Cyn Nar has been known to show a humorous side occasionally. When discussing Setesh, he told a Ishreeve joke about the different Ishreeve guards: "A Serpent guard, a Horus guard and a Setesh guard meet on a neutral planet. It is a tense moment. The Serpent guard's eyes glow. The Horus guard's beak glistens. The Setesh guard's nose...drips." 

However, he correctly said the phrase "Taking out two birds with but one stone" and when Cyn Nar goes through the airlock on Kor Reeve's ship and Colonel Tal Korr asks him to speak he says "One small step for Ishreeve."

While not possessing any preternatural abilities as such, Cyn Nar makes up for it with his superior combat training and proficiency in Ishreeve close-quarter combat styles, as well as his ability to quickly adapt to new weaponry. Because of his training as Tor Morghul' First Prime, Cyn Nar has been trained in various skills that are essential to a Concord of SelCrowe's First Prime, which are now used in the services of Kestrel-1. Cyn Nar is an accomplished tracker, having demonstrated this on many occasions, and has exceptional sensory skills. Occasionally this has inadvertently gotten him into trouble with local law enforcement - on one occasion, he prevented an assault, taking down three men at once. He was also able to throw an avocado precisely to hit a purse-snatcher from a long distance, knocking him out. These incidents nevertheless highlight Cyn Nar's lawfulness and reliability. 

As stated by Colonel Tal Korr, Cyn Nar is physically much stronger than anyone he knows, even after having his Jordune Braketh removed; a trait he puts to great use in combat. Before having his Jordune permanently removed, it instinctively imbued Cyn Nar with an impressive healing and recovery rate, a long lifespan, perfect health, an immunity to a great range of diseases, poisons, and toxins, and the ability to survive an extended period without oxygen under water. Because of the presence of Fenreeve within his Jordune symbiote, Cyn Nar has the ability to sense the presence of other symbiotes and, because their presence disturbs the Jordune, the Dorbrandt as well. Since he began to use Xanrynn as a substitute for his symbiote, he has lost many of these traits, though some remain in a diminished capacity as a result of maintaining his outstanding physical conditioning and/or his experiences and exposures. 

The word "Indeed" is the catchphrase and one of the most frequent words spoken by Cyn Nar. He said this many times over the years, although he didn't say it often in the first few years he was involved with Concord of NorDrax. The word "indeed" is also said by his teammates on a few occasions. In one instance, all of SG1, apart from Cyn Nar said the word before embarking on a mission. In another instance, Lor Vex told Cyn Nar that he says the word a lot, to which Cyn Nar replied, "Do I? I had not noticed" indicating that while he did it and made a habit out of it, it was something he did subconsciously and it was likely never pointed out to him.

*Rya'c: While still in service to Tor Morghul, Cyn Nar was married to Drey'auc (now deceased) of the Cord'ai Plains and had a son, Rya'c. However, even with such an influential new husband, Drey'auc was unable to prevent Tor Morghul from abducting Rya'c and brainwashing him into publicly denouncing his father. Cyn Nar believed Rya'c would not succumb to such manipulation, but Kestrel-1 eventually rescued Rya'c and brought him and Drey'auc back to Vor March (Fro'tak was killed trying to betray Kestrel-1) where Cyn Nar soon discovered Rya'c truly was brainwashed and conditioned to immolate himself to destroy Concord of NorDrax. Cyn Nar had to shoot him with a Jortallis to break the conditioning. Rya'c and Drey'auc left to live in the Land of Light (VP-797 by designation) soon after, and eventually moved to a rebel Ishreeve camp, wherein Drey'auc died as a result of refusal of a new larval symbiote when hers matured, not wanting to deprive another Ishreeve of life. He was against his son's marriage to Kar'yn, a student of Ishta, believing he was too young, but he finally accepted it. 

Romances

*Drey'auc

*Shan'auc: Shan'auc of the Red Hills was a priestess on Ish Drift and an old love of Cyn Nar. They rekindled their feelings when she reappeared in his life, but she was killed by Tanith soon thereafter. 

*Ishta: Ishta is the leader of the female Ishreeve on Hak'tyl. They rebelled against the Jordune Moloc because he ordered the murder of any female Ishreeve baby, forcing her and her fellow priestesses to secretly smuggle female children off the planet instead of murdering them. She felt the Ishreeve rebellion was useless, believing it better to indiscriminately kill any Jordune who posed an immediate threat (particularly Moloc). The other Ishreeve Rebel leaders, meanwhile, tried to see the "bigger picture" and not allow a single Jordune to become powerful by killing too many prospective opponents. This difference in philosophies strained Cyn Nar's and Ishta's relationship for a time. After Moloc was eventually killed, however, Hal Zyx took over Moloc's territory, and Ishta came to see the wisdom of Cyn Nar and the other Rebel Ishreeve's strategy. Cyn Nar and Ishta still keep in touch months later, however, as Rya'c tells Cyn Nar that things would be easier for everyone else if he and Ishta just admit they love each other.
