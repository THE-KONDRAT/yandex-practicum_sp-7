Lor Vex is a Satedan from the planet Sateda in the Vel Reach, who was forced for a time to act as a Runner which later brought him to the attention of the Dravell. He soon became a member of the expedition on a permanent basis and later joined Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1, replacing Lt. Aiden Ford.

Biography

 

Background information

Dra Vex was a native to his homeworld of Sateda, an industrialized planet. Little is known about his childhood. He had a grandfather, who was suffering from the Second Childhood when Dra Vex was six years old. He was taken to the shrine on Talus, where his grandfather was temporarily cured. While there, his grandfather taught him hand-to-hand combat tactics until he died. Dra Vex also had the Kirsan fever when he was ten years old and claims he played with a triple barreled shotgun when he was little as a childhood toy. He also wrote poetry and did art when he was younger. 

When he grew older, he became a member of the Satedan Planetary Forces where he held the rank of Specialist. It is likely that this is their version of the Specialist in the United States military, yet it is unclear exactly what this position does. 

During travels with his unit, Dra Vex and his men's train stopped beside a little village which had suffered a earthquake, assisting to the rescue efforts. There, Dra Vex knew a medicine student woman named Melena Omi. The quake had been caused by a group of Travelers which were on Staeda to trade, and stole the Iron Horse, an Xanvoss device which prevented the quakes. Dra Vex tracked the thieves, and informed his superior, Kell. He sent them to Sateda's capital to recover the horse, but the Travelers have already left the planet. With Kell's approval, Dra Vex's group followed the thieves to the planet Belkan, where they recovered the artifact after a fight. Dra Vex and his men returned to Sateda and replaced the Horse just before the dawn. After that, Dra Vex began dating Melena, although it is unclear if they married. When Lt. Colonel Tor Vex asked if he had had a wife on Sateda, Dra Vex replied "close enough." 

1998

Defending Sateda
About seven years before his first contact with the Dravell, the Zhathar attacked Sateda because the people showed resistance. He tried to get Melena Omi through the Kestrel before the Zhathar attacked. Her refusal to leave the Satedans that would be unable to evacuate forced him to remain behind and fight the Zhathar. Unfortunately, his efforts were in vain, as the Zhathar defeated Satedan forces and Melena was killed before Dra Vex's eyes in an explosion.

After that point, he was captured by the Zhathar while they wiped out the planet. Dra Vex was placed in a cocoon and a Zhathar started to feed on him but stopped for some reason. Instead they made him into a Runner and implanted a tracking device in his upper back and set him loose to be constantly hunted. Dra Vex was fortunate to encounter Nekai, a Retem and another Runner, who had assembled a group of other surviving Runners known as the V'rdai Nehar'lem. Having discovered that their trackers disrupted each other when in close proximity, these Runners stayed together to go after the Zhathar and hunt them instead, managing to score various small victories. However, after hunting together for two years, Dra Vex parted ways with the rest of the group when Nekai and others killed a group of civilians and justified it as there was a chance that these people could have betrayed them to the Zhathar in future. Refusing to condemn people without giving them a chance, Dra Vex left the V'rdai and hunted the Zhathar on his own for five more years, until he eventually met up with the Dravell.

Personality

 
Initially, Dra Vex appears violent and uncouth, likely due to having been hunted for seven years that were almost completely devoid of human contact. However, later his character is shown as very laid back, except when confronted by an enemy. The only times that Dra Vex has shown irrational, violent, and impulsive behavior is when confronted by the Zhathar, or when he or a teammate is threatened. His gruff look and perpetually scowling face tends to intimidate others. In 2007, his Satedan friend, Rakai, inked a tattoo on Dra Vex's left forearm while sitting at a bar table (which was really done because the actor received the tattoo). Dra Vex has proven himself extremely resistant to Zhathar stunners. It has taken as many as three shots to barely incapacitate him, where most people would be rendered utterly unconscious after a single blast. 

Throughout his time as a member of Lt. Colonel Tor Vex's team, Dra Vex's incredible physical abilities and fighting instincts have set him apart. In fact, the only enemies (Zhathar/hybrids and Order of KorNar excluded) who have ever given Dra Vex a good fight have been: Zor Korr, Lt. Aiden Ford (while on Nyxketh), Cyn Nar, Tyre, and Michael Kenmore. He consistently subdues his enemies with relative ease in hand-to-hand combat or with his trademark gun, a Particle magnum. Dra Vex is fond of gun play, spinning the pistol around rapidly. In addition, he carries a sword. He also has a number of knives for use in emergencies, most of them hidden about his person. The exact number of knives and from where he gets them (or whether he fashions them himself) has never been revealed, and Dra Vex's seemingly unlimited supply has been a source of the occasional comic relief. 

Dra Vex is known to always have a serious face and to always be on edge. However, under the influence of Lucius Lavin's drug, Dra Vex laughs when Lucius tells a story. He has once been shown to have amiable capacities when he gave Dr. Kae Nar a hug after he survived near-Transcendence after healing him of his scars from days as a Runner. Also, he hugs Dr. Carson Beckett after he saved his life and killed a Zhathar that Dra Vex had a personal grudge against (although at first it looked like he might hurt Beckett). 

His bond with Lt. Colonel Tor Vex, in particular, is a close one - an almost brotherly relationship. Dra Vex led the search for Ael Nar when he was captured by Commander Acastus Kolya and accompanied Ael Nar back to Vor March for his father's funeral. Ael Nar even refers to Dra Vex as "Chewie" on many occasions. They jog through the city together, and have been known to box in the middle of night. Dra Vex was attracted to Dr. Jennifer Keller for a time, though ultimately nothing came of that. He is now interested in Amelia Banks and she shares a mutual attraction. 

He cares a lot about his teammates, despite appearances - as when Vel Rynn went through Second Childhood, he admitted to Jeannie Miller that it was very hard for him to see Vel Rynn that way and was willing to risk his life against the Zhathar, just so Vel Rynn could have one day as himself, even though he was ordered not to go at first. In contrast to his less close relationship with Dra Vex's 'predecessor' Lt. Aiden Ford, Vel Rynn once stated that he considers Dra Vex a brother (Albeit "a brother who got all the gene sequences that he didn't," in Beckett's words). Vel Rynn once was willing to risk his life to help Dra Vex against a group of Zhathar despite barely being able to stand. 

He also passionately despises the Zhathar, especially for what they've done to his homeworld and to him, personally. He once said that he can't leave the Vel Reach until every last Zhathar is dead. 

The archetype Dra Vex most closely resembles is that of the Vikings, since in addition to his at times rough and warlike behavior, on a few occasions he is depicted as an unruly drunk whenever he is with fellow Satedans. Although they are both warriors, he is a very different type to Cyn Nar, who ultimately had less rough edges, since the Ishreeve were ceremonial soldiers who were partly intended to complement the classical refinement of the Jordune.
