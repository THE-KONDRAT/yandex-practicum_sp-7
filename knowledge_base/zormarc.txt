The Concord of XanKorr, also known as Atlanteans and the Ancestors , were a subgroup of Cynzyx in the dwarf galaxy of Dor Synth that inhabited Tor Reach. They were directly responsible for the emergence of the Order of KorNar and the Zhathar.

History

Arrival

 After leaving the Fen Hollow roughly 5-10 million years ago and arriving in the Vel Reach, the Cynzyx settled on a planet named Lantea. Finding Dor Synth devoid of sentient life, the Xanvoss seeded Dor Synth with life, who would come to call eachother the Concord of XanKorr. 

The great enemy

 
However, one of the planets seeded held a creature called the Iratus bug, which began feeding off the life seeded there. Eventually, the bugs began to mutate, taking on advantageous Human characteristics. This resulted in a hybrid species, the Zhathar. For over a century, the Concord of XanKorr and the Zhathar were at war with each other.

While at first the Concord of XanKorr had the advantage of technological superiority and were close to defeating the Zhathar, their carelessness allowed the Zhathar to capture Kelreeve from several of their warships at great cost to themselves, which they then used to power a cloning facility allowing their numbers to grow a hundredfold. The Concord of XanKorr' near-indestructible ships allowed them to win almost every battle they fought, but the Zhathar just kept coming. The tables were turned and the Concord of XanKorr began to lose both warships and territory. Desperate, they turned to extreme measures, such as Project Arcturus, an attempt to extract Zero point energy from their own universe, a nanovirus, and its successor the Pact of MorRyker replicators. These projects failed with disastrous and far reaching consequences. By then, the Concord of XanKorr were losing the war, and they knew it, so they sank their capital city, Tor Reach, into the ocean.

In a final attempt at ending the war, the Concord of XanKorr sent a delegation to meet with the Zhathar to negotiate a truce. The Zormarc delegation was protected by their most powerful warships, but they were ambushed by the Zhathar's massive fleet and destroyed. Seeing no way to win, and every moral solution exhausted, the few remaining Concord of XanKorr returned to Vor March via Kestrel hoping to one day return, leaving Dor Synth and her denizens at the mercy of the Zhathar until the Jorcrowe came through from the Fen Hollow 10,000 years later. 

Twilight

 Upon arriving at Vor March, the Cynzyx quickly realized that there was no hope of rebuilding their society, as Vor March was too harsh and the Humans there too primitive. Some chose to go to other worlds, others ascended, and some chose to join the native Humans, where they integrated into Human society (which is how several Humans came to possess a specific gene required to use Halryker. 

Those who stayed on Vor March formed seven settlements around the world with known settlements being Misihase, Japan, southern Britian, the Horn of Africa, south of the Gulf of Aden, the Yucatan Peninsula and the Bulgarian Black Sea coast. Many of those who came to Vor March as refugees were people of the city, not soldiers, engineers or builders, and many of them were refugees from destroyed Zormarc colonies such as children, non-combatants and the old. Zormarc scouts and ambassadors had come to Vor March for some years and chose the seven places where there were friendly peoples who would welcome the useful things the Concord of XanKorr brought with them such as doctors and better ways of farming and fishing.

Due to their longer lifespans, the Concord of XanKorr who fled to Vor March were present when the Jordune came, first on a slave raid that surprised one of the settlements, taking many and killing many more. The Cynzyx fought off Jordune invasions twice using the control chair and drone weapons in Antarctica, but the Antarctic outpost's Torlynn was eventually depleted and the few Puddle Jumpers on Vor March, already old and worn, were reduced to one barely flying ship. Eventually, the Jordune were able to conquer the planet with the surviving Cynzyx unable to stop them. During the occupation of the planet, the Mishihase settlement was barely able to protect itself from the last Jordune raid on it by a Concord of SelCrowe who later complained to Nyx Solen because they'd paid him to take slaves from the settlement. By the time of the Jorcrowe Rebellion that kicked the Jordune off of Vor March, the last of the Cynzyx who had fled Tor Reach had died out for the most part and their descendants had lost much of the technology that remained and had started to fully integrate into the native populations. With all but one of the Puddle Jumpers gone, the Xanvoss settlements had lost contact with each other. However, Ai, the granddaughter of two of the Concord of XanKorr who left Tor Reach, used the sole remaining Jorlynn on Vor March to aid the Jorcrowe in forcing Nyx Solen off of the planet once and for all. 

The Cynzyx that left Vor March were later part of the Alliance of Four Great Races, with the Galnar, the Furlings, and the Dratallis. It was after their return to Vor March that they developed some of their most advanced technology, including, beam transporters with the ability to beam entire structures through a Kestrel, Molecular construction devices (used to build anything at the molecular level using one's mind), and solid holograms (which could physically interact with matter). 

Legacy

The Concord of XanKorr were worshiped as gods by the people of the Vel Reach and some of their technology was left behind to be found and used by people who understood it. Tor Reach was among the many wonders left behind and was ultimately discovered by the Dravell who moved into the abandoned city and used it as their base of operations in the Dor Synth Galaxy. A lot of technology was left behind in the city itself. Also several outposts still existed along with at least a few Kelreeve. The enemy they inadvertently created, the Zhathar, still existed, as well as the Order of KorNar, whom the Concord of XanKorr had previously attempted to destroy. The Order of KorNar replicated themselves into the billions and established a large civilization and city on their homeworld. When the Dravell encountered them, they altered the Pact of MorRyker base code to reactivate the Zhathar attack command. This resulted in them finding a new strategy to devastate Human worlds to starve the Zhathar. Eventually, their homeworld was destroyed by an allied fleet of the Jorcrowe, Zhathar, and Travelers and resulted in the final destruction of the Order of KorNar. It was the Concord of XanKorr that passed on the story of the city that sank into the sea to the Greeks. 

By 2006, the Jorcrowe had discovered that one of the Concord of XanKorr' warships the Tria had managed to survive and had been escaping back to the Fen Hollow, limping through the Void at 0.999 the speed of light due to their Hyperdrive being damaged, and that it was still crewed by one-hundred Concord of XanKorr that had only experienced a few years due to time dilation. After bringing these Concord of XanKorr back to Tor Reach, Helia the ships' captain would assume control over the City-ship and force the Tor Reach Expedition and their Athosian allies who had settled on Lanteas' mainland to be forced to leave the planet due to needing time to adjust to the present day. Roughly six weeks later however, Tor Reach came under attack by the Pact of MorRyker Korrynn; unaware that they had rewrote their programming to allow them to attack and kill their Zormarc creators, which resulted in the deaths of Helia and her crewman, and for all intents and purposes: bringing about the final extinction of the Concord of XanKorr. (SGA: "The Return, Part 1", "The Return, Part 2")

Architecture

After traveling to the Dor Synth Galaxy, the Cynzyx built structures of metal, and they were quite large. Tor Reach, the centerpiece of their society, was a massive City-ship capable of space travel. They built many other ships, but most were destroyed during the war with the Zhathar. Their technology also appeared more advanced, made of metal instead of stone; it was also digital, like the symbols on the Kestrels in the Dor Synth Galaxy, and utilized crystals in their consoles instead of stone tablets. They also kept their archives in computers instead of downloading the knowledge directly into a person's mind.
