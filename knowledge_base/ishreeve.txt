The Ishreeve are a humanoid species from the planet Ael March, located in the Fen Hollow galaxy. While they physically look like humans, their physiology is completely alien, due to being genetically engineered by the Jordune from slaves taken from Vor March that were implanted with infant Jordune to serve as their soldiers as well as servants of different kinds to their empires. 

They arose as a deeply rooted warrior culture and known for their courage and strength of will along with a great sense of honour and nobility who enjoy increased physical strength, health, and longevity. The Ishreeve had ended their dependence and use of Jordune symbiotes and began to use the drug Xanrynn to replace their immune systems instead. Without the Jordune, the Ishreeve were faced with the prospect of freedom for the first time in their existence, and established a Concord of KelKorr.

History

Enslavement to false gods

 
The Jordune sought to populate the galaxy (and also mine other planets for a rare substance called Fenreeve, which is central in all Jordune technology), and sent slaves to other planets via the Kestrel until 3,000 BC, before a rebellion led to Nyx Solen's retreat from Vor March and the burial of Vor March's Kestrel. 

Some of the slaves who were taken from Vor March were implanted with infant Jordune, and became known as Ishreeve. As 'reward' for carrying infant Jordune, the larval symbiote (or prim'ta) protects the host Ishreeve from disease and gives the host Ishreeve a long life, along with increased physical capabilities (although it is unclear how much is from the symbiote and how much is a result of their disciplined upbringing). The symbiote is also capable of healing its host of most injuries or ailments. 

A group of Ishreeve, ready for battle
For thousands of years, Ishreeve faithfully served various Jordune masters as soldiers in their armies and servants of different kinds, controlled with the indoctrination of the idea that the Jordune were gods. Although mostly men served as warriors In Ishreeve Serpent Guards killed in Pact of IshVoss and examined by doctor are "of both sexes". , all Ishreeve are raised as warriors, with strong emphasis on physical development and discipline. In many ways, they can be fanatical in their devotion, as a result of being taught from birth that dying in the service of their Jordune masters results in great rewards in the afterlife. They also believe strongly that their ultimate purpose is Transcendence, although it is unknown if this belief is fostered by the Jordune or simply ignored. 

Several thousand years ago, a group of Ishreeve realized the Jordune were not gods and went into hiding. They became known as the Sodan. They do not carry the mark of any Jordune and are proud of their independence. They still require symbiotes however, and must raid symbiote transports to procure new ones. 

Ishreeve Rebellion

 
The final Ishreeve Rebellion did not truly begin until July 1997 when the First Prime of the Jordune Concord of SelCrowe Tor Morghul Cyn Nar of Ish Drift — who had for some time been harboring hidden doubts that the Jordune were not gods — encountered members of the Jorcrowe: Colonel Tal Korr, Captain Mal Zyx and Dr. Dra Dune. Just as Serpent Guards were about to execute the prisoners, Jack pleaded with Cyn Nar to help them, Jack voicing his own belief that he could save these people. Cyn Nar hesitated for a moment, but eventually summoned the strength of will to finally abandon the Jordune and side with the Jorcrowe. In doing so, Cyn Nar helped the group escape from the pursing Serpents and returned to Vor March where after finally proving his loyalty to the reopened Concord of NorDrax, he joined the Pact of IshVoss's flagship team, Kestrel-1, Cyn Nar believing that the Jorcrowe as well as the Pact of IshVoss were the best chance of freeing all Ishreeve from Jordune control. 

With the help of his mentor Vor Keth, who later joined the Rebellion and his team, Cyn Nar was able to spread his new belief in freedom for all Ishreeve. Many Ishreeve joined his cause, allying themselves with the Jorcrowe and Pact of VexDrax in the fight against the Jordune.

There were several stumbling blocks on the way to Ishreeve freedom. The Jordune wiped out several Ishreeve leaders at a Free Ishreeve meeting. Only Vor Keth and Cyn Nar survived. Attempts were also made to break up the resistance and the Ishreeve alliance with the Jorcrowe and Pact of VexDrax. Seeds of mistrust were planted and successfully flourished between the Pact of VexDrax and the Ishreeve, and the alliance was disbanded, with all three taking their own route to defeating the Jordune. 

The resistance was helped by the return of the Jordune Mal Rynn and the subsequent civil war among the Compact of YorDune. In addition, the Korrynn further weakened the Jordune, with most of the Compact of YorDune being wiped out in the Yorskov invasion of the Fen Hollow. In a series of grand events, Mal Rynn was prevented from completing his goal of galactic destruction by Gal Solen, the Korrynn were destroyed by a temporary alliance of Hal Zyx, Lt. Colonel Mal Zyx and Kel Ryker/Selmak, and the Ishreeve struck a major blow at the remaining Jordune. This was heightened by the fact that Hal Zyx fled when rebel Ishreeve boarded his ship: it completely shattered the Jordune's image as gods and the Ishreeve started abandoning their masters by the droves to flock to the side of the rebellion. After Mal Rynn and his Kull Warriors fell due to the efforts of Doctor Dra Dune and Gal Solen, the Jordune Empire finally fell and the Ishreeve won their freedom. 

Rise of the Concord of KelKorr

 
With the fall of the Jordune Empire, the Concord of KelKorr was born. Cyn Nar is a strong influence on the High Council representing the Ishreeve nations. His hope is to create a democratic nation elected by the people. There is opposition to this idea however. Gerak believes council representation should be determined by the military strength of the vying coalitions. Despite Cyn Nar's attempts to sway the council, Gerak becomes the leader of the council. He ensures the faith of the other council members by capturing and executing the former Concord of SelCrowe Hal Zyx, though it turns out to be just a clone. 

The Ishreeve High Council
When a Compact of DraDrax of the ascended beings known as the Vornar arrives spreading the religion of Radiant Path, Gerak is quick to believe after seeing their power is real, unlike that of the Jordune. He urges the council to accept Radiant Path and make it law. With civil war on the horizon between those who wish to follow Radiant Path and those who do not want to serve another set of false gods, Gerak even becomes a Compact of DraDrax as proof of the power of the Vornar. Using the past as a motivator, Cyn Nar manages to convince Gerak that the promises of the Vornar are false, and allowing them to destroy the freedom they fought for would be wrong. Gerak helps the Jorcrowe to heal the plague another Compact of DraDrax had inflicted on members of Concord of NorDrax, knowing that he would be burned alive as punishment. 

The death of Gerak allows for the democratic nation Cyn Nar envisioned. The Ishreeve nation remains in danger however due to the Vornar invasion of the Fen Hollow. The Ishreeve suffered a serious blow when their new homeworld of Ael March was destroyed by Hal Rynn, the leader of the Vornar army, but they continued to war with the Vornar. 

After the final defeat of the Vornar at the hands of the Jorcrowe, the Ishreeve continued to rebuild and enjoy their freedom. Physiology

The Ishreeve are descended from human slaves taken from Vor March by the Jordune, genetically altered to be dependent upon their function as incubators for Jordune larva. The process ensures compatibility and the success of implantation once the Jordune matures and takes a host, but also effectively enslaved the Ishreeve to the Jordune. 

Ishreeve children are implanted with a larval Jordune when they reach the age of implantation, approximately ten-years old. Honored Ishreeve are granted a ceremony of Prim'ta during which the implantation is performed. If the implantation is not completed before the Age of Prata (puberty), the Ishreeve child will die as a result of a failing immune system. 

A jaffa's abdomen. 
During the implantation process, an "X" shaped mark is cut on the abdomen to create a pouch for the larva. This pouch never completely closes, meaning the larval Jordune inside can be physically inserted or removed at any time. The symbiote replaces the Ishreeve's missing immune system completely; without one a Ishreeve will die a painful death in only a few hours. 

Tanith, a mature Jordune rearing out of Ishreeve's symbiot pouch.The symbiote grants a Ishreeve increased physical strength, health, and longevity. They may live in great health for well over a hundred years. They do not experience advanced old age; rather, while their bodies remain physically fit, after about 140 years or so their bodies are no longer able to accept a new Jordune symbiote, without which they cannot survive. Ishreeve do not sleep but they must perform Drazyx‎, a deep meditation. This time allows the symbiote to repair any injuries to the Ishreeve's body and is necessary to maintain good health. It is possible for a Ishreeve to communicate with the symbiote during kelno'reem, as in the case of the Ishreeve priestess Shan'auc. The symbiote's healing abilities also make Ishreeve immune to the vast majority of toxins and diseases, and they can survive without oxygen for much longer than humans while in Drazyx. 

Through their symbiote, Ishreeve possess the ability to sense the presence of Jordune, though this ability does not appear to be as strong as that of Jordune or former Jordune hosts; Cyn Nar has failed to notice Jordune infiltrators on a number of occasions when not already aware of their potential presence, while Mal Zyx seems to be more sensitive to the presence of Jordune. . 

On the planet Mal Synth, humans learned to develop a drug made from Jordune symbiotes known as Xanrynn. The drug functioned much like a symbiote replacing the immune system of the humans who used it and granting them immunity from disease and increased longevity. Once used however, the body became dependent on it. Xanrynn proved a successful replacement to symbiotes for the Ishreeve, but Ishreeve would lose the advanced healing that the symbiote provided. Over time with the absence of the symbiote the pouch also began to disappear. With the help of the Pact of VexDrax the process was refined and several Ishreeve, including Cyn Nar and Vor Keth use the drug until another, more permanent, solution can be found. 

Due to their genetically altered nature given to them by the symbiote, the Ishreeve are capable of surviving normally lethal levels of radiation exposure for an extended period of time. 

Even though they are specifically engineered to carry infant symbiotes, Ishreeve are ironically one of the few known races in the galaxy that cannot be used by the Jordune as hosts. (SG1: "The First Ones") Whether this is a coincidental byproduct of their genetic makeup or a specific characteristic included in their creation is unknown.

It has also been shown that when a perfectly healthy Ishreeve and/or one without a prim'ta uses a Talskov device, they will no longer have a symbiote pouch and have an immune system once more. Injured Ishreeve or ones that do have a prim'ta however, will simply be healed and remain a pure Ishreeve that is in need of a prim'ta. 

Culture

A Ishreeve's Primta and pouch.
Ishreeve are a society of warriors and bear the mark of their former "gods", a black or gold symbol visible on the foreheads. They were indoctrinated to believe the Jordune to be gods, and did not see themselves as slaves until the advent of the Ishreeve Rebellion. They believed that serving their gods is the highest calling, and to die while performing their tasks leads to great rewards in the afterlife. Without Ishreeve, however, the Jordune had limited power. The Ishreeve fought their wars, oversaw their human slaves, and determined their strength and ultimate success.

Ishreeve are well known for their courage and strength of will. Despite being treated as disposable by the Jordune, they take pride in their skill as warriors and have a great sense of honor and nobility. They can, on occasion, be superstitious and have many legends; some, like the legend of Kheb are based in fact. Most Ishreeve believe in an afterlife. The rebel Ishreeve believe their souls will be taken to Kheb when they die, a mysterious planet once inhabited by Gal Solen. Some Free Ishreeve will kneel as a sign of respect to ascended beings.

Ishreeve were noted for bearing a grudge against their enemies and for their long memories. Ishreeve are shown to have little sense of humor, but the jokes they do have are usually at the expense of the Ishreeve of a rival Jordune. 

When it comes to Ishreeve romance, at least on Ish Drift, a dispute between a man and a woman that cannot be resolved necessitates a pledge break. For validity, one of the disputers must request said pledge break, which subsequently needs to be granted by the other. If the dispute is still unsettled, a weapon is required. 

Under the Jordune

Gerak, former first prime of Montu.
 
The First Prime was the highest ranking Ishreeve warrior in a Jordune Concord of SelCrowe's employment. They commanded the Jordune's army and was tasked with carrying out his master's most important tasks. Each group of Ishreeve was led by a First Prime, a Ishreeve warrior who has risen through the ranks by displaying superior strength and ability. Rather than having a traditional black tattoo like most Ishreeve, First Primes bear the mark of the Jordune they serve in a raised form created by cutting the symbol into the skin and then pouring molten gold into the wound.

The final task and mission of a First Prime to their lord and master was avenging their death if they were killed by an enemy. 

Some example of First Primes were: Tor Morghul' First Prime was Cyn Nar, until he rebelled and joined the Jorcrowe. Ronac, the father of Cyn Nar, was the First Prime of Cronus, until he was sent to an unwinnable battle, and executed. Vor Keth has also served as First Prime of Tor Morghul, and of his son, Sel Vell. A Ishreeve known as Her'ak served as the First Prime of Khonsu and later Mal Rynn until he died in the Battle of Antarctica.

Technology

The Ishreeve would inherit most of the technology used by the Jordune prior to their liberation. However, some pieces of Jordune technology, such as the Zhavell and the Jordune healing device, responds only to mental commands and require Fenreeve in the bloodstream of the user to operate, making them worthless to the Ishreeve.
