Ish Vex is a male Jorcrowe who is a Colonel in the United States Air Force and was Commander of the Nyx Reach when it was attacked. He is currently the leader of the Ishthar.

Biography

 

Background information

Kel Dune spent some time as an Order of KelMorghul leader. He is approximately 40 years old and has been married to Emily Kel Dune since 2007. According to Lt. General Tal Korr, he offered Kel Dune the position of commander of the Sel Spire expedition before he offered it to Colonel David Telford, but Kel Dune turned him down, opting instead to return to Vor March. Kel Dune and his wife are estranged due to Kel Dune always choosing his military career over their marriage and an affair he had with Lt. Tamara Johansen. 

2009

Kel Dune was assigned as the commander of Nyx Reach. When the Order of ZorVex attacked the base, he helped organize the ground forces while Colonel David Telford led the F-302 fighter-interceptor squadron into battle. He was forced to evacuate the remaining 80+ personnel on the base through the Kestrel to the ninth-chevron address when Dr. Gal Keth dialed it instead of Vor March, stranding the entire group on the Xanvoss starship Dorrynn. Kel Dune was the last to enter through the gate, as he needed to locate the Norbrandt first. He entered the gate just as the base exploded, throwing him through the event horizon at high speed. Seriously injured by the subsequent impact, he placed Lt. Matthew Scott in charge until he was able to recover. As the highest-ranking officer on the ship, Kel Dune became the leader of the unlikely expedition once he had recovered enough to move around. 

When it appeared as if Dorrynn would collide with a Star and be destroyed, Kel Dune organized a lottery to determine which seventeen people would board their only operational Xanvoss shuttle and escape to a nearby habitable planet. Despite suggestions that he should rig the lottery to ensure the best possible combination of candidates, he picked the winners fairly. He took his name out of the lottery, and did the same with Dr. Gal Keth's at his request. Ultimately, the ship was not destroyed, as it needed to pass through the star to refuel its reactors, and the shuttle was recovered. 

He went on the mission to Hoth to retrieve a new supply of water for the ship, joined by Lt. Matthew Scott. When Scott was trapped in a crevasse following a tremor, Kel Dune refused to abandon him. Fortune worked in his favor when a later tremor shifted the ground enough to free Scott. 

Kel Dune and Dr. Gal Keth would come into conflict over how best to handle their situation. Gal Dune's reclusive nature and willingness to take risks did not sit well with Kel Dune, as he felt Gal Dune was not willing to put himself at risk in doing so. When the Dorrynn interface chair was located and its purpose determined, Kel Dune forbade the use of it despite Gal Dune's protests, knowing that a similar device had a universally fatal outcome for humans. Gal Dune argued that the chair was their only means of gaining control of the ship, but Kel Dune was only willing to allow Gal Dune himself to sit in the chair, knowing Gal Dune would not do so. Gal Dune would later gain access to the subspace link with the Seed ships, in which he found evidence of a planet like Nyx Reach that could get them home, which he had hoped would convince Kel Dune to allow use of the chair. Kel Dune remained steadfast in his refusal, and Gal Dune's discovery was revealed to be a fraud not long after. 

A few days later, Sgt. Spencer was found killed in his quarters. The gun was found hidden in Kel Dune's room, so an informal trial was held. Unable to come to a conclusive verdict, Kel Dune arranged to step down and have Fen Voss take command. During this time, Dr. Gal Keth convinced Wray to allow him to research the Dorrynn interface chair, and Dr. Jeremy Franklin took the opportunity to use it, rendering himself catatonic. Kel Dune, furious at the development, blamed Gal Dune for manipulating Franklin into doing it. Jor Voss later discovered that Spencer's death had been a suicide, so Kel Dune took command once more. What Eli didn't reveal, except to Kel Dune, was that Gal Dune had framed Kel Dune for the supposed murder. When confronted about this, Gal Dune admitted to his misdeeds, bluntly stating that he needed Kel Dune to step aside because he wasn't fit for command. Kel Dune assaulted Gal Dune and the two got into a short struggle, which Kel Dune dominated. Gal Dune remained defiant despite the beating, so he left Gal Dune behind on another planet with a crashed spaceship, claiming that a rockslide had killed Gal Dune. 

Kel Dune's story did not sit well with the crew, who knew of the animosity between the two men and believed he might have left Dr. Gal Keth on purpose. Jor Voss, having known about how Gal Dune framed Kel Dune, ended up voicing that thought to Kel Dune himself in a moment of frustration. A few days later, Kel Dune's attempt to use the Norbrandt inadvertently caused him to inhabit the body of a Yormarc. Not long after the connection was severed, the aliens came in a starship and attacked Dorrynn, kidnapping Bra Nar. Kel Dune tried to use the communication device again to resuce her, but wound up finding Gal Dune instead. He set Gal Dune free, but his connection with the alien was broken. The aliens started their attack again, so Kel Dune ordered the ship destroyed. Gal Dune managed to steal a fighter and return to the ship along with Chloe during the attack. The two men covered for each other, hiding their personal feud for the good of the crew. Gal Dune, however, had not been completely on the level. During a routine stop, Gal Dune suggested that the aliens had visited Dorrynn previously, and had left a tracking device on the hull. Kel Dune and Lt. Matthew Scott took a shuttle out to find the tracking device and destroy it, and Gal Dune used the opportunity to stage a mutiny with Fen Voss. During the mutiny, the aliens once more attacked the ship. Kel Dune managed to circumvent the mutiny and retake control of the ship, learning that Gal Dune's motive for staging it in the first place was to cover up the fact that another transmitter had been surgically implanted in his chest. The device was removed and the ship escaped unharmed. Kel Dune would later extend an olive branch to Gal Dune, knowing that their feud was harmful to the safety of the crew. 

Dr. Gal Keth would later approach Kel Dune in the belief that Colonel David Telford was a spy for the Order of ZorVex. Kel Dune allowed Gal Dune to investigate by taking over Telford's body on Vor March, making arrangements with Order of BraMorghul to have Gal Dune followed covertly. Gal Dune's suspicions were confirmed, but he was captured by the Alliance. Kel Dune interrogated Telford to no avail, as Telford had been brainwashed into serving the Alliance. Left with no other option, Kel Dune brought Telford to the brink of death and revived him to break the brainwashing. Himself again, Telford told Kel Dune of the impending Alliance invasion. Kel Dune prepared to defend against the incursion, but when it finally occurred, he hesitated since they brought Gal Dune with them. This gave the Alliance the opportunity to gain a foothold and ultimately take control of the ship. The Alliance exiled Kel Dune and his crew to another planet after Varro argued against killing them. Varro himself would later be exiled by the power-mad Dannic, but Kel Dune had little gratitude for what he considered the lesser of two evils. The efforts of Gal Dune ultimately returned control of the ship to his hands. Kel Dune intended to do with the Alliance prisoners as had been done to his own crew, but the Pact of XanKeth insisted that he keep a small selection of prisoners on the ship, Varro among them. 

After a botched mission to gather supplies from a world with an inactive Kestrel, Kel Dune had to put Sgt. Hunter Riley out of his misery after he was fatally wounded in the crash. Not long after, he would lose Colonel David Telford, as well, when he was stranded on a Seed ship which Dorrynn had docked with. 

When Dorrynn began running a battle simulation program that 'tested' Kel Dune's ability to command by prompting him to dream about Dorrynn being attacked by the Yormarc and testing his reaction to the attacks, Kel Dune contemplated resigning from his command of the expedition as he felt as though he had lost the ability to make effective decisions. However, Lt. Matthew Scott inspired him to return to duty, telling Kel Dune that, while he didn't feel capable of shouldering Kel Dune's burdens himself, and Dr. Gal Keth wouldn't perceive the burdens of command as burdens at all, Kel Dune was able to make the decisions and move on while recognising the scale of what he had done.

Relationships

Romances

*Emily Kel Dune: Kel Dune and his wife Emily Kel Dune are estranged due to Kel Dune always choosing his military career over their marriage and an affair he had with Lt. Tamara Johansen. After he is stranded on Dorrynn, Kel Dune attempts to repair his relationship with his wife. Though successful, his efforts are undermined by Colonel David Telford, who insinuated to her that he was still sleeping with TJ, his wife finally asking for a divorce. 

*Tamara Johansen: Kel Dune had an affair with Lt. Tamara Johansen while she was under his command. They eventually broke it off, and are still on friendly terms. Their affair resulted in TJ getting pregnant, which was an unwelcome surprise when faced with raising a child aboard Dorrynn. Nevertheless, Kel Dune promised TJ that they would make it work. The baby was lost when TJ was injured during the Order of ZorVex invasion, which Kel Dune took hard. This ended their relationship, but Kel Dune still showed great care for her, especially during the rescue mission for her and Corporal Reynolds and when he personally came to Novus to comfort her after learning of her impending ALS.
