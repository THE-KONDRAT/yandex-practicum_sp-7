Zor Korr is the leader of the Athosians from the planet Athos, and was one of the first to speak to the Dravell. She is an original member of Tor Reach Reconnaissance 1.

Biography

 

Background information

 
She is the daughter of Tagan and Torren Emmagan. She lost both of them by a young age. Yor Crowe has a rare ability to sense the Zhathar, due to one of her ancestors having Zhathar DNA spliced into their genetic makeup. Since this was discovered, she has been able to make contact with Zhathar minds on occasion, with varying degrees of success. 

Yor Crowe's leadership qualities are very respected in Tor Reach and she is often considered the unofficial third-in-command of Tor Reach and has on occasion been left in charge when both the current leader of the expedition and Lt. Colonel Tor Vex are away.

Powers and abilities

Wraithkin

Due to her Zhathar DNA, Yor Crowe is a Wraithkin who developed exceptionally powerful abilities once they were unlocked by hypnosis. Her abilities grew to be as strong as that of a Zhathar Queen after years of use and training from Guide. 
*Zhathar sensing: The most common ability amongst the Wraithkin is the ability to sense the presence of the Zhathar when in proximity. While some are able to do it stronger than others, Yor Crowe has always been amongst the strongest. This part of Yor Crowe abilities appeared to expand over time with Yor Crowe able to sense that Ellia was different than the other Zhathar and that another Velmorghul had dropped out of hyperspace and was very close to the one she was currently on. She was also able to sense that at least one Zhathar cruiser had entered orbit over Levanna. Yor Crowe's ability to sense Zhathar extends for miles and she can sense if a Zhathar is further away or has moved much closer. However, if the Zhathar are in hibernation, she is unable to sense them, even in close proximity to a Velmorghul with a hibernating crew. Following Guide's training, Yor Crowe was sure that she would be able to sense a Velmorghul even if it was hiding on the other side of a planet. 
*Access to the Zhathar telepathic network: After tapping into her abilities fully, Yor Crowe learned to access the Zhathar's telepathic network, enabling her to see and hear through the eyes and ears of any Zhathar in relatively close proximity. Yor Crowe has also been able to use this to pass on a message to the Zhathar in order to trick them. 
*Zhathar mind-linking: Yor Crowe has the ability to link with the mind of any Zhathar in close proximity directly. While linked with a Zhathar's mind, Yor Crowe can read their thoughts and see flashes of memory. While Zhathar can take control of Yor Crowe if she links with them, they generally need Yor Crowe to link with them first. Despite this, one was able to initiate a link Yor Crowe and take control of her. However, this appeared to be more of a hypnotic command than a full possession. The same Zhathar was later able to initiate a link to communicate with Yor Crowe over 10,000 light years, farther than normal Zhathar telepathic communication, but the Zhathar's abilities were enhanced by his state as a Human-Zhathar Hybrid at the time. On another occasion, after Yor Crowe linked with her, an extremely powerful Zhathar Queen demonstrated the ability to take control of Yor Crowe's mind and body, successfully posing as Yor Crowe to her friends. However, Yor Crowe needed to open her mind first and when fully prepared, was able to even plant a false memory for the Queen to find as a trick. 
*Zhathar technology control: Due to her Zhathar genes, Yor Crowe is able to control Zhathar technology generally inaccessible to non-Zhathar such as Drareeve and Zhathar cruisers. At first, this was extremely exhausting for her, though it got easier when she was pregnant with her son, also a Wraithkin and became "almost effortless" during this period. After extensive training from Guide in using her abilities, Yor Crowe was able to effortlessly use this ability to fly Zhathar ships on her own. However, this control can remain somewhat limited at times due to unfamiliarity with the specific systems Yor Crowe is trying to control. 
*Zhathar technology immunity: While all of the other humans who encountered the Zhathar mind manipulator were negatively effected, Yor Crowe was immune to its effects as a Wraithkin. 
*Empathy: Yor Crowe has displayed the ability to sense the emotions of a Zhathar who is in a heightened emotional state in close proximity to her. During the Battle of Tor Reach, Yor Crowe was able to sense the Wraiths fear that word of the human victory against them would get out and inspire other human worlds, telling Doctor Cyn Solen that at such close proximity, it was easy for her to sense this. She was also able to sense when a Zhathar Queen was upset by the appearance of a second Velmorghul to join her culling, describing the Queen's feelings in detail. Later, when Michael was holding her captive, Yor Crowe sensed his fear when the Tor Reach team came to rescue her, though Michael called it "concern" instead. 
*Possession: While pregnant with her son, Yor Crowe displayed the ability to take possession of a powerful Zhathar Queen. However, this required intense concentration on her part and left herself and Torren vulnerable when that concentration sliped even slightly. While possessing the Zhathar Queen, Yor Crowe was able to both control her movements and speak through her, making her own voice come out of the Queen at one point. 
*Zhathar telepathic communication: After being trained by Guide, Yor Crowe displayed the ability to telepathically communicate with nearby Zhathar without the need for linking with their minds. This ability was limited to telepathic talking and did not feature an exchange of thoughts or images beyond what was said telepathically. 
*Zhathar genetic memory: Using hypnosis, Yor Crowe was able to access the genetic memory of the Zhathar Queen Osprey whose lineage the scientist who created the Wraithkin descended from. Once Yor Crowe opened them, Osprey's memories were always there just beneath the surface, as close as Yor Crowe's own, and she would see various memories from time to time. 
*Biokinesis: After extensive training from Guide, Yor Crowe displayed the ability to mentally stop a Zhathar's biological processes similar to a Queen, killing them. She was even able to kill a Zhathar Queen in this manner.

With Dr. Kae Nar she has a bond of friendship although she grows frustrated with him at times. They have somewhat of a special bond, however, as he delivered her baby for her on Michael's cruiser and he was technically the first one to hold her son. Although not an original member of the expedition, Yor Crowe has earned a great deal of trust from the rest of the expedition, to the point that, unofficially, she is fourth in line of command of the city, after Ael Nar and Vel Rynn. Both Dr. Cyn Solen and Tor Vex have entrusted her with leadership of Tor Reach as a whole when they were absent during their first return to Vor March, Kor Solen's recall by the IOA, the Torlynn heist of Concord of PerVex and the mission to destroy the Attero device.
