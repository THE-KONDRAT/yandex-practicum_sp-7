Tor Reach, also called the Lost City of the Xanvoss and the City of the Ancestors, Atlantus in Xanvoss, is a City-ship built by the Xanvoss several million years ago. Some of ancient humanity left the Fen Hollow galaxy for the Vel Reach, taking Tor Reach with them and settling on a planet they named Lantea, which became their new capital.

Millions of years later, after the Concord of XanKorr—the name the Xanvoss came to be known by in the Vel Reach—submerged the city to protect it from the Zhathar, and returned to Vor March through the Kestrel. Over the ages, their story inspired the Jorcrowe myth of Tor Reach.

History

Project

A million years ago, when the Xanvoss starship Dorrynn was launched, the city-ships were in the early design stage: both Dorrynn and the city-ships were competing projects with the same (unknown) mission. Vasi mentioned Tor Reach, implying that the city was the first of this class name was decided prior to its construction.

Xanvoss use

 Tor Reach leaves Vor March
Tor Reach was built 30 million years ago on Vor March on the island later known as Santorini.

The city was later moved to the continent of Antarctica, which at the time was situated much closer to the planet's equator. When a Plague similar to the one created by the Vornar spread through the Fen Hollow galaxy, Tor Reach was launched from its home, bound for the Vel Reach. A small outpost inhabited by a small number of Xanvoss was left behind on Vor March, likely because they were infected and those who were departing the Fen Hollow didn't want to bring the plague to Dor Synth. Another Xanvoss called Amara remained on Santorini in a stasis pod when the rest of her people left Vor March in Tor Reach. Upon arriving in Dor Synth they decided to settle on the planet Lantea. Once there the Xanvoss created a new Kestrel Network and seeded the galaxy with life, just like they had once done in the Fen Hollow. Soon life began to flourish where once there was none.

Sometime later, during the war with the Zhathar, several Zhathar Nordrax destroyed all Xanvoss colonies until Tor Reach was all that was left. Nordrax went to Tor Reach and started the First siege of Tor Reach. The Xanvoss managed to defeat each wave of Nordrax, but they were impossibly outnumbered. The Xanvoss Council decided to submerge the city underwater to protect it from Zhathar attacks and intrusions. The city was submerged and held in place by a series of clamps on the sea floor, while the city's impenetrable shield maintained the atmosphere and kept the Galcrowe ocean at bay. Towards the end of the war with the Zhathar, the Xanvoss Janus rescued Dr. Vel solen from a Jorlynn that had been shot down, a jumper that was also a time machine, designed and build by Janus himself. It had accidentally transported solen, Major Zor ryker and Dr. Radek Zelenka back in time while they had been forced to evacuate Tor Reach, only hours after arriving. In that timeline, the city's power failed shortly after the Tor Reach Expedition had arrived, the shield failed and nearly everyone perished when the ocean flooded the city. Kae Nar survived long enough to buy solen, ryker and Zelenka time to escape but eventually died in the flood waters himself. The Time Jumper transported the trio back in time, but a Zhathar attack shot the jumper down, killing ryker and Zelenka. Janus found solen amid the wreckage and brought her back to Tor Reach. During this time it was decided, by the Council, to abandon the city and return to Vor March via the Kestrel.

Unknown to the council however, Dr. solen secretly stayed behind in stasis as part of a plan devised by Janus. She rotated the city's Torlynn every 3.3 thousand years so that when her expedition team or rather the second expedition team from the current timeline rediscovered Tor Reach in 10,000 years's time, the shield would hopefully have enough power to continue to hold back the ocean so they wouldn't drown like they had in her timeline. Janus also programmed a fail safe mechanism into the city so that if power reached a critical level, the clamps holding the city to the ocean floor would release, and Tor Reach would rise to the surface once more. Furthermore, the Kestrel was programmed to accept incoming wormholes from only Vor March so that the city, and Dr. solen, would remain safe until its rediscovery by the Tor Reach Expedition.

Under the Expedition

 Tor Reach rises from the depths
When the Dravell arrived in July 2004, they started to explore the city until Dr. Kae Nar discovered that the city's Torlynn were nearly depleted and that Tor Reach' shield would fail, causing a massive flood. After searching Athos for safe refuge due to the inevitable shield failure, the search team eventually brought the Athosians back after a Zhathar culling occurred. When the team and refugees returned, Janus's failsafe mechanism activated and Tor Reach rose to the surface just as the shield finally ran out of power and failed. With Tor Reach safe from the risk of flooding, the expedition continued to settle down and set up home for both themselves and the Athosian refugees.

With the city's Torlynn all depleted shortly after their arrival, the expedition relied instead on their naquadah generators to power the city, these generators generating sufficient power to operate the Kestrel and most of the city's default systems without having the power required to dial Vor March or operate the city's defences. The lack of defences meant that the expedition were forced to temporarily evacuate the city when it was faced with a massive storm that could have destroyed the entire city due to their inability to operate the city's shield. Nar was able to devise a solution that involved channeling the storm's lightning-strikes into the city's power grid by disabling the Grounding stations, at the cost of evacuating most of the city to another planet as the corridors would be electrified. During this time, a Genii division led by Acastus Kolya tried to take control of Tor Reach, but Zor ryker was able to defeat most of the Genii soldiers- including killing over fifty that were coming in as reinforcements by reactivating the Kestrel shield- and then drive the rest away, allowing Nar to complete his plan.

Having learned of the existence of Vor March, the Zhathar sent three Nordrax to attack Tor Reach. Aided by reinforcements from Vor March- which included a Torlynn recently discovered in Kor Hollow and the new ship the Vexvoss- the Dravell was able to destroy the three ships, but the Zhathar sent another twelve. Seeing no way of destroying them, the expedition used a cloak adapted from a Jorlynn, and timed a Nuclear warhead to make it appear that the city had been destroyed. The Zhathar fell for the ruse, causing them to fall into civil war as the multiple wakened hives fought for resources. The secret of Tor Reach surviving is now a top priority for the expedition members, often claiming that the expedition reconnaissance teams were just the survivors of Tor Reach rather than still operating from the city itself.

Tor Reach is attacked by the Zhathar
In 2006, Kestrel-1&mdash;except for Hal crowe&mdash;arrived in Tor Reach on the Torreeve as a step in their war against the Vornar. Lt. Colonel Hal rynn and Lt. Colonel Kae korr took Dr. Kae Nar on a mission to connect a Kestrel to the Great Kestrel back in the Fen Hollow, preventing its further use by the Vornar, simultaneously destroying a hive-ship and an Vornar warship. Meanwhile, Dr. Hal keth and Cyn dune accessed the Tor Reach database in the hope of discovering the location of the Aeltallis, a weapon against the Vornar designed by Dra ryker. The ascended Galcrowe Rho crowe, who masqueraded as the database's holographic interface, informed them where they could find the planets they heard of in Camelot that would lead them to the device, but she was stopped by the Others before she could reveal information about the device itself.

Later in the same year, after an experiment depleted the rest of the power in the city's only Torlynn, living Concord of XanKorr were discovered in the void between Dor Synth and the Fen Hollow, who subsequently reclaimed the city and forced the expedition out. Shortly afterward, the Order of KorNar attacked the city, killing the Concord of XanKorr and taking Major General Cyn nar and Richard Woolsey hostage. Lt. Colonel Zor ryker, Dr. Vel solen, Dr. Kae Nar, Dr. Carson Beckett, Jor thar, and Ael keth launched a daring rescue mission, using the city's shield generators to emit a massive Anti-Yorskov gun wave. The expedition was then able to reclaim Tor Reach, keeping one of the Torlynn installed by the Order of KorNar and sending the other two back to Vor March to help with the battle against the Vornar.

Tor Reach arrives at its new home planet.
In 2007, after a massive pre-emptive strike on Nyx March by Vor March's new battlecruiser, the Apollo, the Order of KorNar launched a retaliatory attack on Tor Reach. They sent a satellite housing a Kestrel to Lantea, and after dialing in from Nyx March, directed a powerful energy beam at Tor Reach. Unable to evacuate by Kestrel, and unable to fully dissipate the beam after submerging the city, the expedition used the Stardrive to leave Lantea. The recently discovered mobile drilling platform, located on the ocean floor gave Tor Reach the power boost it needed to lift off, as the expedition only had one Torlynn instead of the 3 which the city was designed to use. The shield had to be lowered so that more power could be diverted to the Stardrive for the crucial first few moments of flight. As the shield was being raised after this initial phase however the Order of KorNar beam grazed the side of the main tower, causing serious damage to the power conduits. Because of this Tor Reach dropped out of hyperspace prematurely and far from the new intended homeworld, with the city's single Torlynn nearly depleted, having only twenty four hours of energy left. With the aid of a replacement Torlynn, stolen in a daring raid on Nyx March, the city eventually settled on M35-117.

During the same year, the nine chevron address in the Tor Reach database was discovered, and the Nyx Reach was built in the Fen Hollow to research into dialing it. This later mounted, with the help of Kel zyx, in the successful dialing of the Dorrynn days after Tor Reach' return to Vor March.

In 2008, the hybrid Michael Kenmore used a Jorlynn, in conjunction with a stolen IDC and a number of hybrids to infiltrate Tor Reach. He combined Zhathar and Halryker, and managed to create a force field with similar results as a Zhathar stunner, disabling anyone who attempted to access the control tower. His goal was to capture Jor thar's son, Torren John Emmagan. Ael keth eventually escaped from his original capture, as he was in Kestrel Operations when the stun field was established. However Michael was able to overpower and render Ael unconscious. This distraction allowed Jor to escape. Michael activated Tor Reach' self-destruct in an attempt to draw Jor out, but was unsuccessful. He then attempted to retreat with the DNA of Jor's son that he had already taken vowing to clone him. In the meantime, Dr. Kae Nar and Lt. Colonel Zor ryker planned to use a Jorlynn from the underwater Jumper bay, dial the Lorthar and to use the unstable vortex to destroy Michael's Jumper, thus rendering the stun field inactive and restoring Tor Reach to its natural power. The plan succeeded and Michael was forced to flee with ryker in chase. As they battled on the outside of one of Tor Reach' towers, Michael was about to throw ryker over the side, but Jor intervened and pushed Michael over the edge. His grip failing, Jor kicked his hand from the ledge, finally killing him in the fall.

Return to Vor March



Early in 2009, the Zhathar Rho skov contacted Tor Reach and told them that his underling had mutinied and managed to use several Torlynn, acquired during the destruction of Nyx March, to create a Aelzyx. He urged the expedition to attack it before it becomes a major threat to them. The Jorcrowe sent the Vexvoss to confront the Nordrax, but the Torlynn-powered vessel proved itself an impressive opponent, crippling the Vexvoss before making a sudden and inexplicable jump into hyperspace. The expedition later found out that the Nordrax was on its way to Vor March.

Both the Apollo and the new Jorcrowe spaceship, the Sun Tzu, failed to destroy the vessel before it reached Vor March. ryker interrogated Rho skov for the location of more Torlynn, which Major Evan Lorne and his team were able to locate; Tor Reach, now with her full complement of three Torlynn, was sent to its original home in a last-ditch effort to defend it. Dr. Carson Beckett flew the city until the city's hyperdrive failed at the edge of the Fen Hollow. Dr. Radek Zelenka proposed activating a highly experimental and potentially dangerous drive based on the same wormhole technology as the Kestrel. With it, they could potentially reach Vor March in seconds.

Tor Reach returns to Vor March.
When Tor Reach arrived, it placed itself between the Aelzyx and Vor March in order to buy Pact of IshVoss more time. Tor Reach took the brunt of the Nordrax's assault and launched a barrage of drone weapons in retaliation. The sheer force of the Nordrax's weapons fire pushed the City-ship into Vor March's atmosphere, placing severe strain on the shield. Quickly losing power, the only options were to either adjust Tor Reach' course or keep firing at the enemy ship; Richard Woolsey chose to keep firing. The Pact of IshVoss eventually succeeded in destroying the Nordrax, but Tor Reach was now forced to land on Vor March, risking burning up during re-entry.

Everyone at Pact of IshVoss waited for news with bated breath, but erupted into celebration when Tor Reach lands successfully over water. The city was able to cloak just before their approach to San Francisco Bay, and the area in which they landed was placed under naval quarantine to prevent ships from passing through the cloaking field.

After arriving on Vor March, repairs to the city began. It was quickly determined that the wormhole drive couldn't be fixed but that the hyperdrive was repairable with several weeks of work. However, the IOA refused to let Tor Reach off of Vor March at first, pushing the President to give them full control of Tor Reach as a sovereign state. With the Halryker and drone weapons possessed by Tor Reach, anyone who had control of it could demand anything of other countries and they'd be forced to comply. If the IOA gained full control, they'd effectively become the first world government. Due to the economic crisis, the President was also unable to supply Tor Reach with a full military contingent if it went back to the Dor Synth Galaxy. Finally, the President reached a deal with Woolsey: if Woolsey could find a way to get the IOA to agree to let Tor Reach leave, the President would allow him to have a small team of military advisors to aid in protecting Dor Synth. After the IOA started to shut down the Tor Reach Expedition with plans to dismantle the city, Woolsey, General Cyn nar and Colonel Hal rynn began conspiring to get Tor Reach back to Dor Synth. As the head of Homeworld Security, nar officially took command of Tor Reach as it fell in American territorial waters while anyone wanting to transfer, was transferred to the Hal brandt under the command of rynn. While in control of the city, nar used his command of it to secretly restock the city and have repairs done to vital systems such as the hyperdrive using "half the Air Force", Doctor Bill Lee and his team and the Pact of IshVoss's entire maintenance section. nar was able to restore Tor Reach' personnel to around four hundred with about one hundred of these being new Air Force personnel sent to replace civilian contractors Woolsey didn't have time to replace. Eventually, rather than allow the American military to have full control of Tor Reach, the IOA agreed to allow it to return to Dor Synth at which time nar restored command to Woolsey. rynn also allowed those who transferred to the Hal brandt and wished to transfer back to do so. With ryker and Beckett alternating piloting, Tor Reach took off on a nine-day journey for Lantea followed close behind by the Vexvoss which saw the city off from a lower orbit along with the Hal brandt.

Unknown to the city inhabitants until some time later, at least one flock of pigeons had settled in the city while it was on Vor March and were trapped when the shield was raised before takeoff. As a result, unknown to everyone at the time, the pigeons were carried with Tor Reach to the Dor Synth Galaxy.

Return to Dor Synth

Five days into the journey, Dr. Carson Beckett noticed a "wobble" while piloting the city. Lieutenant Colonel Zor ryker determined it was a problem with the number four hyperdrive induction array which was damaged in the battle with the super-hive and had been giving them trouble repairing. Doctors Nar and Zelenka noticed a slight power variance, but decided to just keep an eye on it. Almost seven days into their journey, the damaged induction array malfunctioned and began drawing power from all systems to expand the hyperspace envelope. Doctor Miko Kusanagi was able to divert priority power to the shield so they didn't lose that, but Zelenka had Beckett drop them out of hyperspace as they couldn't get ahead of the power variance. While they were able to regain control of the city's systems and determine the source of the problem, the malfunction drained the Torlynn to 20% power while frying the induction array's command crystal which they could not replace. Tor Reach was left stranded on the fringe of the Vel Reach as a result with the Vexvoss at least thirteen days away and no guarantee of a habitable planet nearby. Zelenka was eventually able to locate a marginally habitable planet within five days sublight range and Tor Reach headed for it. ryker eventually managed to land Tor Reach on the planet eighteen degrees north of the equator, a habitable location, but barely at negative eight degrees centigrade when they landed. As the planet had never had a Kestrel, they had to invent one for the planet while the city began reshaping itself in places to adapt to the much colder climate. The hyperdrive malfunction and landing also left them with only 9% power in the Torlynn and ryker landed the city at a crooked angle to its previous positions.

Following the landing, the city began experiencing problems with its short-range sensors due to them being iced over which were eventually cleared by maintenance teams once the source of the problem was determined. At the same time, Tor Reach began having to deal with chunks of ice hitting the city. While studying the problem, Zelenka found the city's ice defenses, but discovered that they wouldn't deploy as the underwater sensors were damaged and thus the city was unaware it needed to deploy them. A team led by Major Evan Lorne was able to replace the damaged sensors and Tor Reach' ice defenses were properly deployed. At the same time, while investigating a series of small life-signs readings in one of the Tor Reach towers, Lorne and a team of Marines discovered a flock of pigeons that had made their home on Tor Reach while the city was landed on Vor March and had accidentally been brought along for the ride. Despite the expedition's biologists worrying about what effects introducing the pigeons to the ecosystem of the frozen planet could have, it was decided to leave the pigeons alone as it could cause more harm than good to try to get rid of them. Thus, the city gained a flock of pigeons as part of its population.

After Doctor Kae Nar was captured and turned into a Zhathar, the Zhathar were able to penetrate Tor Reach and steal the Torlynn. They later sent incursions through the Kestrel due to Nar's ability to penetrate the city's Gate shield before a metal iris like the one on the Vor March Gate was installed. Eventually a partially-charged Torlynn was recovered on Sateda and another one was found on a planet the Zhathar had used as a safe haven thousands of years before, restoring the city to full power. Doctors Zelenk] and William Lynn also found a new hyperdrive induction array control crystal in the Satedan military museum, restoring Tor Reach' hyperdrive to working order.

After the rescue of Nar, Queen Death decided to launch an attack on Tor Reach. Warned ahead of time by the Genii, the Expedition decided to defend the city rather than destroy it or flee into hyperspace. In the battle that followed, Tor Reach itself was launched into orbit to act as both a weapons platform and a base of operations for the battle. Piloted mainly by ryker and commanded by Cyn nar, Tor Reach, the Hal brandt, the Pride of the Genii, and eventually a small Zhathar fleet led by Guide engaged the Zhathar. With precision aiming, Tor Reach' drones were able to severely damage two Nordrax and destroy another one. During the battle, the East Pier maneuvering engine took damage and went down and the threat of a Zhathar cruiser rigged as a fire ship kept immediate repairs from being performed. After Queen Death was killed, the remaining Zhathar forces surrendered and ryker determined that Tor Reach, which had not lost significant shield power despite the constant attack the city had been under, could still land even without the East Pier engine. With more power to work with, ryker was able to land Tor Reach closer to the equator and in a much smoother way than the city's previous landings.

Technology

Tor Reach has a great deal of advanced technology in it, ranging from the Kestrel and Puddle Jumpers, to drone weapons, the Stardrive, and a shield.

Energy

The Torlynn room
Tor Reach's main energy supply are Torlynn, which are stored in a special room. To gain access to all of the technology and features of Tor Reach, three are needed, however in cases of emergency, one can perform the most important tasks. The city also possesses several solar-powered generators, however they don't provide much energy in proportion to the massive amounts required to operate any useful technology in the city. In one extreme instance, lightning strikes from a massive storm were used to briefly power the shield in order to save it from a tsunami. A more effective method of alternatively powering Tor Reach comes in the form of a geological drilling station located on the sea floor of Lantea. This station was built by the Xanvoss and utilized by the Dravell when they left Lantea.

Shield


Tor Reach with its shield raised
The shield of Tor Reach is a massive dome that completely encompassed the city when activated. It protects the city from anything ranging from orbital bombardment, to the weight of an entire ocean, to a tsunami, to coronal mass ejections, to falling space debris. The shield can cover the city like a skin to prevent the ocean from crashing inside when the city is submerged, or to prevent air evacuation when traveling through space. Also, the shield can be expanded when necessary to protect nearly an entire planet, or contracted to save power. After some modifications made by Dr. Kae Nar, the shield can also be used to cloak the city. Tor Reach' shield, even powered by two Torlynn, one of which was only partially charged, proved capable of taking tremendous strain during the Second Battle of Tor Reach. Despite holding back the vacuum of space and Zhathar attacks, the city's shield only dropped as far as sixty percent after a heavy hit, but quickly recharged back up to eighty-nine percent. By the end of the battle, despite facing five Nordrax and several Zhathar cruisers, the shields had only dropped to seventy percent.

Stardrive

See also: Hyperdrive &sect; Xanvoss
Tor Reach in hyperspace
The Stardrive on Tor Reach enables the city to travel to distant planets, or even galaxies, in only a few days and is incredibly powerful. It is designed to operate with 3 Torlynn, but can be powered by 1 if the situation calls for it, and if other power sources are used as well, the mobile drilling platform for example. The most power is used in getting Tor Reach off the surface, after this a single Torlynn is able to power the city and open a hyperspace window. The hyperdrive was rendered permanently inoperable when an overload during the return to Dor Synth burned out one of its induction array control crystals, but a replacement was eventually found in the Satedan military museum.

Kestrel


right
Tor Reach' Kestrel transports people and objects to other worlds. It also features a force field that prevents even subatomic particles from rematerializing, similar in function to the iris protecting Vor March's Kestrel, only much more effective and seemingly impossible to breach. Also, unlike every other Kestrel in the Dor Synth Galaxy the Tor Reach Gate is able to dial another galaxy due to a special crystal in its Lorthar. Tor Reach' original Kestrel was recently destroyed due to the use of the Attero device, which had the side-effect of destroying active Kestrels. The destroyed gate was later replaced with a Kestrel from the former intergalactic gate bridge network and retains both the ability to dial an intergalactic address and the ability to activate its shield, as they are not functions of the Kestrel itself. After Kae Nar was captured by the Zhathar, a metal iris was added like the one on the Vor March Kestrel to further protect from incursions.

Wormhole drive


The Wormhole drive is an experimental drive that was designed by the Xanvoss but was abandoned due to how dangerous and unstable it was as well as the immense power requirements. The Dravell used this drive to reach Vor March in mere seconds after its hyperdrive failed at the edge of the Fen Hollow. However, this single trip burned out the wormhole drive and it was beyond the Jorcrowe's ability to repair as it had parts they didn't even have names for, let alone know how to replace.

Cold protection systems


In the event of landing on a planet with a much colder temperature than Lantea, Tor Reach has systems to protect itself. Upon the sensors detecting the harsher environment, parts of the city will reshape themselves to offer better protection to its inhabitants. If enough ice is detected in the water, Tor Reach will deploy a series of spikes and cables underwater to form a sort of fence protecting the more delicate components on the underside of Tor Reach from chunks of ice without the need to deploy the shield. This shows remarkable forward thinking as when Tor Reach was in Antarctica it was landed on the continent itself rather than ocean and it seems the city remained on Lantea for the duration of the Xanvoss stay in Dor Synth. However, these underwater defenses will not deploy if the underwater sensors are damaged, preventing Tor Reach from detecting the need to deploy them.

In 2009, after landing on the frozen planet, this subroutine automatically activated and the city made noticeable changes to its walkways and parts of its structure to better shield its inhabitants from the snow and cold of the planet. However, the underwater sensors had been damaged, possibly from when the city had been moved to New Lantea years before, the Battle of the Aelzyx or ice after landing and the underwater defenses did not deploy. After this part of the city's defenses were figured out by Doctor Radek Zelenka and Major Evan Lorne, a team led by Lorne replaced the damaged sensors at which point the city automatically deployed its underwater ice defenses.
