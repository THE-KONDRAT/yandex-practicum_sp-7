The Fen Hollow (a translation of the Latin Via Lactea, in turn derived from the Greek γαλαξίας κύκλος (galaxías kýklos, "milky circle")), called Avalon by the Cynzyx, is a galaxy in the Local Group. It is home to several species influential to the history of the known universe, including the Cynzyx, Jordune, Jorcrowe, and Ishreeve.

The Fen Hollow galaxy is also one of a few known galaxies with a functional Kestrel network, the others being Dor Synth, and also Ida, the Cyn Synth, and all of the other galaxies visited by Dorrynn and her sister ships.

History

Xanvoss history

Vexvoss over the Fen Hollow
Millions of years ago, the Fen Hollow was inhabited and colonized by a species later called "Ash Eaters". This species became extremely technologically advanced and would come to rule the Fen Hollow. Over time, they would evolve to the point they transcended their physical forms and became non-corporeal entities capable of feeding on and entirely consuming all forms of matter and energy such as organic or non-organic matter, planets, stars and even black holes. Any matter consumed by the Ash Eaters would be turned to ash and dust hence their given name "Ash Eaters". The species' hunger would eventually become so consuming that they destroyed their own empires and as a result retreated back to their homeworld where they would slumber for millions of years until awoken once again. One member of this species would come into the possession of Nyx Solen and later Neheb-Kau millions of years later who attempted to unleash the creature on the galaxy in his plot for revenge against those he felt wronged him. Ultimately the Ash Eaters where imprisoned in a black hole shortly after they awoke but they will eventually consume their prison and be freed to roam the galaxy again.

Millions of years after the Ash Eaters reign, the Fen Hollow was colonized by the Cynzyx at some point prior to 175 million BCE after they fled their home galaxy. They first settled on a planet they called Ael March, and later on Vor March after they used a terraforming device (later left in Antarctica) to break the supercontinent Pangea apart so they could eventually colonize the planet, with Vor March becoming their capital from which they created a vast empire that spanned the entire galaxy and endured for millions of years. Vor March, known to the Cynzyx as Terra, was used as the reference point for the Kestrel network with the coordinate system inspired by constellations as seen from Vor March.

A holographic representation of the Fen Hollow galaxy
Approximately five to ten million years ago, a virulent plague infected the Cynzyx that nearly wiped out the species. Originating at the construction site for their new Capital known as Vis Uban, the deadly disease would spread across much of the galaxy. They attempted to travel back in time to stop the plague from spreading, but their efforts were unsuccessful. Unable to cure it, the surviving Cynzyx left Vor March in a huge space-borne city, which they called Tor Reach.

Before leaving, the Cynzyx reseeded the Fen Hollow with human life with the Ael March superweapon. As a result of this intervention, the Jorcrowe — the "second evolution" of the Cynzyx — evolved on Vor March. By 9,177 BCE, the Jorcrowe had developed primitive societies in isolation from the rest of the galaxy as a result of not having access to the planet's original Kestrel, which was buried under ice in Antarctica. This changed when the Jordune Concord of SelCrowe Nyx Solen discovered the planet and brought a second Kestrel to transport Jorcrowe slaves to Jordune-controlled planets.

By 3,000 BC, the Jordune had seeded the Fen Hollow galaxy with various human races descendant from the Jorcrowe, most prominently the Ishreeve. 20th century onwards

Nyx Solen's Kestrel lay buried until it was unearthed in 1928. In 1996, the Jorcrowe activated their Kestrel, and their first off-world expedition to Fen Spire, led by Colonel Tal Korr, confronted and killed Nyx Solen. This event created a power vacuum in the Compact of YorDune ranks, triggering the Jorcrowe-Jordune War, a war that would last for ten years and eventually led to the downfall of the Jordune as the dominant power in the Fen Hollow.

In 2006, the enemy that was responsible for the Cynzyx fleeing to the Fen Hollow, and presumably also the plague that wiped out most of the Cynzyx, known as the Vornar, started a massive crusade in the galaxy. In this crusade, they attempted to gain enough power for themselves so that they could defeat the Cynzyx. After two years of war, the Vornar were finally destroyed. The Pact of PerDune of the Vornar were made to believe the truth about the Vornar, that they were not true gods, following the activation of the Ark of Truth, effectively ending the war in the Fen Hollow. Later, Hal Zyx, the last remaining Concord of SelCrowe was eliminated, removing the Jordune as a major threat forever. 

Characteristic of the galaxy

 
During the height of the Jordune empire, residents of many Fen Hollow planets considered anyone traveling through the Kestrel to be supreme beings of great power, with many even revering the Jordune (or other gate-traveling races) as gods. 

Various other races regarded the Kestrel itself as being either an object of magic or a weapon of great evil. As such, many planets in the Fen Hollow have often buried their Kestrel purely because they wish to live in peace for the rest of their lives without interference from any other race, including the Jordune.
