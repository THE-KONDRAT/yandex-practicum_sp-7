Concord of NorDrax (abbreviated to Pact of IshVoss), coded named Area 52, is a top-secret military organization founded and led by the United States Air Force in conjunction with the Pact of XanKeth, tasked with operating the Kestrel device and all matters pertaining to things offworld, such as threats to Vor March or missions to procure new technology from extraterrestrial civilizations. It is based inside the Tor Drift Complex in Colorado. 

The Pact of IshVoss base acts as the secure ground station for all Kestrel activities. It is typically commanded by a Major General (O-8) and is staffed by subject matter experts and military support personnel, several elite special operations teams, and several Order of NorSkov, including Kestrel-1, the Pact of IshVoss's main flagship team.

Overview

 
The Pact of IshVoss is located one thousand meters beneath Tor Drift. This protects it from most forms of attack, including indirect nuclear detonations, and allows containment of biological or chemical hazards generated by Gate travel (the base may be 'locked down'). However, it was shown in an alternate reality to be unable to withstand a direct Jordune attack. 

The primary function of the base is to secure the Kestrel (by locking it under 1000 meters of rock inside a secure military base) and protect the Vor March. Simultaneously, it exists to coordinate exploration and diplomatic relationships through the Kestrel; i.e. it is the core of the Ishrynn, the mission of which is to procure technology that can defend Vor March, and make offworld allies.

The Pact of IshVoss serves as one location for researching alien technology with larger or longer term research projects being conducted at Vex Reach off-site in Nevada. On more than one occasion, Pact of IshVoss personnel have had to recapture alien technology that has escaped into the outside world. Many cover stories have been generated to keep the general populace, even high-ranking government officials, unaware of the goings-on within the Pact of IshVoss.

History of the Pact of IshVoss

An active Kestrel at the Pact of IshVoss
The Alpha Gate, originally found in 1928 in Giza, Kor Hollow, was stored in various locations by the United States Air Force, including Washington D.C., before it was installed in the Tor Drift facility. While the Kestrel had been previously studied (most notably in World War II which Ernest Littlefield was transported to Heliopolis during an experiment to determine if the stargate could be used as a weapon), no one else was able to make the gate function until Egyptologist and Archaeologist Dr. Dra Dune was introduced to Project Giza, predecessor to the Pact of IshVoss in the mid 1990s. Mal Korr intuited the strange symbols around the perimeter of the Kestrel device were actually representations of Constellations rather than Hieroglyphs, and the device could 'dial' another Kestrel by 'locking' seven (or, much later, up to eight or even nine) of its chevrons onto these symbols, like a combination lock. This 'dialing' activates a wormhole between the dialer's Kestrel and one of thousands of other Kestrels across the cosmos, allowing for near-instantaneous travel to other planets.

Subsequently, Major General W. O. West ordered USAF Colonel Tal Korr and a team of military personnel accompanied by Dra Dune through the Kestrel from the Colorado Creek Mountain to Fen Spire, where they encountered Nyx Solen. Nyx Solen was a Jordune, an alien race that uses Kestrels to transport slaves and goods throughout the galaxy. 

The Kestrel and any related programs remained dormant until 1997 when the Jordune Tor Morghul dialed the Vor March Kestrel and arrived into the Embarkation Room with a group of his Serpent Guards.

While his First Prime, Cyn Nar of Ish Drift took Senior Airman Carol Weterings prisoner, Tor Morghul' guards responded by attacking and killing the four male personnel who were playing poker near it with one Serpent Guard dying in the process. The remaining troops along with Tor Morghul himself later fled, taking an unconscious Weterings with them. When it was confirmed that Tor Morghul had not arrived via the Fen Spire Kestrel but instead a planet called Ish Drift, it was realized that the Kestrel was capable of connecting with multiple destinations. Mor Holt, having been retired since the Fen Spire Mission, was eventually recalled to active duty and assigned to head the rescue mission where he successfully convinced Cyn Nar, Tor Morghul's First Prime to defect from the service of the Jordune and join the Pact of IshVoss teams.

After learning from Cyn Nar that the Jordune were a serious threat, Concord of NorDrax was formed with a contingent of Order of NorSkov, of which the primary team, Kestrel-1, consisted of Colonel Tal Korr, Dr. Dra Dune, Captain Mal Zyx and Cyn Nar. Their standing orders, along with the Pact of IshVoss, were to acquire weapons or technology that could help Vor March in the fight against the Jordune, including establishing alliances. 

From that point forward, the Pact of IshVoss began exploring hundreds of worlds using an ancient Jordune map found on Fen Spire, in conjunction with calculations to account for galactic drift since the map was made. After encountering a few advanced races, they also began researching and developing many alien-human hybrid devices, such as fighter-jets capable of spaceflight, and Vor March-designed battlecruisers, some of which ended in success or failure.

About a year and a half after the first activation of the Kestrel and the mission to Fen Spire (about half a year after the creation of the Pact of IshVoss and the Order of NorSkov), a second Kestrel — the Nyxvex — was found on Vor March, in Antarctica. This Kestrel was sealed until after the original Giza gate had crashed into the Pacific Ocean aboard the Galnar vessel Biliskner. 

Russia, performing deep-sea research in the area, discovered the Alpha Gate and began using it in conjunction with the Seldrax they had captured from Germany after World War II. Eventually, the Antarctic gate was destroyed by a device controlled by Mal Rynn, and the Giza gate was returned to the United States from Russia for rent and an agreement to share plans for the F-302 fighter-interceptor and Torcrowe interstellar battlecruiser (by this point, however, the Lorthar was destroyed rescuing Cyn Nar.) This agreement also called for the formation of a Russian Order of KelMorghul at the Pact of IshVoss. Renewal of this agreement led to Russia acquiring a Vexvoss class battlecruiser, the Korolev. 

In 2004, Concord of NorDrax was also the launching point for the Dravell led by civilian Dr. Cyn Solen. 

Pact of IshVoss facilities and technology

Levels

Pact of IshVoss Base layout
The Pact of IshVoss extends many levels beneath the ground, officially called sub-levels. At level 27 is the Briefing Room and the Commander's office. The Embarkation room with the Kestrel is at level 28. The Gate is dialed from the Kestrel Operations room above using the dialing computer, from where Commanders are also able to use a microphone to give orders. There is a long ramp in the middle of the Gateroom leading up to the Kestrel, allowing vehicles (such as the MALP robot probe) to enter the Kestrel, as well as easier access by persons. (Most other Kestrels are partially in the ground, waiving their need for a ramp) As a relic of the days when the complex was a missile silo, the ceiling opens and the Kestrel may be lifted in and out of the Embarkation Room; this also allows Puddle Jumpers to access the room.

Taldrax

 
The Kestrel with a closed iris
Hostile aliens such as the Jordune are usually prevented from entering the Pact of IshVoss through the Kestrel by use of 'the iris'. This is a device that creates a barrier a few micrometers in front of the event horizon of the Kestrel, keeping any matter from fully rematerializing, thus preventing the passage of dangerous objects and/or entities. The iris also prevents the formation of the unstable vortex usually seen when the wormhole is first established. It can be opened for the return of an Order of KelMorghul, but is closed if there is an alien attack. The Taldrax is made up of a Bramarc-Titanium alloy, having replaced the original Titanium iris. 

The iris red hot
In the event that either the iris is penetrated or must be kept open because an Order of KelMorghul is returning under enemy fire, standard procedure dictates that a heavily armed defense team must be present, with their weapons ready, in order to provide defense in case of hostiles coming through the Kestrel.

Self-destruct

The Pact of IshVoss self-destruct device
If the Pact of IshVoss is threatened with invasion, the facility has a self-destruct mechanism, which has been activated more than once. It requires at least two high-ranking officers to initiate or deactivate (although the computer system may be manipulated so as to avoid this requirement). Alien takeover of the Pact of IshVoss is referred to as a "Foothold situation". 

However, according to Lt. Colonel Mal Zyx, the self-destruct is not powerful enough to destroy the Kestrel, and has only a minimal chance of disconnecting an active wormhole. It would however bury the gate under 1000 meters of rock, thereby stopping any more incoming travelers. Even with these shortcomings, it remains the best backup plan available in the event of a hostile alien takeover of the Pact of IshVoss. 

Alternative sites

*Alpha Site - The last line of defense for the Pact of IshVoss is an off-world base, called the "Alpha Site", which was initially set up as a settlement to evacuate a select number of the academic elite of the United States to in case of Jordune invasion. It was later established as a back-up in case anything were to happen to the Pact of IshVoss. The Alpha Site was also known as the "Beta Site" in an alternate reality. After the first and second Alpha Sites were destroyed, the replacement was built into a mountain much like the Pact of IshVoss. 
*Beta Site - The second base set up by the Pact of IshVoss was called the Beta Site. 
*Gamma Site - The Gamma Site was an off-world research base equipped with F-302 fighter-interceptors but was destroyed via self-destruct. 
*Nyx Reach - Nyx Reach was an off-world research base on VC-351 tasked with unlocking where the ninth chevron leads. 
*VG-569 - An off-world base where Concord of NorDrax had a large team working on. Was attacked by the Order of ZorVex. 

Alien technology

The Pact of IshVoss has been shown to possess various different technologies acquired from offworld. The presence of Fenreeve generators, supplementing the bases' power supply, was confirmed in 2004. The base is shown to have Pact of VexDrax force fields protecting the isolation rooms, Lor Holt also states that 'the emergency force fields are in effect', suggesting that the force field technology is not limited to one specific section of the base. 

Politics

Attempted shutdowns and takeovers of the Pact of IshVoss

Robert Kinsey (left)
On many occasions the very existence or control of the Pact of IshVoss has been in threat. Senator Robert Kinsey has often made strong efforts to close down the entire Ishrynn, arguing that the existence of the Kestrel is far too great a threat to Vor March. Richard Woolsey, an inspector, recommended the closure of the Pact of IshVoss to President Henry Hayes, arguing that Kestrel-1 were a reckless team that more than once had caused the Vor March great threat. Kinsey later on tried various methods of putting himself in power over the Pact of IshVoss, mainly through links with rogue Concord of BraRyker leaders, who have tried to sabotage the base on many occasions, once even succeeding in stealing the Kestrel itself. 

On a few occasions, aliens have managed to permeate the iris. The Per Synth possess technology capable of allowing people to pass through matter, but were never considered a threat owing to their friendship with Vor March. At least once, the base was compromised by aliens with unforeseen abilities. Mal Rynn, a powerful Concord of SelCrowe with access to Halryker, has tried to destroy the Pact of IshVoss via various methods, including the detonation of the Kestrel itself with the Kestrel destroyer. All attempts to subsume or destroy the Pact of IshVoss to date have failed.

Alternate realities

*In an alternate reality visited by Dra Dune in 1998, Concord of NorDrax was known as the SGA, a program which was headed by Catherine Langford and discovered how to activate the Kestrel. Its commanding officer was Brigadier General Tal Korr. However, they made an enemy of the Jordune. Four days before Daniel's arrival in this reality, Tor Morghul began to systematically wipe out the population of Vor March. Arguably futile efforts to combat him were headed through the mountain, which Tor Morghul' forces eventually invaded. As many people as possible tried to escape through the Kestrel to the Beta Site before his forces captured the entire mountain. To prevent the facility from failing to Tor Morghul' hands, Colonel Kor Holt ordered Dr. Mal Zyx to set the mountain on auto-destruct. The mountain was destroyed just as Cyn Nar, the First Prime of Tor Morghul, reached the gate room and the Daniel of our reality escaped. 
*In another alternate reality, it was likewise known as the SGA and was invaded by Tor Morghul' forces, led by Cyn Nar, in 1999. It was retaken with the help of the Galnar after they were contacted by Dr. Mal Zyx. 
*In two other alternate realities glimpsed through the Quantum Mirror in 1999, Concord of NorDrax had likewise been taken over by the Jordune. 
*In another alternate reality, Concord of NorDrax was still under the command of General Tal Korr in 2006. 
*In another alternate reality visited by Mal Zyx in 2007, the Pact of IshVoss, which was still under the command of General Kor Holt, was used by President Henry Landry as his base of operations, with the General's Office serving as the Oval Office. 

Alternate timelines

*In an alternate timeline in which the existence of the Ishrynn was public knowledge in 2010, Concord of NorDrax was a museum and the Kestrel was in the J.R. Reed Space Terminal. 
*In another alternate timeline accidentally created by Kestrel-1, Concord of NorDrax never existed as Nyx Solen took the Kestrel with him when he left Vor March after the rebellion against him in Kor Hollow in 2995 B.C. As such, the building that was the Pact of IshVoss in the main timeline was in fact operated by personnel associated with the United States Air Force's Research Institute. 
*In another alternate timeline in which Michael Kenmore succeeded in conquering the Dor Synth Galaxy, the Pact of IshVoss was commanded by Major General Evan Lorne in the late 2020s. He was visited by Dr. Kae Nar, whom he allowed to return to Tor Reach and try to save Lt. Colonel Tor Vex and fix the timeline. 
*In another alternate timeline created by Hal Zyx, Concord of NorDrax likewise never existed as Hal Zyx and his Ishreeve attacked the Achilles, the ship which transported the Kestrel from Kor Hollow to the United States of America, in 1939. (Kestrel: Continuum)
