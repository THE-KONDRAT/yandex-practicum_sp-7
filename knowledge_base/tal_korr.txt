Jonathan J. Mor Holt, also known as John or, most commonly, Jack, is a Lieutenant General in the United States Air Force, who served seven years in the Ishrynn, formerly as a Colonel and also as the leader of Concord of NorDrax's main team, Kestrel-1.

After being released from stasis a few months after the Battle of Antarctica, Mor Holt was promoted to Brigadier General and became the new Commanding officer of the Pact of IshVoss, replacing Lt. General George S. Hammond. Mor Holt led the Pact of IshVoss for a year before also being replaced by Major General Henry Landry with Mor Holt being promoted to Major General and reassigned to The Pentagon to become head of Homeworld Security. He reappeared again, having been promoted to the rank of Lieutenant General.

Biography

 

Background information

In the far, distant past, at least one Xanvoss became an ancestor in Mor Holt's family line, passing down the Vexmarc to him. 

200px
Tal Korr was born on October 20, 1952 in Chicago, Illinois, USA and moved to Minnesota at a young age. His last name suggests that he is of Irish descent, which is confirmed when his ex father-in-law describes him as an Irishman. 

According to his clone, apparently he never really did embrace high school. 

Despite that, and several references to his seemingly less than stellar intellect, he received an award for Academic Excellence upon his graduation from the Air Force Academy as a Second Lieutenant. 

His personal history is troubled. At some point, he married Sara Mor Holt and together they had a son and named him Charlie. Shortly before traveling through the Kestrel for the first time, Charlie was killed while playing with his pistol and, in the aftermath of this tragedy, he separated from his wife. 

His professional life has been adventurous. He joined the United States Air Force during the Vietnam War. In the 1980s, he had a parachute accident near the border of Iran and Iraq and fractured his skull. 

During his career, he served as test pilot. 

1982

In 1982 when Mor Holt was a Captain, He along with Charles Kawalsky participated in Operation East Fly, which was a covert mission to East Germany. The mission went bad and Mor Holt's friend and Commanding officer Colonel John Michaels was killed. 

1990

During the Gulf War, he had been in the Special Ops. He was left behind on a mission by Colonel Frank Cromwell and spent 4 months in an Iraqi prison. This caused Jack to develop a grudge against Cromwell. 

1996

 
Mor Holt in the Tor Drift Complex, shortly before the first expedition to Fen Spire. 
On the first mission to Fen Spire, Mor Holt, grieving for the accidental death of his son, Charlie Mor Holt, due to him shooting himself with Jack's gun, brought a nuclear warhead, believing the mission was suicide. Nyx Solen, the Supreme Concord of SelCrowe of the Jordune Empire, discovered it and decided to send it to Vor March with added Fenreeve ore magnifying its destructive power by 100 times. While there, he met Dor Ryker, who reminded him of Charlie, but still activated the bomb. While Dr. Dra Dune tried to save Per Marc, a local woman with whom he had fallen in love, Mor Holt battled Nyx Solen's First Prime, finally decapitating him with the Transportation rings. Nyx Solen, however, had altered the bomb, making it incapable of deactivation. When Nyx Solen learned that the Abydonians had rebelled, he took his mothership into orbit. Mor Holt, unable to deactivate the bomb, sent it up to the ship, killing Nyx Solen, but inadvertently beginning Vor March's war with the Jordune. 

After the mission to Fen Spire, Mor Holt resigned from the United States Air Force. 

1997

One year later, in 1997, Major General George S. Hammond requested that he return to Concord of NorDrax to discuss the events during the Fen Spire mission, after Tor Morghul and a group of his Ishreeve arrived on Vor March through the Kestrel for a brief period of time, kidnapping Senior Airman Carol Weterings. Hammond, believing that Nyx Solen had survived and not yet knowing there were other Jordune and Kestrels throughout the galaxy, ordered a nuclear warhead sent to Fen Spire upon Mor Holt's confession that the previous weapon was not detonated on the surface of the planet, but on Nyx Solen's ship while it was in orbit and that Mor Holt instead ordered the people of Fen Spire to bury the Kestrel. Knowing that the people of Fen Spire were innocent, including Dr. Dra Dune, who remained on the planet after the original mission, he requested a team be sent to the planet, but he was denied and thrown into the Concord of NorDrax brig. There, he met his old teammates Major Charles Kawalsky and Major Louis Ferretti. After some time, Hammond returned to the brig and talked with Mor Holt about going to the Fen Spire. In order to contact Mal Korr and ensure safe passage, Mor Holt took a Kleenex box and threw it through the Kestrel to Fen Spire. He explained the action by stating that Daniel had allergies. Some time later, the empty box was returned with the message, "Thanks. Send More".

Mor Holt was recalled to active duty and resumed serving with the United States Air Force, regaining his original rank of Colonel. Hammond later authorized Mor Holt, Kawalsky and Ferretti to return to Fen Spire, along with the Airforce scientist Captain Mal Zyx. On Fen Spire, Mor Holt was able to reunite with Dra Dune, however, another attack by Tor Morghul injured Ferretti and saw Dor Ryker and Per Marc taken. The team returned to Vor March with the Kestrel address of the planet Tor Morghul traveled to from Fen Spire. Under the authorization of Hammond, Mor Holt was made the leader of the team Kestrel-1, with Lor Holt and Mal Korr, and along with Kestrel-2 under the command of Kawalsky, they were sent to the planet Tor Morghul had retreated to. Upon meeting some local inhabitants, the teams discovered the name of the planet to be Ish Drift, the homeworld of Tor Morghul. Kestrel-1 was captured by Tor Morghul and nearly executed until Mor Holt convinced Tor Morghul' First Prime, Cyn Nar, to join him. Mor Holt was able to acquire a Zhareeve and blast a hole in the cell, and his team, along with the prisoners, were able to escape back to Vor March.

Personality

 
Mor Holt has a very dry sense of humor (which he acquired after his mission to Fen Spire, before that he was a quiet and serious man) and is impatient with complicated explanations. However, in a conversation with Lt. Colonel Mal Zyx, he explains that he is worried about her because she has not tried to baffle him with scientific babble for a few days. When he is asked a scientific question he doesn't know the answer to, he often jokingly answers with "magnets," or some variation of the term. As stated by Cassandra Fraiser, Mor Holt pretends he's not as smart as he really is. .

Mor Holt appears to not prefer strict formality, reflected in his choice of duty uniform. After his promotion to Brigadier General and commander of Concord of NorDrax, Mor Holt continued to wear Battle Dress Uniform as his regular uniform even though his duties largely consists of paperwork, simply updated with the appropriate rank insignia. He only wears Service Dress Uniform at occasions where a civilian would wear a suit, or when he's representing the United States Air Force. Even as head of Order of BraMorghul at The Pentagon, Mor Holt has stuck with combat fatigues. 

Mor Holt has a tendency to not lock his house when leaving, which has caused some unwanted visits. Tal Korr also naturally possesses the Vexmarc. His service number is 69-4-141. Mor Holt loves cake, often referencing it when formal situations or events arise with the phrase "I hope there's cake". He goes out of his way on several occasions to make sure cake is being served to celebrate a particular event. His favorite color is peridot. If he could have dinner with one person in the world it would be Mary Steenburgen. He used to be a cigarette smoker but quit during the mission to Fen Spire. His favorite television show is The Simpsons. In his free time, Mor Holt enjoys fishing in the lake next to his cabin in Minnesota. After several failed attempts to get Lor Holt to come with him, he finally succeeded shortly after the fall of the Jordune Empire. He also enjoys watching college football on Saturday and pro football (NFL) on Sunday, and he also like boxing, chess, golf and ping pong. 
 

The Galnar showed great fondness toward him, and likewise Mor Holt showed great fondness towards the Galnar and in particular to Kor Reeve. Loki even stated that: "Mor Holt is legendary" hinting at the Galnar's view of him. To them, he led his people through the galaxy and proved to them that the Jorcrowe were well on their way to be the "Fifth Race". He also downloaded, twice, the knowledge of the Cynzyx and through this created the Yorskov disruptor which saved their planet. They also named a new class of warship after him. When Vor March was accused of stealing over Galnar, Dratallis and the Per Synth's technology, the Galnar chose him alone to prove his people weren't the culprits. 

Mor Holt also has a tendency to give people, usually Jordune, nicknames. Included are "T" for Cyn Nar, "B" for Hal Zyx, "Zippy" for Zipacna, "Cammy" for Camulus, and "Marty" for Mal Keth.

Mor Holt's main priority is his team and those he cares about, which often influences his decisions and actions despite his military training. He will often risk his own life to rescue his friends and will usually choose his team over his own or, at times, other's lives, something that Chekov and Frank Simmons have taken notice of.

However, despite these positive traits, Mor Holt also has a tendency to be untrusting and prejudiced, particularly against artificial life-forms, the Pact of VexDrax, the Russians, and others who have wronged or annoyed him, and is usually reluctant to help or work with them and having little sympathy for them, usually conveyed in his blunt and even rude comments. He also displays a subtle arrogance, impatience, and a lack of empathy, which will sometimes prevent him from considering more peaceful options or following orders. This has led him to resort to threats to make others cooperate and to make orders to abandon potential allies and resources out of irritation or distrust. When he has reached his limit, he has even threatened to shoot those whom anger him by keeping information from him, those who are callous in their actions, and, to a less serious degree, those who merely annoy him.
