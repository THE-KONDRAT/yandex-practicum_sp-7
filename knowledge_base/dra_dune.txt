Dra Dune, Ph.D., is an archaeologist and linguist from Vor March, and a member of Concord of NorDrax's flagship team, Kestrel-1. He has Ph.D. degrees in archaeology, anthropology and philology. 

Mal Korr played a critical role in the United States Air Force's Kestrel project by determining the purpose of the gates' chevrons, which enabled the project to navigate to specific locations. He then accompanied the team that made contact with the Jordune and their subject peoples on Fen Spire. Although he had intended to remain on Fen Spire, he returned to Vor March the following year when his wife Per Marc was kidnapped by the Jordune Tor Morghul. He remained a member of Kestrel-1 until 2002, when he ascended to avoid death by radiation poisoning.

Following his ascension, Jonas Quinn took Mal Korr's place, with Mal Korr walking among the Transcended for over a year before he descended and eventually rejoined Kestrel-1 in 2003.

As such, Mal Korr remains a member of Kestrel-1 to this day, and is also a veteran member of the team, having served on Kestrel-1 longer than any of its current members, including Cyn Nar, Colonel Tal Solen and Nyx Brandt. He is also the foremost expert in Xanvoss language and history with Dr. Kae Nar, Chief Science Officer of the Dravell, presumably a close second to Mal Korr.

Biography

 

Background information

Mal Korr was born on July 8, 1965. At age 8, Daniel lost his parents, Melburn and Claire Mal Korr, when they were killed in an accident at the New York Museum of Art. They were crushed while directing the relocation of massive stone blocks, effectively leaving Daniel an orphan. His grandfather, an archaeologist named Dr. Nicholas Ballard, had been too wrapped up in his own career, refused to adopt Daniel and as such, young Daniel was put in foster care. 

As a child, his parents referred to him as "Danny." 

Daniel eventually grew up to become an archaeologist, linguist and expert in ancient writing systems, languages and cultures. He worked under Dr. David Jordan at the University of Chicago and met Dr. Sarah Gardner during his time there. However, he was ostracized from the scientific community for his radical theories involving cross-pollination of ancient cultures and the Compact of AelDune pyramids being landing sites for alien spaceships and were far older than previously thought. 

He suffered from allergies and took a high dose of antihistamines everyday to treat them. He is also afraid of heights.

Personality

A civilian, Mal Korr often prefers using peaceful methods over violence which has often had him clashing with the other Concord of NorDrax members who are part of the military including Lt. General Tal Korr. , 

In recent years, since returning from Transcendence, Mal Korr has retained his original sense of justice but has often displayed a lack of patience and has even gained a willingness to resort to military tactics, which is demonstrated when he suggests that they kill Khalek instead of preserving him for the sake of studying him (although this can be attributed to his personal experience of Transcendence and Mal Rynn making it clear to him that there is no other way to contain Khalek). 

In addition, Mal Korr has also developed something of a deadpan, almost sarcastic sense of humor.

Despite being calm and the voice of reason for Kestrel-1, Mal Korr is by no means a pushover or someone who can be simply underestimated. He can easily fight back and has even shown that his mental will is far stronger than anyone else's which is shown when he goes up against Yorskov Lor Holt. 

This is also demonstrated by his use of a M9 pistol, rather than a standard H&K MP5A3 submachine gun or FN P90 Personal Defense Weapon in most of his career with Kestrel-1. Despite this, he eventually became capable with such weapons as seen during the battle with the Korrynn on Cronus' Rhoketh and the rescue of Mor Holt and "Lt. Tyler" from Ishreeve, demonstrating training in small team tactics. When he first believed Nyx Brandt to be a dangerous Kull warrior on their first meeting, he showed no hesitation in trying to kill the intruder with a Kull disruptor. In more recent years, Mal Korr has taken to carrying the P90 and other heavy weapons on missions with the emergence of the Vornar and the Great Enlightenment, actively fighting Hal Zyx's Ishreeve and Vornar Army soldiers alongside the soldiers of other Order of NorSkov. While recovering the Kestrels stolen by Hal Zyx, Mal Korr demonstrated a military precision and skill matching that of the rest of Kestrel-1. 

Due to his wife, Per Marc being taken as a Jordune host, Mal Korr has developed something of a strong grudge against all Jordune. This is evident when he shoots a tank containing infant Jordune symbiotes despite Captain Mal Zyx warning him not to do so, stating that if he did do it, then they would be no better than the Jordune themselves.

Following his Descension, Daniel displayed a few residual abilities from his time as an Transcended Being:
*Instinctual Knowledge and Understanding of the Xanvoss language: Following his return from Transcendence, Daniel possessed an immediate understanding of the Xanvoss language, even when memory-less and thus without the knowledge of the pre-Transcendence Daniel. When looking over a translation based on his pre-Transcended self's work, the amnesiac Daniel noted that he had been "way wrong" with his interpretation after gaining this new instinctual knowledge of the language. 
*Knowledge of the Cynzyx: As discovered by Yorskov Lor Holt, Daniel possessed within his subconscious the collective knowledge of the Cynzyx, but lacked the ability to access it due to the limitations of the human mind. It is unknown if he still retains this following his second Transcendence and return. 
**Telepathy: While being mind-probed by Yorskov Lor Holt, Daniel was able to access and wield the knowledge of the Cynzyx she was accessing to read and later enter her mind himself through the connection she established between them through the Yorskov mind probe. He was also able to at least temporarily prevent Hal Rynn from reading his mind.
**Yorskov Collective control: Using the Xanvoss knowledge exposed by Yorskov Lor Holt, Daniel was able to enter Yorskov Lor Holt's mind and take control of the entire Yorskov collective, forcing every Yorskov in existence to freeze in place. However, as a human, Daniel lacked the endurance to maintain this control for long. 
*Transcended Being Recognition: Following his Ascensions, Daniel displayed the ability to see through an Transcended Being's various illusions and recognize them for what they really were. 

Bra Thar/Compact of DraDrax

While containing Bra Thar's consciousness, and as a Compact of DraDrax, Daniel gained several near ascension abilities temporarily:
*Access to all of Bra Thar's knowledge: As a result of having Bra Thar's consciousness in his body, Daniel gained access to all of his knowledge and memories, and was able to utilize this to build a new Aeltallis. Before becoming a Compact of DraDrax, using Bra Thar's knowledge in this way was very draining on Daniel. He was able to use this knowledge to grant the Torreeve a cloak presumably based on Zormarc technology with the help of the Torreeve's new Torlynn.

As a Compact of DraDrax, Daniel retained this ability and used it to blast through Hal Rynn's personal shield and stun her while using a Compact of DraDrax disruptor to block her powers. However, breaking through Hal Rynn's personal shield required a great deal of energy from Daniel who was close to reverting to human and left him near-unconscious. 
*Technopathy: After becoming a Compact of DraDrax, Daniel displayed the ability to take mental control of various systems. Using this, Daniel was able to take control of the Torreeve and an Vornar warship. He was also capable of dialing the Gallynn with this ability.
