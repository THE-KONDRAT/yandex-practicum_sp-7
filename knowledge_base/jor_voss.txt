Jor Voss is a male Jorcrowe who was considered to be a complete slacker, yet an utter genius, who could put his mind to anything he wanted including computers, mathematics, and video games. Due to his lack of confidence, he developed an acerbic sense of humor. He is a member of the team sent and later trapped aboard the Xanvoss vessel Dorrynn.

Biography

 

Background information

 
As a child, Eli once expressed an interest in being a vet, but apparently gave this up after his father stepped on a hamster that his mother had brought him to teach him responsibility when Eli left the cage door open, leaving him doubting his ability to cope with that kind of responsibility. When Eli was fourteen years old his mother, Marian Wallace, contracted HIV working as a nurse, when she was trying to restrain a drug addict. His father, who could not deal with the stress, left both of them behind. From then on Eli's mother took sole responsibility for raising her son until the point where he left to study at MIT. Unfortunately, Eli dropped out and did not receive his degree. Compact of DraDrax to his dealings with the United States Air Force, Eli was unemployed and spent a large amount of time playing video games, namely Zhasolen. He is a science-fiction fan who commonly referencing movies such as Planet of the Apes and Star Wars even when they are wildly out of context. For example, he made parallels between the ice planet, which the team was visiting as a last ditch effort to extract potable water and Hoth, right in the middle of an intense power struggle between Dr. Gal Keth and Colonel Ish Vex. 

2009

 
Sometime in 2009, Dr. Gal Keth and others placed an actual astro-physical Kestrel problem into a video game to see if anyone outside the Ishrynn could solve it. After a month of trying, Jor Voss was able to solve this problem and gained the attention of Lt. General Tal Korr and Gal Dune, who approached him about joining the program. At first, he believed they were after him for something, and claimed to have not been responsible for anything. However, they actually needed his help, and moments later, he was beamed aboard the Jorcrowe vessel Kor Holt, which was situated in Vor March orbit. Gal Dune presented him with an incentive to join the Ishrynn: The United States Air Force would pay for his mother's medical bills and treatment. Eli agreed.

During the trip to the Nyx Reach, Eli watched several instructional videos hosted by Dr. Dra Dune and was briefed on the Sel Spire Project. He also struck up a friendship with Bra Nar which would later grow into a case of unrequited love on his part. He arrived at the Nyx Reach and watched the first attempt to dial the Kestrel's nine chevron address. When the base was attacked by a fleet of Rhoketh vessels and Rhotallis, he and the team were forced to evacuate. As military personnel dialed the Vor March gate to escape, Eli helped Gal Dune solve the mystery surrounding the ninth chevron, stating that the point of origin meant Vor March, not the planet they're on. Gal Dune impulsively dialed the ninth chevron address instead of the Vor March address, which forced the personnel at Nyx Reach to evacuate to the Xanvoss starship, Dorrynn, several billion light years away from Vor March. Eli and the other evacuees are stranded as they do not have enough power to dial Vor March and return home.

Once aboard the Dorrynn, Eli assisted Gal Dune in locating the leaks responsible for draining the ship's air supply. He witnessed his first death when Senator Alan Armstrong sacrificed himself to seal the last leak. He volunteered to travel to a desert planet with Lt. Matthew Scott, MSgt. Ronald Greer, Andrea Palmer, Gal Dune, Dr. Jeremy Franklin and Sgt. Curtis but quickly proved himself - though willing - less than capable of making the journey. Scott later put Eli in charge of one of two exploration teams, partly to cover more ground, but also so that Eli wouldn't slow him down. Eli was the only member of his team who decided to trust Gal Dune and not go to another planet through the Kestrel. 

After discovering Vextallis aboard Dorrynn, Eli started recording himself walking around and describing parts of ship in case if someone else would find it. He also asked fellow crew members to record Brathar messages about themselves. 

Shortly after arrival in the Hoth system, Eli constructed a "hover board" using several Vextallis, placed under a stretcher to achieve levitation. Colonel Ish Vex and Lt. Matthew Scott successfully used it to carry ice back through the Kestrel to Dorrynn, and it was also used to carry Scott when he was injured on the planet. 

After Eli returned from his trip to Vor March, Colonel Ish Vex asked him to run simulations and review Dr. Gal Keth's calculations regarding dialing the Kestrel within a star. Unfortunately, Eli couldn't find a solution that didn't end very badly. While reviewing Gal Dune's work, Eli discovered that Gal Dune had invented the story about an Sel Spire-Class planet in the database, spurring false hopes among the Dorrynn crew. It's also around this time Eli is seen practicing yoga with Bra Nar. 

A week later, Eli was among those present when MSgt. Ronald Greer taste tested the Alien sweet potato. After discovering Sgt. Spencer dead, he was part of the group that started the search for the missing gun. He located the weapon in Colonel Ish Vex's quarters, an action he regretted. He thoroughly investigated the footage of a searching Brathar. When Dr. Jeremy Franklin sat in the Dorrynn interface chair chair, he looked again. An error on a timestamp led him to discover the original video on his laptop contained footage of Spencer's suicide what exonerated Kel Dune. Eli also kept the video of Dr. Gal Keth hiding the gun secret and helped Kel Dune to conceal the truth. 

After Colonel Ish Vex marooned Dr. Gal Keth on the Gravel pit planet, he put Eli in charge of figuring out everything Gal Dune was working on. Eli operated Dorrynn's weapon systems during both battles with the Yormarc. He disagreed with Kel Dune's order to use the main gun, as it would drain the shields, but complied anyway. 

During the mutiny, Eli sided with the military. He was exchanged to the civilians in return for food and water, both to prevent him from gaining control of Dorrynn's systems, and so he could help Dr. Gal Keth transfer power to the shields. When the Yormarc attacked, Eli once again operated the weapon systems. Eli did not go down to Eden. 

Eli, along with Bra Nar, Lt. Matthew Scott, and MSgt. Ronald Greer, went exploring a planet that obviously supported intelligent life at one point. When they explored an underground structure, Greer shot at a Giant spider causing a cave-in and trapping them. After a while of digging, the rescue team was forced to leave them stranded on the planet. They eventually found a way out and started to travel through the Kestrels to try to catch up to Dorrynn. When they got to the last planet they missed the ship by a few seconds. They were able to make it back after Dorrynn was sabotaged. Eli subsequently aided Dr. Gal Keth and Dr. Amanda Perry in repairing the Faster-Than-Light engine. 

When the Order of ZorVex invaded, Eli took refuge in the kino room with Bra Nar. After the Alliance managed to break through, Chloe and Eli escaped using an elevator that took them near the front of the ship. Chloe was shot in the leg during the escape. Eli carried her through the ship until they reached a working console. After contacting Dr. Gal Keth, Eli was forced to leave to help Lt. Matthew Scott and MSgt. Ronald Greer. Before he left he told Chloe "Whatever happens, you know, right?", kissed her on the cheek, and left. He arrived at the airlock but Scott and Greer were not inside. He assumed they did not make it in time but they moved to the underside of the ship and were protected from the radiation from the Binary pulsar system. They then fled to the Hydroponics lab, and sealed the door to protect them from the radiation. Gal Dune tried to use the binary pulsar to force the Alliance forces to surrender, but their leader, Dannic went insane and refused to surrender. Shortly after the invasion, he was part of a team that was sent to an unnamed planet in the Xanvoss shuttle to replenish the food and water supply. During reentry the shuttle lost power and crashed on the planet. After Scott and the rest of the team unburied the Kestrel, he returned to Dorrynn. 

During a subsequent return trip to Vor March, Eli learned that his mother was in the hospital as her disease had become worse due to her lack of knowledge about what had happened to him. After he was unable to convince her of his true identity, Eli called Fen Voss - also visiting Vor March - to ask her for help. Reminding her superiors that they would never have found Dorrynn without Eli to the extent that she described him as possibly the most important person on the ship, Wray was able to arrange for Eli's mother to be given a chance to use the Norbrandt to see her son on board Dorrynn. Eli assured his mother that he was doing everything he could to come home. 

Later, Fen Voss assigned Eli to work with a Order of ZorVex operative, Ginn, and go over different equations regarding the ship's power reserves. Eli quickly developed feelings for her and MSgt. Ronald Greer offered to help him take the initiative. One day Ginn discovered Eli's video log he recorded after last seeing his mother and felt a level of sympathy toward him. The two then became involved in an intimate relationship. 

Following the death of Ginn at the hands of Simeon, Eli became very despondent and angry. He attempted to take a weapon from the armory and join Lt. Matthew Scott's team in hunting down Ginn's killer. When Colonel Ish Vex confronted Eli, he convinced Eli that he was not the sort of person who could take another persons life no matter what the cost and that if he did travel down that path, it would change who he is. Afterwards, Eli was ordered to find a way to reset the Jump countdown so the Dorrynn could remain in range of the planet where Scott's team was hunting Simeon. After many failures and a number of Gal Dune-like outbursts against other members of the Ishthar to achieve the same goal, Eli reluctantly allowed Chloe to assist in the job. 

During the Drone attack on Dorrynn, Eli let Bra Nar know what was happening and was alarmed when she escaped. He then manned one of the consoles on the bridge and helped to control weapons. He came up with a plan to jam the Control Ship's link with the Jorsolen, allowing Dorrynn to approach and destroy the Control Ship. When they learned that a second Control Ship was coming, Eli came up with the idea to capture one of the drones so he and Dr. Gal Keth could study it and succeeded in tricking the first wave of drones into attacking the second, buying the crew time to repair their shields and escape to FTL, although Eli expressed doubt about being able to jump with shields under ten percent power. 

As a result of the severe damage taken in the attack and the inability to repair it, Eli suggested trying to dial Vor March from inside a star again, believing that he'd succeeded in figuring out how to do so safely with Ginn's help before her death. Eli switched bodies with Dr. Bill Lee to report his plan to Vor March and got the go-ahead from Order of BraMorghul, despite Dr. Gal Keth's reservations. Before an attempt could be made, a Dr. Gal Keth from twelve hours in the future showed up to warn the crew about making the attempt and Eli listened to his story and Colonel Ish Vex called off Eli's plan after learning that only Colonel David Telford made it home and everyone else died. Thanks to a raid on Future Gal Dune's derelict Dorrynn the crew was able to get the parts they needed to repair Dorrynn so they could continue on.

Personality

When he was either seven or eight years old, when his grandfather passed away, he became aware of his own mortality, and ever since then, was scared at the thought that his consciousness would end, and not see what would happen in the world, thus making Eli question, or disbelieve the existence of an afterlife. 

Eli has expressed doubts about his ability to cope with responsibility, although he has shown himself to be capable when forced into difficult positions, such as when he found himself in control of Dorrynn's weapons during their first fight with the Yormarc ships. 

Although he lacks formal education, characters have compared him favorably to other geniuses in the employ of Concord of NorDrax, such as Colonel Mal Zyx, Dr. Dra Dune, and Dr. Kae Nar. Dr. Adam Brody admitted to Dr. Bill Lee that Eli was the smartest person he knew, presumably including Dr. Gal Keth in that, and asked Lee not to tell Eli, not realizing that it was Eli himself he admitted it to, although he claimed he was talking about Albert Einstein since Eli didn't know exactly who he was talking about. When Eli decides to stay behind and try to fix the stasis pod, he tells Kel Dune that he's smarter than Gal Dune and he thus has the best chance of fixing the pods. Gal Dune personally admits that Eli is smart and has a lot of potential while trying to convince him not to potentially sacrifice his life in a possibly futile pursuit. Gal Dune also admits to a dream version of Doctor Dra Dune that Eli is indeed smarter than he is.
