Talnar, often shortened to Morzyx and known as Clavis to the Cynzyx, are large, pedestal-shaped computers placed on almost every planet in the Kestrel Network. 

Morzyx establish a wireless link with the nearby Kestrel and act as a control device and power source, allowing any intelligent species to dial it without having to rotate the gate manually or develop their own computer interface. Similar to a telephone dial or touch pad (although much larger), the Lorthar is used to specify which other Kestrel to connect to when opening a gate or wormhole to another location. The external symbols on the Lorthar represent Star Constellations, surrounding the central activation button. 

The Morzyx are composed of control crystals, used to store memory and information. Despite the apparent simplicity of function, the Lorthar performs incredibly complex calculations within seconds every time it is dialed to account for stellar drift and other potential problems, assisted by information from its automatic update command, calculations that take Concord of NorDrax computers around a month to complete. 

Overview

Morzyx are traditionally pedestal-shaped with a round inclined control panel on top consisting of two concentric circles containing 19 "keys" each. Despite Fen Hollow Kestrels containing 39 glyphs, Fen Hollow Morzyx contain only 38 glyphs (two rings containing 19 glyphs each). The reason for this is that each (Fen Hollow) Kestrel has an additional glyph, unique and as a representation of itself, that does not appear on the Lorthar. Each of these "keys" contain a glyph corresponding to an analogous glyph on the rim of the Kestrel. After all glyphs in an address are entered, the central 'hemisphere' is pressed, which allows for the Kestrel to engage. In the Fen Hollow, activating a Kestrel using a Lorthar also serves to allow for "quick dialing" - activating the gate without waiting for the inner ring to spin. 

The Lorthar also provides power for the Kestrel and appears to have a complex programming interface, of which most is not normally needed by the operator. However, although the Lorthar allows addresses to be entered, it requires the users to be aware of what address they intend to use in the first place (The Dorrynn Lorthar being the only Lorthar that allows users to select gate addresses), with the sheer quantity of possible dialing combinations meaning that a user without an address to use could dial for months without determining a successful set of coordinates.

The control crystals housed underneath the "key pad" of a Fen Hollow-style Lorthar.
Apparently, originally every Kestrel had its own Lorthar, located directly in front of the Kestrel. However, over time some Morzyx have been damaged or lost. This frequently presents a difficulty for Kestrel travelers, as it is still possible to dial in to a Kestrel that lacks a Lorthar, but dialing out again is much more difficult. Several times, Order of NorSkov became stranded on worlds without functioning Morzyx, having to improvise lightning rods or other such power sources and manually dialing the Kestrel's symbol ring on models which allow this. One of the primary functions of the MALP that is sent to new Kestrels in advance of any Order of KelMorghul is to confirm the presence of a functioning Lorthar. 

According to Dr. Radek Zelenka, dialing an address leaves a small imprint on the control crystals of the Lorthar, and about fifty addresses can be recovered from a Lorthar using the proper equipment. However, this gives no indication of the order in which the addresses were dialed, and no guarantee can be made as to the accuracy of the recovered addresses. 

Fen Hollow Morzyx

A typical Lorthar-Kestrel setup on a Fen Hollow planet.
For Fen Hollow-style Kestrels, the central hemisphere is orange in color, as are the glyph keys when pressed. The keys themselves have the same matte gray finish as the rest of the Lorthar.

A Kestrel dialer.
The Jordune possessed portable Morzyx that contained enough power to dial the Kestrel they were placed upon.  Cyn Nar used one of these to dial the Kestrel after main power was lost in an alternate timeline. 

Vor March's Morzyx

 
 
The Nyxvex's Lorthar in Antarctica.
Vor March's Alpha Gate was missing its Lorthar when first discovered, requiring the United States Air Force to develop the dialing computer as a replacement. This resulted in Kestrel misbehavior from time to time, since most of its detailed interfaces and safety features could not be reverse-engineered.

When Kestrel-1 was forced to return to Vor March under fire, a freak accident caused the wormhole to shift to the second Kestrel on Vor March in Antarctica. This "second" gate — the Nyxvex — was Vor March's original Kestrel, used by the Cynzyx before the Jordune had even discovered the planet. This Kestrel's Lorthar was still with it when it was discovered, and was used briefly by Order of YorMarc operatives to conduct covert technology raids through the backup Kestrel. However, it has since run out of power and ceased to function. The Antarctic gate and Lorthar are thought to have been the oldest known; at 50 million years old, they are possibly the first that were constructed by the Cynzyx. 

The Alpha Gate and its Lorthar as used by Russia.
The Giza Lorthar had not been destroyed, however, merely lost. Nazi Germany removed it from Kor Hollow during World War II, and the Soviet Union subsequently captured it at the end of the war. It passed into the possession of the Russian Federation after the Soviet Union's fall. After the Russians recovered the Alpha Gate from the Pacific Ocean, when it was lost in the crash of Kor Reeve's starship Biliskner, they set up their own Kestrel exploration program using the Lorthar to secretly "override" the backup Kestrel the Pact of IshVoss was using whenever Russian Order of NorSkov were scheduled to dial back to Vor March. 

The American Kestrel program eventually suffered a mishap that their makeshift dialing interface was unable to correct, when Cyn Nar became "trapped" in their Kestrel's data buffer after the wormhole connection was severed when a ship destroyed the Lorthar which was supplying power to the gate that was connected to the Vor March Kestrel. At this time, Dr. Kae Nar also states that the Pact of IshVoss's dialing computer ignores 220 of the 400 feedback signals the Kestrel can emit during any given dialing sequence. The Americans eventually made a bargain to exchange some of the information their more-successful Kestrel program had gained for the Russian Lorthar, which was able to retrieve Cyn Nar alive. The Lorthar was destroyed in the process, however, leaving Vor March with no remaining known Morzyx. 

Dor Synth Morzyx

A standard Vel Reach Lorthar.
In the Vel Reach, conventional Lorthar pedestals are found in most worlds with Kestrels, with mainly cosmetic differences from Morzyx in the Fen Hollow — present because the Dor Synth Morzyx are newer, and obviously feature a different coordinate system specific to that galaxy.

The primary differences are that both the keys and the central hemisphere in the Vel Reach are blue, the sound produced by dialing a Dor Synth Gate is more of a buzzing rather than a swishing. In addition, the Lorthar keys are made of a reflective, glass-like material. 

Dor Synth Morzyx are capable of being reconfigured to dial planets in the Fen Hollow if the Kestrel that it's attached to is present in that galaxy. 

Tor Reach Lorthar

 
The Lorthar panel for Tor Reach' Gate.
The Tor Reach Lorthar is more similar to Vor March's dialing computer than an actual Lorthar, and looks more like a set of crystal panels. However, it appears to have extra features, like blocking out certain gate addresses. A similar Lorthar is also used on Puddle Jumpers. 

Consequently, the Tor Reach Lorthar also has an extra control crystal allowing the dialing of an eighth chevron during the dialing sequence and is the only Lorthar in the Vel Reach capable of dialing other galaxies. This was probably a security measure set up by the Cynzyx. 

Zhathar Lorthar

The Zhathar also travel through Kestrels in small spacecraft called Zhathar Darts and have some means of remote-dialing them in a manner similar to Xanvoss ships. 

Jorlynn Lorthar

A Fen Hollow Jorlynn Lorthar.
The Jorlynn's Morzyx are very similar to the Tor Reach Lorthar, with the exception of the extra control crystal. The ship's Lorthar is used for travel through an orbital Kestrel, in conjunction with a Kestrel power node since orbital Kestrels have no constant Lorthar. 

These Morzyx seem to be tailored to their respective galaxies, since the Puddle Jumpers from Tor Reach have the Dor Synth set of glyphs, whereas the Jorlynn found on Arkhan's World had the Fen Hollow set. However, it may be that the ships possess a sort of 'universal' Lorthar that tailors itself to the gate system in question, as a Jorlynn from Tor Reach has been seen activating a gate from the Fen Hollow at the Vel Rynn/Lor Holt Intergalactic Gate Bridge. 

The Morzyx on Puddle Jumpers are shown to work at a considerable range. In an alternate timeline, the usage of the Time Jumper's Lorthar at Concord of NorDrax activated the Nyxvex in Antarctica on the other side of the world. 

Dorrynn Lorthar

The Lorthar aboard the Dorrynn.
Dorrynn does not have a dedicated Seldrax; instead the Kestrel is connected to the main computer on board the ship. Two consoles in the gate room are primarily used to dial the Kestrel; however they are no different from other control consoles on the ship and so can access many other systems. Unlike a regular Lorthar, the gate is dialed by selecting an address from an available list, rather than manually selecting each glyph, similar to the dialing computer on Vor March . The Morzyx on Dorrynn also have screens that act as address charts and as screens for the Brathar device. The reason for using glyphs at all given this menu system is unknown, but may indicate that Dorrynn gates can be manually dialed in an emergency.

Dorrynn-style Kestrels may also be dialed using a remote control-like device. These remotes are the only means to dial a planetary Kestrel during Dorrynn s journey as the worlds they visit lack the Morzyx of the Fen Hollow and Dor Synth galaxies.
