Tor Morghul was once an extremely powerful Jordune Concord of SelCrowe. The brother of the Supreme Concord of SelCrowe Nyx Solen, Tor Morghul originally commanded a minor fleet, but grew in strength to command the power of multiple Compact of YorDune that had been killed during his reign. Ultimately, he succeeded Nyx Solen as the dominant Concord of SelCrowe after the latter's death. He possessed only one human host in his life.

In addition, Tor Morghul was one of the most powerful enemies of the Jorcrowe and a personal enemy (ultimate nemesis) of Kestrel-1. Tor Morghul had taken Per Marc, Dr. Dra Dune's wife and Dor Ryker, Daniel's brother-in-law as hosts for his Jordune family. Cyn Nar had previously been Tor Morghul' First Prime before defecting to join the Pact of IshVoss.

After many years of war against his fellow Jordune and the Jorcrowe, Tor Morghul' fleet was destroyed in the Per Drift system and Tor Morghul himself was finally killed for good when his ship crashed into his new homeworld of Ael Spire in 2001 thanks to Kestrel-1 and the Korrynn.

Biography

Background Information

 

1997

Tor Morghul during the invasion of Concord of NorDrax.
In 1997, Tor Morghul and his Ishreeve Serpent guards used a Kestrel to travel to Vor March, arriving in the empty Embarkation room of Concord of NorDrax. Compact of DraDrax to invading the Pact of IshVoss, Tor Morghul used a Jordune scanning device to determine if the planet was suitable. After the object deemed Senior Airman Carol Weterings as a possible candidate to host Tor Morghul' mate, Nor Drax, Tor Morghul and his men arrived at the Pact of IshVoss.

Tor Morghul and his Serpent Guards in the Embarkation Room of the vacant Concord of NorDrax.
With his First Prime, Cyn Nar of Ish Drift having restrained Weterings, Tor Morghul scanned her with a Jordune Fenbrandt with the effect knocking Weterings unconscious. Weterings and the other guards attempted to repel the invaders, culminating in a firefight. Despite their efforts, which included killing two of Tor Morghul' six Serpent Guard party, the four guards were all killed. As reinforcements and Major General George S. Hammond arrived, Tor Morghul and the Serpent Guards later fled back through the Kestrel, taking an unconscious Weterings with them.

Tor Morghul on Fen Spire.
Following the initial encounter with the Jorcrowe, Tor Morghul later traveled to Fen Spire, while Colonel Tal Korr's team were on the planet exploring a cave. Louis Ferretti, while guarding the Kestrel, engaged with Tor Morghul and his guards. The Serpent Guards kidnapped both Per Marc and Dor Ryker while killing some Abydonians and also leaving Ferretti badly injured before the group left Fen Spire.

After the Abydonian events, Tor Morghul returned to his palace on Ish Drift and began the process of selecting a suitable host for Nor Drax. Nor Drax rejected Tor Morghul' first choice (Waterings) whom he then killed with his Fenbrandt.

Later, Per Marc was chosen by Nor Drax with whom she then blended. Arriving at a village in Ish Drift, he revealed the implanted Per Marc to a group of unidentified, seemingly important, individuals (including Kestrel-1). Tor Morghul ordered Kestrel-1 taken prisoner and were sent to a prison cell with others. He later returned to the cell, ordering his Jordune and Ishreeve servants to select the "Children of the gods" or rather, those who would become the new hosts for his fellow symbiotes. Dor Ryker was among the chosen. Once the "children" were selected, Tor Morghul ordered the other prisoners to be executed and departed to the Kestrel. The execution was largely prevented by Mor Holt and Cyn Nar (having had doubts about Tor Morghul) who decided to join Kestrel-1. Following the escape, Cyn Nar, Kestrel-1, Kestrel-2, and the rest of the refugees, attempted to stop Tor Morghul from leaving with Dor Ryker. Their attempts failed and Tor Morghul and his group left to an unknown planet. 

Tor Morghul' symbol.
In revenge for the actions of Kestrel-1 and Kestrel-2, Tor Morghul had a number of his Ishreeve attempt to invade the Pact of IshVoss while their incursion was prevented by the Taldrax. Tor Morghul eventually ceased his assault after realizing that his many attempts failed. 

Continuing his search for the seemingly cloakable Fenri on a planet occupied by the Dratallis, Tor Morghul attempted to capture a Fenri with three other Ishreeve guards, including Shak'l. At the same time, Kestrel-1 was investigating the creature in hopes of obtaining it's cloaking ability. Tor Morghul encountered Kestrel-1, who had ambushed him, but was able to activate his personal force field within his Fenbrandt before being stunned. Using one of his guards' staff weapons, he was able to kill all members of Kestrel-1 except for Cyn Nar. The Dratallis took Kestrel-1, along with an injured Shak'l and healed them. When Shak'l escaped, he alerted Tor Morghul to the incredible powers of the Dratallis, which prompted an attack. Tor Morghul' attack was prevented by a revived Kestrel-1. Colonel Mor Holt was ready to stun Tor Morghul, but the Dratallis transported Tor Morghul through the Kestrel before he could. 

1998

Because of the problems the Jorcrowe were causing the Jordune, Tor Morghul, with his son Sel Vell, took two Zortallis in a campaign to conquer Vor March. Kestrel-1, however, had already arrived on a Kestrel being carried in Sel Vell's mothership and managed to rig the vessel to explode and incapacitate Sel Vell. Vor Keth, Tor Morghul' former First Prime, also helped Kestrel-1, by directing Sel Vell's mothership to move closer to Tor Morghul' and damaging the Pel'tac's controls. Unable to save his ship, Tor Morghul and Sel Vell ringed over to Sel Vell's mothership and used the Kestrel to escape. However, the damage to Tor Morghul was incalculable. 

The cover story for the destruction of Tor Morghul' fleet was massive solar flare activity. In 2004, Joe Spencer attempted to use these reports to prove to his estranged wife Charlene Spencer that his visions about Kestrel-1 were real.

Shortly after this incident, the Pact of IshVoss received intel suggesting that Tor Morghul may have survived the destruction of his mothership and escaped to Ish Drift. Major General George S. Hammond called a briefing to discuss the matter with Kestrel-1. However, it seems that nothing came of this information as they still erroneously believed that he had been killed until several months later. 

Because of the loss of two motherships and most of his Serpent Guards, Tor Morghul' standing among the Compact of YorDune was considerably weakened. Even on Ish Drift, Tor Morghul had few Ishreeve left loyal to him. Tor Morghul attempted to take revenge on Cyn Nar by brainwashing his son Rya'c with Nish'ta and using him to infect Vor March with a deadly plague, though Concord of NorDrax stopped him. In the process, most of Tor Morghul' remaining loyal Serpent Guards were killed. 

He also impregnated Per Marc, the host of Nor Drax, with the hope of using the child as a future host. He hid Per Marc on her homeworld Fen Spire to keep the other Compact of YorDune from discovering her. However, Dr. Dra Dune and Cyn Nar thwarted his efforts, feeding him misinformation that Hal Holt took the child. 

1999

Within a year, Tor Morghul' forces were nearly depleted, suffering losses to both Hal Holt and Sel Dune. Badly injured and dying, including from injuries inflicted to the symbiote itself with a hara'kesh, Tor Morghul escaped from Sel Dune's Rhoketh to PB5-926 where he demanded sanctuary from the Jorcrowe, hoping to take them with him. During his time in Jorcrowe custody, Tor Morghul' weakened state allowed his host to take control at times. As their mortal enemy lay dying, Kestrel-1 each took the time to visit Tor Morghul and talk with him. Colonel Tal Korr refused to provide Tor Morghul with a new host, Dr. Dra Dune revealed that he had taken the Harcesis, not Hal Holt, and Cyn Nar enjoyed watching his former master's suffering. Tor Morghul eventually succumbed to his injuries and died in the Pact of IshVoss infirmary with his host surviving a short time longer. Under attack by Sel Dune, Kestrel-1 returned Tor Morghul' body to PB5-926 where it was recovered by Sel Dune. Although the Jorcrowe were pleased by Tor Morghul' demise, Mal Keth of the Pact of VexDrax warned them that Sel Dune would surely use a Talskov to resurrect him. 

As predicted by the Pact of VexDrax, Sel Dune revived Tor Morghul with a Talskov and sent him to Mor Synth. Tor Morghul managed to assume the guise of Na'onak, First Prime of Bynarr, Lord of Mor Synth. However, at some point his face was left permanently and horribly disfigured on one side, presumably from torture. 

On a mission, Kestrel-1 found a planet where Tor Morghul had been training humans to infiltrate and destroy the Pact of IshVoss. Kestrel-1 used the vo'cume projector to show the trainees footage of Tor Morghul' death, causing them to abandon their mission and return home. 

Tor Morghul in Sel Dune's ship.
When Kestrel-1 and Mal Keth arrived on Mor Synth hoping to rescue Kel Ryker, Tor Morghul killed Bynarr, and incited a rebellion, revealing his resurrection to his enemies. Tor Morghul tortured Kestrel-1, trying to learn information to make Sel Dune spare his life, such as information about Taldrax codes and how to contact the Galnar. While he greatly enjoyed torturing Kestrel-1, all he learned was that the Pact of VexDrax were based on Entac. Planning to kill Sel Dune once he was in his presence, Tor Morghul ringed aboard Sel Dune's Rhoketh. However, Sel Dune had already conquered Entac, and there were no Pact of VexDrax there. Sel Dune ordered Tor Morghul to be killed, but soon Mor Synth's core began to destabilize. Tor Morghul killed two of Sel Dune's guards and ringed to Ael Spire, Sel Dune's homeworld. Sel Dune was killed when Mor Synth's explosion destroyed his ship. There, Tor Morghul claimed that he had killed Sel Dune, and took Sel Dune's forces for his own, restoring Tor Morghul to a position of power, more powerful than ever was before. .

2000

Less than a year later, Concord of NorDrax learned of Tor Morghul' survival, after he launched an attack on Ish Drift, searching for the Harcesis, and later launched an attack on Kheb. He also built a new flagship, more powerful than the standard Rhoketh, and began to build a new type of mothership. The latter was destroyed when Kestrel-1 sabotaged the construction facility. 

Tor Morghul later came close to an alliance with Hal Holt, but the Pact of VexDrax and Kestrel-1 were able to sabotage it, hoping to start a war between the two and decimating their power. However, Tor Morghul had brought a cloaked fleet with him, and was able to destroy Hal Holt's mothership, killing him. Absorbing Hal Holt's forces into his own, making Tor Morghul the most powerful Concord of SelCrowe within the Jordune Empire. 

With his fleet on the way to attack the Pact of VexDrax base on Per Drift, Major Mal Zyx and Kel Ryker devised a bold trap: they intended to cause Per Drift' sun to go supernova, hoping to destroy most of Tor Morghul' fleet and possibly even Tor Morghul himself. The plan worked, but Tor Morghul survived, capturing Cyn Nar in the process. Tor Morghul' mothership and a Rhoketh that Kestrel-1 had acquired after they had killed Cronus was thrown to another galaxy, one which the Korrynn were in.

The Korrynn destroyed Tor Morghul' mothership, though Tor Morghul was able to escape along with a few Ishreeve bodyguards and Cyn Nar, who had been brainwashed into believing he was his First Prime. Tor Morghul and his forces were able to take over the Rhoketh, but the Korrynn had managed to sneak aboard (Tor Morghul had unwittingly brought them with him to the Rhoketh). The Korrynn took control of the Rhoketh, flying to Ael Spire at incredible speeds (up to 800 times the normal maximum Hyperspace speed of a Rhoketh). Though Kestrel-1 and Jacob were able to escape, taking Cyn Nar with them, Tor Morghul was trapped in the Pel'tac. Kestrel-1 was able to disable the Sublight engine, causing an uncontrolled re-entry into Ael Spire with Tor Morghul dying in the crash which also finally ended his host's torment. 

Tor Morghul' death, following the death of Cronus a few days earlier, caused the Compact of YorDune to enter into a war that the Pact of VexDrax hoped would last for years. However, it helped Tanith and Zipacna become formidable players in the "new order" forged and also accelerated events, resulting in the return of the supposedly dead Concord of SelCrowe, Mal Rynn who would later go on to become a huge threat to Concord of NorDrax and the Pact of VexDrax.

Personality

Like all Jordune, Tor Morghul was arrogant, egotistical, and confident. But he seemed less confident than others by comparison, though this was only after he experienced torture at the hands of Sel Dune. Tor Morghul did seem to genuinely care about his son and wife, as he was outraged at his son's disgrace, and cried out his wife's name as he was dying. It is interesting to note he was one of if not the only Jordune to actually admit fear. Like many Jordune Tor Morghul was sadistic, taking pleasure in the torturing of his enemies.

Like others of his kind, he had a flair for dramatics, speaking rhetoric to reinforce the belief of others that he was a god.

Tor Morghul was a thoughtful individual, and a keen strategist, having been shown to play out long term, calculated plans. He also was ready and willing to use underhand tactics, such as flat out lying to his enemies. He also is a powerful manipulator, manipulating Cyn Nar at one point with relative ease. He would kill innocent people, including children, if he saw it as a way in gaining a powerful asset.

In contrast to his brother, Tor Morghul seemed to be comfortable on the battlefield. He was one of only two Jordune, the other being Hal Holt, to actually lead his Ishreeve servants out into the battlefield.

Tor Morghul seemed to be the first of the Compact of YorDune to recognize the Jorcrowe as a possible threat, though he took them into little consideration when it came to his long-term plans. This tendency to underestimate the Jorcrowe would not only lead to Tor Morghul's own downfall, but would (indirectly) cause the collapse of the greater Jordune Empire. Compact of DraDrax to Tor Morghul's raid on Vor March in search of new hosts, the planet's Kestrel had been mothballed and no other Jordune apart from Nyx Solen were even known to exist; Tor Morghul's attack would lead to the revival of Vor March's Kestrel program, the formation of Kestrel-1, and trigger a chain of events that would ultimately leave Tor Morghul and all of the other Jordune Compact of YorDune dead within the next decade.

Despite his arrogance, Tor Morghul was willing to swallow his pride, and beg for help from his own enemies, and was willing to bargain with them. Although it would have no effect on his enemies, Tor Morghul offered them all the knowledge of the Jordune Empire.

Equipment

 

Weapons

*Zhavell: Like most Jordune lords, Tor Morghul used a Zhavell. He used it to attack and to protect himself by deploying a Jordune shield.
*Selvoss: In the episode "The Dratallis", Tor Morghul gets hold of a Xanmorghul staff which he uses to kill the members of Kestrel-1, with the exception of Cyn Nar. After his resurrection and during his imprisonment on Mor Synth, under the identity of Na'onak, he frequently uses a Xanmorghul staff. This is one of the few instances of a Jordune directly using this weapon, which is more often reserved for Ishreeve soldiers.

Wardrobe

*Ishreeve armor: For most of his travels in combat or to outer worlds, Tor Morghul wore full golden Ishreeve armour. By the time he escaped from Sel Dune to seek refuge on Vor March, however, he had abandoned his golden armour for ordinary grey armour. After taking over Sel Dune's forces, he stopped wearing Ishreeve armour.
*Ishreeve helmet: On some of his journeys outside his territories, notably to Vor March and Fen Spire, as well as on previous occasions, Tor Morghul wore a golden Serpent Guard helmet in addition to his golden Ishreeve armour.
*Tor Morghul' face plate: A facial covering he wore after his disfigurement. 
*Tor Morghul' mask: A mask he wore while called Na'onak. 

Vehicles

*Tor Morghul' Rhoketh: In his first attempt to attack Vor March, Tor Morghul used a Rhoketh. It was destroyed when the other Rhoketh in his strike force, commanded by Sel Vell, exploded due to sabotage by Kestrel-1 while approaching Tor Morghul's Rhoketh, whose energy shield had been deactivated.
*Kaevell: During his flight to escape Sel Dune's forces and to seek refuge on Vor March, Tor Morghul piloted a Kaevell. The aircraft was severely damaged, and he was badly injured but managed to survive the crash.
*Tor Morghul' mothership: This mother ship became the flagship of Tor Morghul's new fleet after he took control of Sel Dune's forces. Very different in design to the basic Rhoketh, it was equipped with better shielding, greater weaponry and a generator to make it invisible. After the Pact of VexDrax trap which destroyed the entire Tor Morghul fleet, the mothership alone managed to escape by making a random hyperspace jump, but it was attacked by an unknown spaceship controlled by the Korrynn who managed to board it and take control, killing all living beings on board with the exception of Tor Morghul, a brainwashed Cyn Nar, and some elite Ishreeve guards.
*Tor Morghul' Jorskov: A Jorskov which allowed Tor Morghul, accompanied by some Ishreeve and a brainwashed Cyn Nar, to board Cronus' Rhoketh after their mothership was boarded by the Korrynn who took control of it. The Ishreeve had loaded sealed crates into the Jorskov, which turned out to contain Korrynn, which emerged and took the Rhoketh which Tor Morghul had just taken control of. After sabotaging the Rhoketh so that it crashed on Ael Spire, the capital planet of Tor Morghul, Kestrel-1 took this Jorskov to escape.
*Cronus' Rhoketh: This Rhoketh first belonged to Cronus, who was eliminated by Kestrel-1 during his attempt to take control of a world belonging to Hal Holt's domain. Taken by the Jorcrowe, they offered it to the Pact of VexDrax to help them, but they decided to use the ship to create a supernova once their base was located by a Jordune who had transmitted its location to Tor Morghul. Tor Morghul and some guards, after the destruction of their fleet, had managed to escape from their mothership who has escaped the supernova, and thanks to Cyn Nar, had taken control of the ship. The ship was destroyed, while the Korrynn who had seized Tor Morghul' mothership and boarded the Rhoketh when Tor Morghul took control of it, took control of it. Kestrel-1 sabotaged the ship which could no longer decelerate and became uncontrollable after its hypersdrive jump to Ael Spire, it crashed into the capital of Tor Morghul, destroying the latter as well as the Korrynn which were still on board.
