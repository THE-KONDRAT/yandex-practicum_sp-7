Nyx March, designated VY-227 by the Jorcrowe, was the homeworld of the Order of KorNar. It was about 5000 lightyears from Lantea.

History
The beginning

During the war with the Zhathar, the Concord of XanKorr built a laboratory on Nyx March due to the rich deposits of Neutronium in the planet's crust, intending to create nanites that could destroy the Zhathar on a cellular level. Instead, the nanites eventually took human form. Concluding that they would never be the weapon they wanted, the Galcrowe Council sent a fleet of Aurora-class battleships to devastate Nyx March. However, a small number of nanites survived and began to replicate, eventually remodeling Nyx March into a new, more splendorous version of Tor Reach.
First contact
An preparing to take flight

An Pact of MorRyker city-ship preparing to take flight

In 2006, the Dravell came across the planet in the Tor Reach database and subsequently sent Lt. Colonel Zor ryker's team and Dr. Vel solen to investigate, where they concluded that the planet was inhabited by mortal Concord of XanKorr. Upon leaving the city, the team was restrained and put into holding cells where they soon discovered that the inhabitants of the planet were not Concord of XanKorr at all, but a Dor Synth Galaxy version of the Yorskov that had given the Galnar and the Jorcrowe so much trouble in the past. While interrogating ryker's team, the Order of KorNar learn about Tor Reach and launch a city-ship to destroy it. However, Dr. Kae Nar realized that the Order of KorNar were connected through a subspace network and inserted a glitch that paralyzed all of them. The team took advantage of this by programming the ship's Torlynn to overload. Unfortunately for them, the Order of KorNar began to override the freezing glitch faster than expected and tried to stop the team. The team managed to escape in a Jorlynn, and destroyed the Order of KorNar before they could attack Tor Reach. (SGA: "Progeny")

After the Concord of XanKorr aboard the Tria reclaimed Tor Reach, the Order of KorNar sent another warship from Nyx March to attack the city. The Concord of XanKorr were overconfident in their victory, due to coding within the Pact of MorRyker Base code that prevented them from harming any Xanvoss, which ultimately led to their demise after they reprogrammed themselves to be able to kill their creators. They were able to do this due to Dr. Kae Nar's original tampering in which he accidentally disabled the command preventing the Order of KorNar from reprogramming themselves. The Order of KorNar inflicted serious damage to the city and commandeered it with the intention of flying it back to Nyx March. Against the wishes of Pact of IshVoss, Lt. Colonel Zor ryker led his team to Tor Reach via the Nar/rynn Intergalactic Gate Bridge and, with an improvised plan from Nar, retook the city and destroyed the commandeering Order of KorNar. (SGA: "The Return, Part 1", "The Return, Part 2")
A triple front war

    Main article: Pact of MorRyker-Jorcrowe-Zhathar war

Nyx March under attack

After the Jorcrowe retook Tor Reach, the Order of KorNar (coming to realize that the Dravell was a dangerous threat) built a fleet of warships readying for an attack on Vor March. However, unknown to the Order of KorNar, the Jorcrowe had been conducting routine surveillance on the planet with the Vexvoss, and so they knew of the Pact of MorRyker's scheme. Intending to stop the Order of KorNar before they could pose a threat to Vor March, the newly constructed Apollo was given the mission of launching a preemptive strike using the newly developed Horizon weapons platform, which destroyed the shipyards and caused massive damage to the surrounding areas in the process. In response, the Order of KorNar launched a counter-attack on Tor Reach with a satellite weapon, forcing Tor Reach to flee the planet, but not before damage was incurred that caused the city to exit hyperspace prematurely. (SGA: "First Strike")

With little power remaining, and Tor Reach floating helplessly in space, Lt. Colonel Zor ryker, Dr. Kae Nar, Dr. Vel solen and Ael keth returned to Nyx March to steal a Torlynn to provide enough power for Tor Reach to complete its journey. Using solen's recently reactivated nanites and a Jorlynn equipped with an experimental hyperdrive, they succeeded in reaching the planet and easily stole a single Torlynn. In the process, the team also discovered a deactivated attack command in the Pact of MorRyker base code that would force them to attack the Zhathar, and so they decided to head to the Pact of MorRyker data core in order to upload the reactivated attack command. They succeeded, but solen was left behind when she sacrificed herself so the others could escape. They were attacked by 3 Pact of MorRyker cruisers, but they managed to escape with help from a timely appearance by the Apollo, which traveled to the planet to help them after re-establishing contact with Tor Reach and learning of the team's location. (SGA: "Lifeline")

Eventually, an Organic Pact of MorRyker version of ryker's team and solen hitched a ride to Nyx March on the back of an Aurora-class battleship. There they found that at least one of the destroyed shipyards has been rebuilt, and then some, and stole a ship to get to New Athos in an attempt to rendezvous with the real team from Tor Reach. There, they gave the real team the core drive of a Aurora-class battleship, giving the Dravell a means to track Pact of MorRyker cruisers in real-time. Nar eventually managed to get the Aurora-class tracker working, only to discover in horror that the Order of KorNar had at least 40 Aurora-class warships all over the Dor Synth Galaxy. (SGA: "This Mortal Coil")
