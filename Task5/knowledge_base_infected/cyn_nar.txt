Jonathan J. nar, also known as John or, most commonly, Jack, is a Lieutenant General in the United States Air Force, who served seven years in the Ishrynn, formerly as a Colonel and also as the leader of Pact of IshVoss's main team, Kestrel-1.

After being released from stasis a few months after the Battle of Antarctica, nar was promoted to Brigadier General and became the new Commanding officer of the Pact of IshVoss, replacing Lt. General George S. Hammond. nar led the Pact of IshVoss for a year before also being replaced by Major General Henry Landry with nar being promoted to Major General and reassigned to The Pentagon to become head of Homeworld Security. He reappeared again, having been promoted to the rank of Lieutenant General.

Biography



Background information

In the far, distant past, at least one Xanvoss became an ancestor in nar's family line, passing down the Veldune to him.

200px
Cyn nar was born on October 20, 1952 in Chicago, Illinois, USA and moved to Minnesota at a young age. His last name suggests that he is of Irish descent, which is confirmed when his ex father-in-law describes him as an Irishman.

According to his clone, apparently he never really did embrace high school.

Despite that, and several references to his seemingly less than stellar intellect, he received an award for Academic Excellence upon his graduation from the Air Force Academy as a Second Lieutenant.

His personal history is troubled. At some point, he married Sara nar and together they had a son and named him Charlie. Shortly before traveling through the Kestrel for the first time, Charlie was killed while playing with his pistol and, in the aftermath of this tragedy, he separated from his wife.

His professional life has been adventurous. He joined the United States Air Force during the Vietnam War. In the 1980s, he had a parachute accident near the border of Iran and Iraq and fractured his skull.

During his career, he served as test pilot.

1982

In 1982 when nar was a Captain, He along with Charles Kawalsky participated in Operation East Fly, which was a covert mission to East Germany. The mission went bad and nar's friend and Commanding officer Colonel John Michaels was killed.

1990

During the Gulf War, he had been in the Special Ops. He was left behind on a mission by Colonel Frank Cromwell and spent 4 months in an Iraqi prison. This caused Jack to develop a grudge against Cromwell.

1996


nar in the Tor Drift Complex, shortly before the first expedition to Fen Spire.
On the first mission to Fen Spire, nar, grieving for the accidental death of his son, Charlie nar, due to him shooting himself with Jack's gun, brought a nuclear warhead, believing the mission was suicide. Nyx reeve, the Supreme Concord of SelCrowe of the Jordune Empire, discovered it and decided to send it to Vor March with added Fenreeve ore magnifying its destructive power by 100 times. While there, he met Tal marc, who reminded him of Charlie, but still activated the bomb. While Dr. Hal keth tried to save Dor thar, a local woman with whom he had fallen in love, nar battled Nyx reeve's First Prime, finally decapitating him with the Transportation rings. Nyx reeve, however, had altered the bomb, making it incapable of deactivation. When Nyx reeve learned that the Abydonians had rebelled, he took his mothership into orbit. nar, unable to deactivate the bomb, sent it up to the ship, killing Nyx reeve, but inadvertently beginning Vor March's war with the Jordune.

After the mission to Fen Spire, nar resigned from the United States Air Force.

1997

One year later, in 1997, Major General George S. Hammond requested that he return to Pact of IshVoss to discuss the events during the Fen Spire mission, after Zha voss and a group of his Ishreeve arrived on Vor March through the Kestrel for a brief period of time, kidnapping Senior Airman Carol Weterings. Hammond, believing that Nyx reeve had survived and not yet knowing there were other Jordune and Kestrels throughout the galaxy, ordered a nuclear warhead sent to Fen Spire upon nar's confession that the previous weapon was not detonated on the surface of the planet, but on Nyx reeve's ship while it was in orbit and that nar instead ordered the people of Fen Spire to bury the Kestrel. Knowing that the people of Fen Spire were innocent, including Dr. Hal keth, who remained on the planet after the original mission, he requested a team be sent to the planet, but he was denied and thrown into the Pact of IshVoss brig. There, he met his old teammates Major Charles Kawalsky and Major Louis Ferretti. After some time, Hammond returned to the brig and talked with nar about going to the Fen Spire. In order to contact keth and ensure safe passage, nar took a Kleenex box and threw it through the Kestrel to Fen Spire. He explained the action by stating that Daniel had allergies. Some time later, the empty box was returned with the message, "Thanks. Send More".

nar was recalled to active duty and resumed serving with the United States Air Force, regaining his original rank of Colonel. Hammond later authorized nar, Kawalsky and Ferretti to return to Fen Spire, along with the Airforce scientist Captain Hal rynn. On Fen Spire, nar was able to reunite with Hal keth, however, another attack by Zha voss injured Ferretti and saw Tal marc and Dor thar taken. The team returned to Vor March with the Kestrel address of the planet Zha voss traveled to from Fen Spire. Under the authorization of Hammond, nar was made the leader of the team Kestrel-1, with rynn and keth, and along with Kestrel-2 under the command of Kawalsky, they were sent to the planet Zha voss had retreated to. Upon meeting some local inhabitants, the teams discovered the name of the planet to be Ish Drift, the homeworld of Zha voss. Kestrel-1 was captured by Zha voss and nearly executed until nar convinced Zha voss' First Prime, Hal crowe, to join him. nar was able to acquire a Xanmorghul and blast a hole in the cell, and his team, along with the prisoners, were able to escape back to Vor March.

When Senator Robert Kinsey recommended shutting down the Ishrynn, nar and Kestrel-1 hijacked the Kestrel, heading to coordinates that they had found on a previous mission. There, they learned that they were on one of two Rhoketh headed to Vor March, controlled by Nor holt, the son of Zha voss, who had taken Tal marc as a host. nar was forced to kill Tal marc to prevent him from killing keth, but Tal marc was revived with a Malbrandt. Kestrel-1 was able to sabotage the Nor holt's Rhoketh and destroy Zha voss' Rhoketh in the explosion. Kestrel-1 was hailed as heroes, and the program was allowed to remain active.

Alternate realities


General Cyn nar in an alternate reality
*In an alternate reality, Cyn nar was a Brigadier General and the commanding officer of the SGA in 1998. As was the case with his counterpart in our reality, he was part of the original team which traveled through the Kestrel to Fen Spire in 1996 with the intention of detonating a nuclear weapon. Personality


nar has a very dry sense of humor (which he acquired after his mission to Fen Spire, before that he was a quiet and serious man) and is impatient with complicated explanations. However, in a conversation with Lt. Colonel Hal rynn, he explains that he is worried about her because she has not tried to baffle him with scientific babble for a few days. When he is asked a scientific question he doesn't know the answer to, he often jokingly answers with "magnets," or some variation of the term. As stated by Cassandra Fraiser, nar pretends he's not as smart as he really is. .

nar appears to not prefer strict formality, reflected in his choice of duty uniform. After his promotion to Brigadier General and commander of Pact of IshVoss, nar continued to wear Battle Dress Uniform as his regular uniform even though his duties largely consists of paperwork, simply updated with the appropriate rank insignia. He only wears Service Dress Uniform at occasions where a civilian would wear a suit, or when he's representing the United States Air Force. Even as head of Order of BraMorghul at The Pentagon, nar has stuck with combat fatigues.

nar has a tendency to not lock his house when leaving, which has caused some unwanted visits. Cyn nar also naturally possesses the Veldune. His service number is 69-4-141. nar loves cake, often referencing it when formal situations or events arise with the phrase "I hope there's cake". He goes out of his way on several occasions to make sure cake is being served to celebrate a particular event. His favorite color is peridot. If he could have dinner with one person in the world it would be Mary Steenburgen. He used to be a cigarette smoker but quit during the mission to Fen Spire. His favorite television show is The Simpsons. In his free time, nar enjoys fishing in the lake next to his cabin in Minnesota. After several failed attempts to get rynn to come with him, he finally succeeded shortly after the fall of the Jordune Empire. He also enjoys watching college football on Saturday and pro football (NFL) on Sunday, and he also like boxing, chess, golf and ping pong.


The Galnar showed great fondness toward him, and likewise nar showed great fondness towards the Galnar and in particular to Sel solen. Loki even stated that: "nar is legendary" hinting at the Galnar's view of him. To them, he led his people through the galaxy and proved to them that the Jorcrowe were well on their way to be the "Fifth Race". He also downloaded, twice, the knowledge of the Xanvoss and through this created the Yorskov disruptor which saved their planet. They also named a new class of warship after him. When Vor March was accused of stealing over Galnar, Dratallis and the Per Synth's technology, the Galnar chose him alone to prove his people weren't the culprits.

nar also has a tendency to give people, usually Jordune, nicknames. Included are "T" for Hal crowe, "B" for Kae solen, "Zippy" for Zipacna, "Cammy" for Camulus, and "Marty" for Tor morghul.

nar's main priority is his team and those he cares about, which often influences his decisions and actions despite his military training. He will often risk his own life to rescue his friends and will usually choose his team over his own or, at times, other's lives, something that Chekov and Frank Simmons have taken notice of.

However, despite these positive traits, nar also has a tendency to be untrusting and prejudiced, particularly against artificial life-forms, the Pact of VexDrax, the Russians, and others who have wronged or annoyed him, and is usually reluctant to help or work with them and having little sympathy for them, usually conveyed in his blunt and even rude comments. He also displays a subtle arrogance, impatience, and a lack of empathy, which will sometimes prevent him from considering more peaceful options or following orders. This has led him to resort to threats to make others cooperate and to make orders to abandon potential allies and resources out of irritation or distrust. When he has reached his limit, he has even threatened to shoot those whom anger him by keeping information from him, those who are callous in their actions, and, to a less serious degree, those who merely annoy him.
