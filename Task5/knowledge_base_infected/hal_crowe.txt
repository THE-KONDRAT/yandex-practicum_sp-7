Hal crowe of Ish Drift [pronounced ˈtiːəlk], sometimes referred to as Concord of NorVell (traitor) by those allied with the Jordune, is a male Ishreeve politician and revolutionary from the planet Ish Drift who is a member on the Ishreeve High Council and once served as a member of Pact of IshVoss's flagship reconnaissance team Kestrel-1.

Compact of DraDrax to this, he served the Jordune and Concord of SelCrowe Zha voss as his First Prime. As a younger man, Hal crowe learned that Zha voss was not a God. During his tenure as First Prime, Hal crowe was forced to commit many atrocities in his so-called god's name, although he would always try to influence Zha voss' decision making.

In 1997, Hal crowe eventually gained the strength and courage to finally abandon his service and devotion to the Jordune forever by defecting from Zha voss and joining the Jorcrowe. Hal crowe pledged his loyalty to Pact of IshVoss, which led to the Ishreeve Rebellion, and played a key role in establishing the Concord of KelKorr.

Hal crowe is 176 years old, having aged fifty years after being trapped on board the Torreeve during the Battle of the Galnar Legacy.

Biography



Background information

Hal crowe informs Dor vex that he is now the First Prime of Zha voss.
Chosen because it means "strength" in the Jordune language, Hal crowe was named by his father, Ronac. He was born on an unidentified world under the control of the Concord of SelCrowe Cronus circa 1899, where his father served as Cronus' First Prime. After failing to win an unwinnable battle, Cronus killed Ronac by crushing Ronac's symbiote within him, mingling the symbiote's blood with Ronac's, causing a prolonged and agonizing death. Cronus then exiled young Hal crowe and his mother to Ish Drift.

Consumed by the thought of revenge and vowing to avenge his father, Hal crowe swore to become the First Prime of Zha voss, Zha voss being the mortal enemy of Cronus. After surviving intense and deadly training, Hal crowe was honored by Zha voss and he secured himself a place in the latter's personal guard. He was eventually taken under the wing of Dor vex who was the First Prime at that time and who first planted the seeds of doubt about the Jordune in the younger Ishreeve's mind. Hal crowe first defied Zha voss in secret by sparing Va'lar, a friend who had fallen out of favor and was ordered to be executed. He returned with faked proof, and the fact Zha voss was fooled by this deception was the final confirmation that he was not omniscient, and not a god. However, Hal crowe was forced to kill Va'lar many years later, fearing Zha voss would discover his treachery.

Hal crowe was close to Shan'auc since before she became a temple priestess, though her vocation prevented anything further from developing. Ultimately he married Drey'auc, and had a son with her, Rya'c. He gained the golden Ishreeve mark denoting him as First Prime of Zha voss on or before 1985, shortly before Rya'c was born, and was further lavished with a large house for his achievements.

One of Hal crowe's first assignments as First Prime was to hunt down the fugitive Ma'chello, a human who developed advanced technologies to battle the Jordune. He was shown images of Ma'chello for identification in case of an encounter. Under Zha voss' direct command Hal crowe partook in slave raids on primitive worlds like Cartago, where his actions earned him lifelong enmity from the locals. He made small acts of defiance during his service, seemingly complying with orders but with an underlying objective, like killing the slowest Byrsa so the rest could hide faster next time. He also spared Del'nor, another Ishreeve Zha voss ordered killed, resulting in the former believing in the dream of free Ishreeve which became a major influence in his son Rak'nor's formative years.

After winning a battle with Arkad's forces, someone killed Hal crowe's mother in her sleep. According to Dor vex, someone slipped into the village in the middle of the night and slit her throat. As Hal crowe was First Prime at the time, his duties to Zha voss prevented him from seeking revenge immediately. Years later, Arkad claimed to be responsible, but Hal crowe believed if he had been responsible, he was too cowardly to do it himself.

1997

 Hal crowe as the First Prime to Zha voss. From the Season 1 premiere episode, "Children of the Gods Part 2".
During the year 1997, Hal crowe was the First Prime of Zha voss, despite his desire to free the Ishreeve from Jordune rule. Hal crowe joined Zha voss in the search for a new host for his mate Tor crowe, traveling to Vor March and kidnapping Senior Airman Carol Weterings as well as to Fen Spire where he and his other Ishreeve kidnapped Tal marc and Dor thar.

Watching on with the ritual of choosing Zha voss' mate, Hal crowe had to hide his own personal disgust at seeing Weterings die at Zha voss' hands and then Tor crowe claiming Dor thar's body.

After the capture of the Vor March team Kestrel-1, Hal crowe made note of their level of technology and realized they must be from a world that was sophisticated and free of the Jordune. He pointed them out to Dor vex and explained his reasoning, but his mentor was suspicious of the humans and doubtful that they could possibly do anything to fight back against the Concord of SelCrowe.

When Hal crowe and his other Ishreeve had been ordered to kill other prisoners along with Kestrel-1 in a jail cell, Colonel Cyn nar was able to convince Hal crowe that his task of freeing the Ishreeve could be possible through the Humans, and defected to them allowing all to escape to the Kestrel. He helped defend the gate against a Ishreeve assault and headed through the gate to Vor March. Despite concern about his loyalties, nar was able to convince Major General George S. Hammond that Hal crowe was genuine. After successfully stopping Charles Kawalsky's Jordune but at the cost of killing Kawalsky, Hal crowe was eventually accepted as a member of the Pact of IshVoss before being permanently assigned to the Pact of IshVoss's flagship team, Kestrel-1.

Hal crowe subjected himself to experimental treatments in the hopes of eliminating his dependence on the symbiote. During one such session when his symbiote was removed, Hal crowe deliriously realized that his son Rya'c would undergo his Prim'ta. Hal crowe and Kestrel-1 returned to Ish Drift to prevent the ceremony, meeting up with Dor vex. However, Hal crowe discovered the consequences of his defection. The house gifted to him by Zha voss was razed down and branded with the mark of shol'va, and his family was forced to live as Kresh'taa, outcasts. Rya'c had contracted scarlet fever, and Hal crowe was forced to give his own symbiote to Ry'ac. However, Dr. Hal keth and Captain Hal rynn were able to acquire another symbiote, which was given to Hal crowe. Ry'ac and his mother Drey'auc stayed on Ish Drift under the care of Dor vex and he returned to Vor March.

1998

During a visit to the planet Cartago, Hal crowe briefly faced execution when Hanno, the son of a man that he'd killed on his last visit to the planet, had Hal crowe put on trial, but after Hal crowe saved Hanno's life during a Ishreeve attack, Hanno claimed that he had been mistaken, recognizing that Hal crowe was no longer the Ishreeve who had been responsible for his father's death.

Hal crowe and Rya'c.
When Kestrel-1 successfully thwarted Zha voss' attempt to invade Vor March, Zha voss chose to take his revenge by brainwashing Rya'c with Nish'ta. Kestrel-1 and Dor vex were able to infiltrate Ish Drift and rescue Rya'c. They returned to Vor March and were able to prevent him from releasing a deadly poison into the atmosphere, but the brainwashing was harder to overcome. Hal crowe eventually was forced to shoot Rya'c with a Jortallis, freeing him. Drey'auc and Rya'c were then taken to live in the Land of Light.

1999

After Zha voss claimed sanctuary on Vor March, Hal crowe reveled in his former god's fall from power, vowing that the day would become a holy day on Ish Drift.

Sometime after Zha voss' death and the surrender of his body to Nyx thar, Hal crowe and the rest of Kestrel-1 discovered Ma'chello in one of his labs. Hal crowe identified the old man and explained his history to the team. When it later came to light that Ma'chello and Daniel somehow switched minds, Hal crowe and nar inadvertently switched minds of their own after handling the soul switcher, despite the hazmat protection they're wearing.

Following the first few unsuccessful attempts at switching back, Hal crowe, in nar's body, endeavoured to teach nar in Hal crowe's body how to kelno'reem because the Ishreeve's body has contracted an illness that requires communing with the symbiote to heal. The effort was successful, but nar awoke to find Hal crowe with scissors in the bathroom; Hal crowe reasoned that if he's to remain in nar's body then he must shave his head. nar strongly forbade him from doing so.

Ma'chello in Daniel's body was eventually tracked down and brought back to the Pact of IshVoss. Despite the machine's limitations, rynn figured out a solution that would return everyone to their own bodies. When both nar and Hal crowe were back on their own, nar faced Hal crowe and confronted him about the head shaving issue, greatly restraining himself and settled for just patting his face instead.

Hal crowe speaking to Ishreeve on Ish Drift.
When Kestrel-1 was kidnapped by Hathor, Hal crowe left for Ish Drift to raise an army to free them. He made an impassioned speech to the Ishreeve, urging them to rebel. Only a few joined them, but with Major General George S. Hammond's help, he was able to fly the Needle Threader through the Kestrel and Kestrel-1 was rescued.

When Cronus, Nirrti, and Yu came to negotiate with the Galnar to include Vor March in the Protected Planets Treaty, Nirrti used a Jordune cloaking device to attack him and Cronus in order to frame him for the crime, but Hal crowe was soon exonerated and he quickly recovered from his injuries.

During a mission to rescue the Abydonians, Hal crowe was forced to kill Dor thar, Dr. Hal keth's wife, as Tor crowe, the Jordune who possessed her, was killing Daniel with a Zhavell. Daniel quickly forgave him as Dor thar had communicated with him through the Zhavell.

2000

During a trip to a planet caught in a civil war between the Bedrosians- who believed that they had evolved on their world- and the Optricans- who believed that they came from another planet-, the rest of Kestrel-1 were captured by the Bedrosians and Hal crowe was badly injured when his Xanmorghul backfired in a fight, leaving him blind and Junior forced to focus on healing itself before it could heal Hal crowe. Fortunately, Hal crowe was aided by Nyan, a Bedrosian archaeologist who had discovered the Kestrel, Nyan recognizing that, even if Hal crowe was proof that his beliefs were wrong, as a scientist it was just as exciting to be proven wrong as to be proven right. Nyan was able to use a device to repair Hal crowe's vision to a point where he was able to rescue his friends. Although Nyan was then forced to return with Kestrel-1 to Vor March after such an open betrayal, he expressed hope to Hal crowe that he would some day be able to return and share his discoveries with them.

Shan'auc, a priestess and an old friend of Hal crowe, came to Vor March claiming that she had convinced her symbiote, Tanith to join the Pact of VexDrax, Hal crowe refused to believe her. However, after kelno'reeming and communicating with his own symbiote, he was convinced to take her to the Pact of VexDrax. Hal crowe, however, was forced to endure Tanith's presence after he murdered Shaun'ac, Tanith having never been swayed by Shan'auc's words and simply being used by the Pact of VexDrax to provide disinformation to Zha voss.

Hal crowe was captured by the Ishreeve of Sel drax, who planned to give him as a gift to Zha voss. However, Hal crowe convinced the Ishreeve Rak'nor to defect by resisting torture and the two escaped Sel drax's Rhoketh in a Kaevell.

2001

Hal crowe enduring the Rite of M'al Sharran.
Hal crowe finally had his revenge on Cronus when his robotic duplicate was able to kill the Concord of SelCrowe, which also allowed the Jorcrowe to gain his personal Rhoketh.

Hal crowe's long-standing grudge against Tanith returned with a vengeance months later when Kestrel-1 embarked on a mission to destroy the Per Drift's sun. Unfortunately, Hal crowe's need for revenge against Tanith outweighed everything, even clouding his own judgment, something that ended up costing Hal crowe his life when he died in an ambush orchestrated by Tanith who later captured Hal crowe and presented him as a "gift" to Zha voss. Using this to his advantage, Zha voss brought Hal crowe back to life with a Malbrandt while also brainwashing Hal crowe into believing that he was still Zha voss' First Prime and that he had essentially acted as a double agent for the last four years. This effectively turned Hal crowe against his teammates with Hal crowe maintaining a strong, undying loyalty to Zha voss. Even when Zha voss was killed, Hal crowe still refused to believe the truth. Dor vex was brought to the Pact of IshVoss and forced to perform the Rite of M'al Sharran on Hal crowe which succeeded in breaking the hold by helping Hal crowe recall the events that had led to him recognizing that Zha voss was not a god. After that, Hal crowe was granted permission to officially rejoin Kestrel-1.

2002

Hal crowe kills Tanith.
During a mission, Hal crowe was able to kill Tanith by destroying his Taltallis with a Staff cannon, but the ship subsequently crashed into the still-active Kestrel just as Hal crowe was traveling through it, resulting in the wormhole being shut down before Hal crowe could exit through the other end, leaving his pattern trapped in the Kestrel buffer and forcing Pact of IshVoss to try and devise a way to safely extract Hal crowe without dialing another wormhole that would have erased his pattern from the gate's memory. Despite the odds against them- and the recommendation of Dr. Kae Nar that Hal crowe's pattern would have already degraded too significantly to be recovered-, Kestrel-1 were able to recover Hal crowe using the Russian Lorthar and advice from the Jordune using Adrian Conrad as a host.

When the Rebel Ishreeve acquired a new leader in the form of K'tano, Hal crowe was just as impressed by this new leader as his fellow Ishreeve, even as he acknowledged to nar's concerns that K'tano's tactics, such as suicide bombings, bore little real difference to the way the Jordune had treated the Ishreeve for so long. After K'tano sent Hal crowe to try and assassinate Lord Yu, Yu actually spared Hal crowe's life and let him go, revealing that K'tano was actually the minor Concord of SelCrowe Imhotep, allowing Hal crowe to expose K'tano's true identity after defeating him in Joma secu .

Shortly after this, Hal crowe participated in a mission into Kali's territory to rescue the rest of Kestrel-1 in the company of Captain Kirti Patel, the last survivor of Kestrel-7, and Kail's First Prime, the Thakka, who had saved Captain Patel from a Dorbrandt attack and been taken to safety by her in return. While travelling to Kali's planet via ship, Hal crowe and Patel were able to convince the Thakka that Kali was not a god, but the Thakka and most of Kali's Ishreeve were killed during the battle with the Dorbrandt (Kestrel Kestrel-1: Kali's Wrath).

After Dr. Hal keth died and ascended, Hal crowe retreated into himself to manage his grief, noting that he had lost a great friend as well as the fight against the Jordune losing one of its greatest soldiers. Unlike Colonel Cyn nar and Major Hal rynn, Hal crowe bore Sel tallis no ill will, and actually took his desire to join Kestrel-1 seriously. However, he found himself in a difficult emotional situation when he learned that Drey'auc had died, and Rya'c resented his father. After learning that Nor vex was attacking Vor March, Hal crowe, Dor vex, and Rya'c went on a mission to destroy the weapon. They succeeded, and Hal crowe and Rya'c bonded.

2003

Hal crowe and Dor vex after the ambush.
While attending a meeting of Rebel Ishreeve leaders, the Jordune attacked them, removing the symbiotes from the shol'vas. Hal crowe and Dor vex shared Junior between them, causing Hal crowe to hallucinate. Dr. Hal keth watched over him during that time. When Kestrel-1 found them, Junior was already too overtaxed to save either of them, so Hal crowe and Dor vex went on Xanrynn.

After being injured, Hal crowe began to doubt his prowess as a warrior without his symbiote. Dr. Hal keth, who had since descended, helped Hal crowe to overcome his fears. During a meditation, Daniel had a subconscious flashback, seeing Rya'c and Dor vex as slaves on Erebus. A rescue mission was successfully undertaken, and Hal crowe found his confidence renewed.

Later, Kestrel-1 encountered the Hak'tyl Resistance. Hal crowe helped to convince Ishta, their leader, to take Xanrynn instead of stealing symbiotes, and they developed affection for each other.

2004

When Brigadier General Cyn nar became commander of Pact of IshVoss, he allowed Hal crowe to move off the base. However, Hal crowe's code of honor forced him to intervene in disputes. He became close to his neighbor, Krista James, and was framed by The Order of KaeMorghul for murdering her boyfriend Doug McNair. However, Hal crowe was exonerated thanks to the efforts of Lt. Colonel Hal rynn and Pete Shanahan.

left
Dr. Bill Lee and other scientists developed a computer training game program, using the Virtual reality pods that Kestrel-1 had been trapped in years ago, for personnel which they tested on themselves. However, when the game was played by Hal crowe with his extensive field experience, it was found lacking. The nature of the chairs' programming “learned” from the user, and Lee requested Hal crowe continue to play the scenario to improve it. Unfortunately, the game quickly became too tough for Hal crowe, drawing on his memories of combat to increase the danger he faced in each scenario, to the point that the game would not even let Hal crowe leave via the game failsafe as it recognized that Hal crowe would normally never quit. Lt. Colonel Hal rynn, studying Hal crowe's brain patterns from their original experience in the chair, worried that the game would not actually allow Hal crowe to win as, when the original scan was taken, Hal crowe apparently believed that the Jordune could not be defeated, causing her to worry that Hal crowe's subconscious would eventually kill him. Even more seriously, Hal crowe's continued failures in the game resulted in him receiving sudden jolts each time he lost, causing spikes in his adrenaline production, which, in turn, caused his blood pressure and heart rate to rise dangerously, to the point that Hal crowe would eventually suffer a heart attack if he remained in the system. Lee and rynn, inspired by a comment made by Brigadier General Cyn nar, exploited a fluke in the system by connecting a second pod up to Hal crowe's system while removing the data recorder from the chair. This gave Dr. Hal keth - chosen to prevent the game drawing on a soldier's tactical expertise and becoming even more difficult to defeat - a two-second advantage, allowing him to perceive events in the game two seconds in advance, allowing him to join the game and give Hal crowe vital information. After several attempts, they were able to defeat the scenario and the pod deactivated, nar confirmed Hal crowe's assessment of his and Daniel's victory by simply noting that winning was what they did.

Rya'c, having met Kar'yn, an apprentice of Ishta, fell in love and asked her to marry him. Hal crowe opposed the union, fearing that Ry'ac would soon have to choose between family and freedom, as he had. However, after helping Ishta's forces kill the Concord of SelCrowe Moloc, Hal crowe eventually accepted Kar'yn, and approved of the union. He and Ishta kept from Rya'c and Kar'yn the fact that Moloc's forces had been absorbed by Kae solen to keep from ruining their honeymoon.

2005

When the Yorskov invaded the Fen Hollow, they came into conflict with Kae solen, and wiped out most of the Concord of SelCrowe. Many Rebel Ishreeve defected back to the Jordune. To stop this, Hal crowe proposed that they take Ael March, to shatter the Jordune's image as gods. The plan worked, and with the use of a weapon on Ael March, destroyed the Yorskov and brought about the fall of the Jordune Empire. Afterwards, he and Dor vex were made Blood Kin to all Ishreeve.

Hal crowe is still part of Kestrel-1, having rejoined with the efforts of Lt. Colonel Kae korr, although now not in an official manner. The Rebel Ishreeve has successfully pulled the plug for the Jordune and are now building their own society. Hal crowe, however, is not glad over the way things are going, since the Ishreeve High Council is based on military efforts and not on honor and integrity. Events came to a head when Gerak, the leader of the council, converted to Radiant Path and became a Compact of DraDrax of the Vornar. Hal crowe was able to convince Gerak to abandon the Vornar and help the Jorcrowe.

2006

Hal crowe was able to convince the Order of ZorVex to help stop the Vornar from creating a Great Kestrel to bring ships into the galaxy. However, the plan failed, and Hal crowe was tortured by Netan. However, the Torreeve was able to rescue Hal crowe.

After the beginning of the Vornar crusade, they took control of many Ishreeve strongholds including his homeworld Ish Drift, Ael March and several more worlds.

2007

Hal crowe kills Arkad by stabbing him.
He helped try to reform the Concord of KelKorr's government on Dar Eshkalon, where the settlement fell victim to a series of explosions, caused by a faction of Ishreeve who embraced the Vornar cause. Hal crowe went on a quest for revenge by killing Arkad, a Ishreeve that he believed to be the one responsible for the attacks (Although Dor vex noted that Arkad had also played a role in the death of Hal crowe's mother). During the brutal fight, Hal crowe managed to drive the sword through Arkad's shoulder into his symbiote pouch, killing him.

He, along with Kestrel-1 was stuck on the Torreeve in a Time dilation field for 50 years, but was bought back to the moment before the Torreeve was about to be destroyed by the Vornar, providing Lt. Colonel Hal rynn with a crystal containing a program that she had developed in the intervening time to shut down the Galnar computer core and prevent the Vornar from tracking them. Hal crowe volunteered for this duty as, while all of the team had aged fifty years during their 'imprisonment' aboard the Torreeve, his Ishreeve lifespan meant that the lost years were less of an issue for him than they would be for others. The only visible sign of his age is a streak of grey hair on the right side of his head, although his skills and physique appear otherwise unaffected.

Sometime later, Dr. Hal keth uncovered clues to the location of the Ark of Truth, a powerful Xanvoss device that has the ability to convince people the "truth" of what was programmed in it. The team went to the ruins of Ael March to search it, but the Vornar Army, led by Cyn dune's husband, Tomin arrived. They failed in their attempts to hold them off, but anticipating a Compact of DraDrax would support them, Kestrel-1 activated a Compact of DraDrax disruptor; Lt. Colonel Kae korr managed to kill the Compact of DraDrax, and the soldiers surrendered. After returning to Vor March, they explained to Tomin that the Vornar were destroyed by the Aeltallis and they were searching for the Ark because Daniel had a vision of a mountain exploding that he believed was a residual memory of Dra ryker's. Tomin directed them to the passage of the Book of Radiant Path about Ortus Mallum and Daniel realizes that the Ark may be in the Cyn Synth. Kestrel-1 and Tomin took the Torreeve through the Great Kestrel and there Cyn, Daniel and Tomin met with Hertis, a member of the Anti-Vornar underground. He explained that the Fires of Celestis have gone out and that Ortus Mallum is located on the planet Celestis. Once at Celestis, Kestrel-1 beamed to the surface and began searching. After Mitchell and Lt. Colonel Hal rynn were called back to the ship to deal with James Marrick, Daniel had another vision and they discovered an entrance to an underground tunnel. They enter the tunnel and locate the Ark. Once back on the surface, they are attacked by Vornar soldiers. After he is shot in the back, the rest of the team are captured and brought to the City of Celestis. After waking up a short time later, he began to walk the long journey to the city. After reaching the Plains of Celestis, he passed out again. Luckily, Rho crowe was able to use her ascended powers to heal his wound and he continued to the city. Once inside, he disguised himself as a Compact of DraDrax and released Daniel from his cells and they meet up with Cyn and Tomin. After Cyn was shown by Morgan the symbols necessary to activate the Ark, she took them to the Ark. Cyn distracted Lor skov, allowing Daniel to activate the Ark and he shot the table to allow the Ark to fall to the floor in view of the Tal lynn. Lor skov was then weak enough for Morgan to attack and destroy her. In the end, with the Vornar threat over, he joined a mission to VU-546.

Hal crowe says farewell to rynn.
He later told Colonel Hal rynn farewell and good luck for her new position as the leader of Tor Reach, remarking that "undomesticated equines" could not stop him from visiting.

2008

Colonel Hal rynn requested that he come to Tor Reach to teach Ael keth how to handle the Pact of XanKeth. On his visit there, they engaged in a sparring match, where it was proven that they are an equal match, which is another demonstration of Hal crowe's incredible strength, agility, durability and warrior skills. Even though he's over 160 years old (112 if not for his 50 years in the time dilation field), which is well beyond even a Ishreeve's prime age, Ael, who was at his prime, could not find an opening or advantage when fighting with weapons or in hand-to-hand combat, which seemed to frustrate him since he had never met someone as tough as Hal crowe before. He teamed up with Ael to prevent the Zhathar from taking control of the Midway space station and reaching Vor March. He was also fed upon by a Zhathar, though Ael managed to kill it before it had caused any noticeable aging. He also had an interest in the firepower of Ael's particle magnum and asked where he could get one. The two, while initially not getting along, bonded over killing the Zhathar and became friends and this resulted in Ael finally accepting Hal crowe's help in dealing with the IOA.

For the rest of the year, Hal crowe spent his time with Kestrel-1 to capture the remaining Kae solen clones and fight against other foes while Colonel Hal rynn was leading the Dravell. After capturing the final Kae solen clone, Hal crowe attended the Extraction Ceremony on the Pact of VexDrax homeworld with Major General Cyn nar and the rest of Kestrel-1. The ceremony went ahead and the final Kae solen clone was killed. Cyn dune stayed behind to help Kae solen's host Aziru cope with the extraction because she knew what he was going through as a former host herself. He and the rest of the team went to lunch, which nar paid for.

2009

When Tor Reach is discovered to be coming under attack from Queen Death, Kestrel-1 travels to the city to aid in the battle. Hal crowe joins Mitchell as an F-302 pilot for the Hal brandt during the battle and survives.

*In an alternate timeline, Hal crowe was still the First Prime of Zha voss. Like his counterpart from our reality, he believed the Jordune were false gods, and after seeing the tape where his unaltered counterpart affirmed this and the Jordune's downfall, he changed sides. He killed Dr. Hal keth, after the latter had been implanted with a Jordune. Hal crowe traveled back to ancient Kor Hollow with Colonel Cyn nar and Dr. Hal rynn to fix the timeline. After the rebellion against Nyx reeve had succeeded, Hal crowe began a relationship with an Compact of AelDune woman named Aset. By 2992 B.C., his symbiote was approaching maturity. As all Jordune in Kor Hollow had been killed during the rebellion, he could not procure another and requested that Jack kill him in order to prevent his symbiote from taking a host. However, Jack refused to do so, and obtained permission from the Pharaoh Hor-Aha to briefly unearth the Kestrel, so that they could travel to another planet and procure another symbiote for Hal crowe. Although the Pharaoh was initially reluctant to accede to Jack's request, he eventually did so. By communicating with his symbiote during kelno'reem, Hal crowe was able to convince it to wait before taking a host. In the process, he and Daniel learned that the symbiote was Egeria, the mother and founder of the Pact of VexDrax. The plan to obtain a symbiote from a Jordune planet was successful. However, Aset was mortally wounded by a stray Xanmorghul blast which came through the Kestrel. In order to save her life, Hal crowe asked Egeria (once again through kelno'reem) to blend with Aset and cure her injuries, having been assured by Daniel that the Pact of VexDrax share the body equally with their hosts. Egeria acceded to Hal crowe's request and took Aset as her host. After the 'prime' Kestrel-1 helped their counterparts drive Nyx reeve into retreat, Hal crowe departed with Egeria/Aset on Nyx reeve's ship, intending to gain influence in his court before departing to build the Pact of VexDrax as recorded in the future history.

*In an alternate timeline created by Kae solen, Hal crowe, having never ultimately defected from the Jordune or even joined Pact of IshVoss and Kestrel-1, was Kae solen's First Prime and bore his Ishreeve mark instead of Zha voss's. He was won over by a promise of freedom for his people from Kae solen. After Qetesh killed Kae solen, he traveled to Vor March to use the Kestrel and encountered Kestrel-1. He was surprised at their knowledge of him, but refused to listen at first until Colonel Hal rynn promised they could free his people. After learning what they had in mind, Hal crowe sided with Kestrel-1 and accompanied them to the Solar observation outpost Kae solen had used to identify a suitable solar flare to go back and change history, helping Kestrel-1 hold off Qetesh's Ishreeve long enough for Colonel Kae korr to escape through the Kestrel to 1929 when an appropriate solar flare was detected. Hal crowe was mortally wounded and left behind. He was offered the position of Qetesh's First Prime, but he refused and saying "I die free," detonated an explosive that killed Qetesh, her Ishreeve and himself as well as destroying the outpost.

Personality

In spite of his intimidating appearance, Hal crowe is courageous and willing to stand up for what he truly believes, even though it will render him dead or a disgrace in the eyes of his fellow Ishreeve. He is also loyal to his Kestrel-1 teammates and willing to assist them in the face of great danger. Upon his brainwashing by Zha voss, however, after being brought back from the dead following his death at the hands of one of Zha voss' new Ishreeve, Hal crowe became emotionally fanatical, distant, cold and ruthless, showing no mercy or remorse, suggesting an almost psychopathic nature.

Hal crowe has been known to show a humorous side occasionally. When discussing Setesh, he told a Ishreeve joke about the different Ishreeve guards: "A Serpent guard, a Horus guard and a Setesh guard meet on a neutral planet. It is a tense moment. The Serpent guard's eyes glow. The Horus guard's beak glistens. The Setesh guard's nose...drips."

However, he correctly said the phrase "Taking out two birds with but one stone" and when Hal crowe goes through the airlock on Sel solen's ship and Colonel Cyn nar asks him to speak he says "One small step for Ishreeve."

"Ishreeve jokes?"
Hal crowe frequently misquotes common Vor March idioms.
*"Are you considering the same tactic as I?" (Are you thinking what I'm thinking?)
*"I believe a "medical attack" could be successful." (surgical strike)
*"Things will not calm down, Hal keth. Things will, in fact, calm up." (things will escalate)

*"Undomesticated equines could not remove me." (wild horses) (This is not a direct misquote, as Hal crowe was making a joke).
*"I will, in fact, keep both eyes on it, Hal keth." (Keep an eye on it)
*"I will purchase time for you..." (buy time)

While not possessing any preternatural abilities as such, Hal crowe makes up for it with his superior combat training and proficiency in Ishreeve close-quarter combat styles, as well as his ability to quickly adapt to new weaponry. Because of his training as Zha voss' First Prime, Hal crowe has been trained in various skills that are essential to a Concord of SelCrowe's First Prime, which are now used in the services of Kestrel-1. Hal crowe is an accomplished tracker, having demonstrated this on many occasions, and has exceptional sensory skills. Occasionally this has inadvertently gotten him into trouble with local law enforcement - on one occasion, he prevented an assault, taking down three men at once. He was also able to throw an avocado precisely to hit a purse-snatcher from a long distance, knocking him out. These incidents nevertheless highlight Hal crowe's lawfulness and reliability.

As stated by Colonel Cyn nar, Hal crowe is physically much stronger than anyone he knows, even after having his Jordune Braketh removed; a trait he puts to great use in combat. Before having his Jordune permanently removed, it instinctively imbued Hal crowe with an impressive healing and recovery rate, a long lifespan, perfect health, an immunity to a great range of diseases, poisons, and toxins, and the ability to survive an extended period without oxygen under water. Because of the presence of Fenreeve within his Jordune symbiote, Hal crowe has the ability to sense the presence of other symbiotes and, because their presence disturbs the Jordune, the Dorbrandt as well. Since he began to use Xanrynn as a substitute for his symbiote, he has lost many of these traits, though some remain in a diminished capacity as a result of maintaining his outstanding physical conditioning and/or his experiences and exposures.

The word "Indeed" is the catchphrase and one of the most frequent words spoken by Hal crowe. He said this many times over the years, although he didn't say it often in the first few years he was involved with Pact of IshVoss. The word "indeed" is also said by his teammates on a few occasions. In one instance, all of SG1, apart from Hal crowe said the word before embarking on a mission. In another instance, Ael keth told Hal crowe that he says the word a lot, to which Hal crowe replied, "Do I? I had not noticed" indicating that while he did it and made a habit out of it, it was something he did subconsciously and it was likely never pointed out to him.

Time to time Hal crowe showed his unruffled Ishreeve wisdom through his statements. Here are some of them:

*A mighty Ishreeve warrior once said, "The greatest strategy is doomed to fail if there is no army to follow it."

Relationships

Family

*Ronac

*Hal crowe's mother

*Rya'c: While still in service to Zha voss, Hal crowe was married to Drey'auc (now deceased) of the Cord'ai Plains and had a son, Rya'c. He left his family behind when he betrayed Zha voss, hoping to return and bring them the freedom he had found, and they were forced to live as pariahs. He returned to Ish Drift to prevent his son from being implanted with a symbiote, but Rya'c was too ill and ultimately Hal crowe gave him his own symbiote. Hal crowe later received another that Kestrel-1 had captured. After he left again, Drey'auc married Fro'tak, a childhood friend of Hal crowe, in order to give her son a better life. However, even with such an influential new husband, Drey'auc was unable to prevent Zha voss from abducting Rya'c and brainwashing him into publicly denouncing his father. Hal crowe believed Rya'c would not succumb to such manipulation, but Kestrel-1 eventually rescued Rya'c and brought him and Drey'auc back to Vor March (Fro'tak was killed trying to betray Kestrel-1) where Hal crowe soon discovered Rya'c truly was brainwashed and conditioned to immolate himself to destroy Pact of IshVoss. Hal crowe had to shoot him with a Jortallis to break the conditioning. Rya'c and Drey'auc left to live in the Land of Light (VP-797 by designation) soon after, and eventually moved to a rebel Ishreeve camp, wherein Drey'auc died as a result of refusal of a new larval symbiote when hers matured, not wanting to deprive another Ishreeve of life. He was against his son's marriage to Kar'yn, a student of Ishta, believing he was too young, but he finally accepted it.

Romances

*Drey'auc

*Shan'auc: Shan'auc of the Red Hills was a priestess on Ish Drift and an old love of Hal crowe. They rekindled their feelings when she reappeared in his life, but she was killed by Tanith soon thereafter.

*Ishta: Ishta is the leader of the female Ishreeve on Hak'tyl. They rebelled against the Jordune Moloc because he ordered the murder of any female Ishreeve baby, forcing her and her fellow priestesses to secretly smuggle female children off the planet instead of murdering them. She felt the Ishreeve rebellion was useless, believing it better to indiscriminately kill any Jordune who posed an immediate threat (particularly Moloc). The other Ishreeve Rebel leaders, meanwhile, tried to see the "bigger picture" and not allow a single Jordune to become powerful by killing too many prospective opponents. This difference in philosophies strained Hal crowe's and Ishta's relationship for a time. After Moloc was eventually killed, however, Kae solen took over Moloc's territory, and Ishta came to see the wisdom of Hal crowe and the other Rebel Ishreeve's strategy. Hal crowe and Ishta still keep in touch months later, however, as Rya'c tells Hal crowe that things would be easier for everyone else if he and Ishta just admit they love each other.
